## Michaelmas 2014

### Week 1

**Reading material**

- *Le Cid*, Corneille
- Speech extract by Henri IV
- *Saül,* André Gide

**Grammar and structures**

- Revision : the Present tense, the Future tense, the Imperative, aller + inf., venir de + inf.
- possessives, emphatic pronouns, gerund

### Week 2

**Reading material**

- *L'expiation, Châtiments*, Victor Hugo
- Les Cathares
- Entretien avec F. Mitterrand

**Grammar and structures**

- Negations, demonstratives, imperfect, pluperfect, lequel

### Week 3

**Reading material**

- *La Nausée,* J.P. Sartre

**Grammar and structures**

- Perfect tense, allait/venait de + inf.

### Week 4

**Reading material**

- *Histoire d'Espagne*
- *Mélanges, La Pléiade,* Voltaire

**Grammar and structures**

- Present Conditional

### Week 5

**Reading material**

- *Le grand Meaulnes*, A. Fournier
- Histoire de Roussillon

**Grammar and structures**

- Past historic

### Week 6

**Reading material**

- La création de l'école publique

**Grammar and structures**

- Past conditional

### Week 7

**Reading material**

- *Verdi, Star du public et du politique*

**Grammar and structures**

- Relative pronouns
