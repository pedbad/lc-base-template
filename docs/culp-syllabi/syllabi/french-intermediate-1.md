## Lesson 1: TALKING ABOUT YOURSELF

**Functions**

- Introductions
- Stating your name, nationality, age, place and date of birth, marital status, occupation etc
- Saying which languages you speak

**Grammar**

- present tense verbs
- adjectives
- interrogatives
- indefinite and definite articles
- prepositions (in/ to countries and towns)
- prepositional phrases
- negation
- cardinals 1 - 100
- registers: tu/vous

## Lesson 2: TALKING ABOUT FRIENDS and FAMILY

**Functions**

- Describing your family
- Spelling in French

**Grammar**

- Alphabet
- possessive adjectives
- adjectives: agreements, irregular forms, position
- comparatives: plus jeune, plus grand, etc

## Lesson 3: DAILY ROUTINE + FREE TIME

**Functions**

- Talking about hobbies, interests and weekend activities
- Saying what you like doing
- Saying how long you've been working/studying at Cambridge
- Saying when your day starts and ends
- Describing what you do during a typical week day

**Grammar**

- present tense cont'd: regular, irregular and reflexive verbs
- to play: (jouer à, jouer de)
- partitives
- depuis and ça fait

## Lesson 4: TALKING ABOUT HOW LIFE USED TO BE

**Functions**

- Saying what you did yesterday
- Describing your daily routine when you were at school
- Describing home life in the past

**Grammar**

- perfect tense:
  - regular, irregular and reflexive verbs
- imperfect:
  - regular, irregular and reflexive verbs

## Lesson 5: GETTING ABOUT: TOWN v COUNTRY

**Functions**

- Saying where you used to live as a child
- Describing your home/present accommodation
- Saying what you think of your home town and where you live at the moment and why
- Asking for directions in town
- Getting about on foot
- Travelling by car

**Grammar**

- Adjectives including irregular forms
- Prepositions of place: derrière, devant, à, à côté de, en face de, près de, (pas) loin de
- There is/are/isn't/aren't (Il y a / Il n'y a pas de / d')
- Imperative

## Lesson 6: TRAVELLING + MAKING BOOKINGS

**Functions**

- Planning a journey
- Booking flights online
- Saying where you will stay: hotel, gîte, campsite
- Saying what you will do during the trip

**Grammar**

- to have to (devoir/ il faut)
- to be able to (pouvoir)
- to want (vouloir)
- partitives: negation
- future

## Lesson 8: HEALTH + FITNESS

**Functions**

- Describing ailments
- Talking about healthy eating
- Saying what you do, or ought to do, to maintain a good level of fitness
- Talking about what you do to relax

**Grammar**

- Avoir mal à
- Se sentir
- conditional
- adverbs of time
- gerunds
- infinitives followed by de: éviter de, essayer de etc

## Lesson 9: WORLD OF WORK

**Functions**

- Talking about your own and others' studies and occupations
- Saying how long you've been studying/had this occupation
- Talking about past work or work experience

**Grammar**

- to be (être) + profession
- use of tenses with pendant, depuis and il y a
