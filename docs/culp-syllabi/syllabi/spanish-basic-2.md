# CULP Programa de Estudios Básico 2

*Last reviewed Monday 04 August 2025*

**Prerequisites:** Level A1 of the CEFR or equivalent. This is a continuation of the Spanish Basic course, suitable for students who have picked up the basics of the language when travelling or at school.

**Target level:** Level A2 of the CEFR

## Approach

The classes are **communicative** and consist of tasks oriented at helping students understand Spanish in use and to acquire it by use. All four communicative skills will be developed and/or strengthened – listening, speaking, reading and writing. The students will engage in interactive language activities, participating in group and pair work according to a syllabus based on systematic grammatical progression. Each class focuses on a topic that is related to the previous and following one; activities build on the students' abilities and on their previous knowledge. Grammar and vocabulary are practiced and perfected in context. Students will also be offered guidance in self-study and be set optional homework tasks.

## Course main objectives

- A communicative approach is used. All four skills (speaking, reading, listening and writing) are practised, with the main emphasis on speaking.
- To revise the basic grammatical structures and lexis, and build on them.
- To strengthen a positive and confident attitude towards language learning.
- To encourage speaking and ease the transition to the next level (intermediate 1).
- To promote cultural understanding.
- To be able to communicate in everyday situations.
- To be able to understand general and detailed information and to communicate in standard speech on a range of everyday issues.
- To be able to read printed and handwritten scripts on a limited number of everyday topics.
- To be able to write simple texts using descriptive language accurately.

## CULP Spanish Basic 2: Programa de estudios

### Lesson 1: Somos amigos

**Language functions**

- A bit of vocabulary: we all know *español*!
- Greetings and introductions
- Are you *tú* or *usted*?
- *Me llamo*… saying who we are and introducing friends
- saying our nationality and language
- stating our job/profession
- talking about languages

**Grammar and usage**

- Basic vocabulary: greetings and introductions
- Personal pronouns
- Informal (tú) and formal (usted) registers
- Demonstrative adjectives and pronouns: *Este/Esta es… Estos/Estas son…*
- Verbs: *Ser, Estar y Tener*
- Numbers 1 to 30
- Basic vocabulary: family, jobs
- Adjectives of origin (gentilicios)

**Work after class**

- Activities on Moodle
- Homework set by teacher

### Lesson 2: Hablemos de ¿Cuándo? ¿Dónde? ¿Cómo? ¿Quién? ¿Por qué?

**Language functions**

- Vocabulary related: Family members, professions
- Talking in detail about profession and studies
- Daily routines
- Role-play – interviewing – asking personal questions, making friends.

**Grammar and usage**

- The Spanish alphabet and pronunciation
- Verbs: SER/ESTAR/TENER Part II.
- Expressions with TENER
- Structure of the sentence.
- Informative questions
- General and specific answers.
- Some adjectives.
- Interrogative pronouns: *¿qué? ¿cómo? ¿quién?*
- Reflexive verbs: *llamarse*
- Adjectives of comparison: *mayor/menor*
- The present of *–ar, -er* and *–ir* verbs
- Cardinal numbers 11 to 100

**Work after class**

- Activities on Moodle
- Homework set by teacher

### Lesson 3: ¿De dónde eres?

**Language functions**

- Saying where you live and where you are from
- Describing places: describing your home
- Renting a house/apartment
- Parts of the house
- Furniture
- Facilities
- Expressing likes and dislikes

**Grammar and usage**

- Basic vocabulary: places and buildings
- Verbs: *vivir, ser, estar*
- Ordinal numbers: 1st to 10th
- Adverbs and adverbial phrases: *aquí/acá… a la derecha/izquierda…al lado de, debajo de…*
- Verbo *gustar* and similar
- Stem-changing verbs: *empiezo, vuelvo, me visto*
- Expressions with HAY

**Work after class**

- Activities on Moodle
- Homework set by teacher

### Lesson 4: Los fines de semana yo…

**Language functions**

- Describing our routine
- Telling the time and when we do things
- What we like to do in our free time
- Sports and hobbies

**Grammar and usage**

- Basic vocabulary: the days of the week, sports and hobbies, food and drink
- Verbs: gustar-type verbs, *odiar,* stem changing verbs: *pedir, querer*
- *Me gusta… me encanta… odio…* expressing our likes (verbs)
- Stem-changing verbs (*empiezo, me acuesto*)
- Reflexive verbs (*me levanto, me baño*).

**Work after class**

- Activities on Moodle
- Homework set by teacher

### Lesson 5: La diversión. Un vistazo gastronómico

**Language functions**

- Talking about food and timetables
- Asking for the bill
- Booking a table in a restaurant
- Choosing food items from a menu
- Learning about traditional food
- Asking for the bill: numbers from 100 to 1 million

**Grammar and usage**

- Vocabulary: food and drinks (generic and country-specific)
- Numbers and numerals: 0 to 1 million
- Adjectives and agreement
- Impersonal HABER (hay…)
- Verb QUERER
- Indirect object pronouns: *me, nos* (e.g. *¿Nos trae la cuenta por favor?*)

**Work after class**

- Activities on Moodle
- Homework set by teacher

### Lesson 10: La salud y el bienestar

> [!important] Assessment
> **Moodle quiz 3: listening comprehension**: after Lesson 10, closed before Lesson 11, 15% of the final mark

**Language functions**

- Saying how you are
- Parts of the body
- Aches and pains
- Pharmaceutical remedies

**Grammar and usage**

- Vocabulary: physical description, parts of the body
- Irregular verb: *doler* – to hurt
- Irregular verbs: *estar, sentirse, encontrarse* – to be feeling
- Modal verbs: *deber, tener que* – to have to
- The present perfect (*pretérito perfecto*): *me he quemado la mano*
- Revision: *pretérito indefinido*
- The *pretérito imperfecto* in context

**Work after class**

- Activities on Moodle and Moodle quiz 3: listening comprehension
