## Week 1: INTRODUCTION

**Language functions**

- Course introduction: aims, resources, assessment
- Introduction to Russian alphabet, pronunciation and phonetics

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Alphabet, phonetics, reading of basic words and phrases

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to pronounce, read and recognise Russian alphabet and read/recognise some basic words and phrases

## Week 2: UNIT 1

**Language functions**

- Introductions: saying hello, good bye, my name is…, etc.
- Stating what you do
- Saying/replying to "how are you?"

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Verb "to be" in present tense
- Review of gender (nouns, some personal/possessive pronouns)

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say hello, present oneself, answer/ask relevant questions
- To be able to say how are you?

## Week 3: UNIT 2

> [!important] Assessment
> **Written Assignment 1**

**Language functions**

- Stating where you come from
- Talking about countries/nationalities
- Stating what languages you speak

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Introduction to verbal conjugation
- Initial review of case usage (Prepositional Case) - phrases "I am from…", "I live in/I was born in…"
- Verb "to speak"
- Basic ordinal numbers

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say where you are from
- To be able to talk about countries and nationalities
- To be able to state which languages one speaks

**Written Assignment 1**

## Week 4: UNIT 3

**Language functions**

- Stating what you study/where you work/what is your area of research

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- General review of verbal conjugation. Verbs "to work", "to study"
- Use of Accusative Case (phrases "I study…")
- Use of Instrumental Case (phrases "I work as…")

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say what is your profession/occupation/area of study and/or research
- To be able to conjugate some basic verbs
- To recognise gender of nouns
- To use Accusative and Instrumental cases in some set phrases

## Week 5: UNIT 4

**Language functions**

- Talk about one's family
- Talk about one's age and other people's age

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Verbal phrases of possession in affirmative and negative: "I have – I don't have"
- Conjugation of verbs "to like", "to see"
- Review of relationships between nouns and adjectives
- Use of Dative Case in stating age
- Review of cardinal numerals

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's family, marital status, etc.
- To be able to state one's age.
- To be able to express likes/dislikes
- To be able to agree basic nouns and adjectives

## Week 7: UNIT 5

> [!important] Assessment
> **Grammar Assessment 1**
> **Assessment 1: Grammar test of approximately 15 minutes to cover Weeks 1-6**

**Language functions**

- **Grammar Assessment 1**
- Talk about hobbies, interests, daily routine
- Talk about days of the week and times of day

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- ***Assessment 1: Grammar test of approximately 15 minutes to cover Weeks 1-6***
- Use of Instrumental and Accusative cases in relevant set phrases
- Use of relevant prepositions and cases when referring to days of the week/times of day

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's hobbies, interests and routine
- To be able to refer to days of the week/times of day

## Week 8: UNITS 6 and 7

**Language functions**

- Talk about main meals of the day, types of food
- Talk about one's preferences in food
- Talk about traditional Russian cuisine
- Ordering food in a café/restaurant

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Use of verbs "to eat", "to drink", "to prefer", etc.
- Use of expressions "do you have + Accusative Case" in affirmative and negative
- Use of double negative
- Review of the use of adjectives and adverbs in appropriate contexts
- Expressions of request

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about meals of the day
- To be able to identify and talk about traditional Russian cuisine
- To be able to read and understand a Russian menu, ask for a bill, etc.

## Week 9: NO RELEVANT UNIT

> [!important] Assessment
> **Written Assignment 2**

**Language functions**

- Describe where you went and what you did during your last holiday
- Talk about the way things were when you were younger

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Use of past tense
- Review of verbal conjugation in past tense
- Review of the Verbs of motion
- This year, last year, when I was + comparative adjective
- Prepositions to express periods of time/ duration

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about events in the past
- To be able to describe one's holiday or experience (in the past) to include verbs of motion
- To use comparative form of adjectives ("younger", "older", "earlier")

**Written Assignment 2**

## Week 10: UNIT 8

**Language functions**

- Talk about booking a hotel
- Asking and receiving details about accommodation, hotel's amenities, etc.
- Making a reservation

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Review/refine expressions of request
- Review of Genitive and Prepositional case usage
- Ordinal numerals in reference to floors

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to ask and receive information about hotel bookings/amenities/facilities
- To be able to identify which floor a room/facility is on

## Week 11: UNITS 9 and 10

**Language functions**

- Talk about travelling by metro, train, plane
- Talk about sightseeing, asking directions
- Talk about places of interest in Russia

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Review Verbs of motion in various contexts of travel
- Review use of adverbs and adjectives

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to find one's way
- To be able to buy/book tickets for travel
- To be able to talk about places of interest in Russia
