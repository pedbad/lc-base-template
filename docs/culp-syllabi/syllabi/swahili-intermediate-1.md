## Lesson 1: Introducing yourself and others (i.e. family and Friends)

**Language Function**

- Talking about yourself, family members and friends

**Grammar Structure**

- Personal and Demonstrative pronouns
- Present and Habitual tenses

**Work after Class**

- Writing profile and simple biography

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to introduce themselves, and talk about family and other people

## Lesson 2: Daily routine - activities, hobbies, food, drinks, likes and dislikes

**Language Function**

- Talking about personal habits
- Expressing likes and dislikes
- Expressing the reasons for likes and dislikes
- Expressing preferences

**Grammar Structure**

- Habitual & present tenses – affirmative and negative forms
- Verb to – is/are: affirmative and negative
- Comparative markers

**Work after Class**

- Preparing a timetable or a table of daily events/activities
- Writing a passage about likes /dislikes, hobbies and preferences

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about everyday activities, events and their preferences

## Lesson 3: Shopping: Clothes and foodstuff including the concept of bargaining

**Language Function**

- Buying clothes, talking about prices, colours, design and quality
- Negotiating prices and expressing preferences

**Grammar Structure**

- Demonstrative pronouns
- Adjectives and noun class 9/10
- Subjunctive forms

**Work after Class**

- Writing a dialogue between the seller and the customer buying, selling and negotiating prices

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to do shopping, asking the price and negotiate prices

## Lesson 4: Shopping: foodstuff (Continued)

**Language Function**

- Buying foodstuff, talking about taste i.e. tasty, sour etc.
- Talking about healthy/unhealthy foods and drinks

**Grammar Structure**

- Adjectives and noun class agreements
- Demonstrative pronouns
- Subjunctive forms

**Work after Class**

- Writing a dialogue about buying, selling and negotiating prices

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to do shopping, asking the price and negotiate prices

## Lesson 5: Talking about future plans and dreams (i.e. Travel)

**Language Function**

- Expressing future plans, intentions, dreams and expectations
- Talking about future career plans

**Grammar Structure**

- Future tenses and future conditional *-ki* and *–po* (when)

**Work after Class**

- Writing a passage expressing future plans, dreams etc.

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about what they will do in the future, their dreams and plans

## Lesson 6: Swahili recipes and ordering meal and drinks & Revision

**Language Function**

- Talk and express opinions on different culture foods and drinks
- Talk about familiar recipes of preferences
- Talk and demonstrate how to prepare food of choice
- Order meals and drinks in hotels and restaurants

**Grammar Structure**

- Imperative verbs and their uses i.e. subjunctive
- Conditional forms– *nge*

**Work after Class**

- Writing a passage or step by step instructions on how to prepare food of choice i.e. omelette
- Preparing a dialogue ordering meal and drinks in a restaurant

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about different culture food, order meal or drinks in a Hotel or restaurant and be able to give simple cooking instructions

## Lesson 7: Talking about Swahili East Africa: cities, hotels, game parks, beaches etc.

> [!important] Assessment
> **(Short Quiz)**

**Language Function**

- Name, compare and contrast different three East African cities: Nairobi Kampala and Tanzania
- Talk about Safari at East Africa game parks and about animals

**Grammar Structure**

- PO-KO-MO locative class
- The use of '*kuna*', '*ingi*'
- –me +sha tense: 'already' and 'yet'

**Work after Class**

- Writing short passage(s) about East Africa cities – geographical location, population, language etc.

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about three major cities of East Africa and compare between them

## Lesson 8: Talking about Swahili East Africa: game parks, beaches and hotels (Continued)

**Language Function**

- Compare different hotels in terms of cost, quality services etc. in East Africa and express opinions on favourite accommodation
- Talk about beaches in East Africa coast

**Grammar Structure**

- Comparative adjectives
- The use of 'kuna'; '*–ingi*'
- –me +sha tense 'already' and 'yet'

**Work after Class**

- Writing short passages about East Africa Safari at game parks, beaches and/or hotels

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about animals, beaches and hotels in East Africa

## Lesson 9: Talking about major cities worldwide weather, population, cost of living and safety

**Language Function**

- Compare different world's major cities in terms of costs of living, safety etc.
- Talking about population and geographical locations of selected cities
- Talking about notable features, symbols that makes the city famous

**Grammar Structure**

- The use of '*kuna*'; '*–ingi*'
- –me +sha tense 'already' and 'yet'

**Work after Class**

- Writing a passage to compare life, weather, safety, population etc of different major cities of the world

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk and compare between different major cities of the world

## Lesson 10: Renting accommodation

**Language Function**

- Finding a place to live
- Describing living conditions

**Grammar Structure**

- Noun Class agreement 7/8; 9/10 etc.
- PO-KO-MO locative class
- The use of *'kuna'*; '*hakuna*'; and *'enye'*

**Work after Class**

- Writing a dialogue between landlord/lady and the tenant about renting a room, flat or a house

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about accommodation and rent a room, flat, house etc

## Lesson 11: Giving advice, opinions and expressing feelings

**Language Function**

- Advise others on different matters arising or facing them
- Express opinions and feelings on different issues or views
- Take part in the exchange of feelings, ideas, and opinions with others

**Grammar Structure**

- Subjunctive verbs i.e. *Inabidi ufanye kazi* (You ought to do the work)
- Comparative– 'kuliko' (than)

**Work after Class**

- Preparing a short role play showing an expert giving advice to the client on familiar issues or matters

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to advise someone and express own feelings on different familiar issues

## Lesson 12: Story telling in Swahili culture & Revision

**Language Function**

- Talk about storytelling tradition
- Tell an interesting story or a funny story, tale or joke

**Grammar Structure**

- Past tense *–li-* and *-ka-* narrative past tense

**Work after Class**

- Writing a short fictional or non-fictional story. Students may also write a funny story or a joke

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to narrate a story/tell a funny story

## Lesson 13: Understanding Swahili Culture - Wedding customs, Beliefs etc

> [!important] Assessment
> **(Short quiz)**

**Language Function**

- Talk and compare different wedding customs, beliefs and traditions
- Talk about Swahili superstitions

**Grammar Structure**

- Adjectives and noun class agreement
- Habitual tense

**Work after Class**

- Writing a passage about Swahili wedding customs and beliefs or superstitions

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to understand and appreciate Swahili Culture as well as comparing and talking about Swahili and other cultures

## Lesson 14: Understanding Swahili culture (continued) and LISTENING EXAM

> [!important] Assessment
> **LISTENING EXAM**

**Language Function**

- Talk about Kanga and names on Kanga
- Talk about Swahili music, festivities and eating etiquette

**Grammar Structure**

- Comparative adjectives
- Noun class agreement
- Habitual tense

**Work after Class**

- Writing about the significance of message on Kanga or Swahili music, festivities and eating manners

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to understand and appreciate Swahili Culture as well as comparing and talking about Swahili and other cultures
