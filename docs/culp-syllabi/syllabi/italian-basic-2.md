## Lesson 1: Talking about yourself

**Language Functions**

- Introductions
- Stating your marital status
- Stating your occupation
- Stating your nationality
- Saying where you come from and which languages you speak
- Saying your age and your birthday

**Grammar structures**

- Present tense
- Regular are verbs
- Some irregular verbs
- Negation
- Adjectives of nationality
- Interrogatives
- Definite and indefinite articles
- Cardinals 1 – 100
- Registers: tu/Lei

**Work after class**

- JiTG: Essentials
- JiTG: L'indicativo
- Presente indicativo esercizi: http://telelinea.free.fr/italien/presente.htm

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- Be able to present oneself and ask interlocutor for information.

## Lesson 2: Talking about friends, family & neighbours

**Language Functions**

- Talking about other people
- Describing appearances and personalities
- Talking about occupations

**Grammar structures**

- Interrogatives
- Agreement and position of adjectives
- Possessive adjectives
- The verb *fare*
- *Prepositions*
- Definite articles

**Work after class**

- JiTG: Preposizioni
- Preposizioni esercizi: http://telelinea.free.fr/italien/preposizioni.htm

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- Talk about friends and family inclusive of professions.

## Lesson 3: Talking about where you live

> [!important] Assessment
> **Moodle test no. 1**

**Language Functions**

- Saying where you live
- Describing your house/flat/room
- Saying where your family lives
- Stating your place and date of birth

**Grammar structures**

- *Vivere, abitare*
- Prepositions before countries and towns
- Prepositional phrases: in campagna, al primo piano etc.
- Agreement & position of adjectives
- More interrogatives
- *Il condizionale*

**Work after class**

- Moodle test no. 1

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to describe one's dwelling and their flatmates/family members.

## Lesson 4: Shopping

**Language Functions**

- Stating your requirements
- Talking about the colour, size and fabric of the required garment
- Saying whether a garment fits or suits someone

**Grammar structures**

- *Cercare*
- Direct object pronouns (*la prendo* etc.)
- Indirect object pronouns (*gli do la chiave* etc.)

**Work after class**

- Online Mat. Unit 10
- Fare lo shopping: https://quizlet.com/65512176/espressioni-di-fare-lo-shopping-flash-cards/
- Direct object pronouns esercizi: http://telelinea.free.fr/italien/pronomi-dir.htm

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To ask for information in shops and purchase items of interest.

## Lesson 5: Eating out & eating in, cooking

**Language Functions**

- Talking about mealtimes and what's eaten when
- Reserving a table in a restaurant
- Ordering a meal
- Paying and querying the bill
- Read recipes

**Grammar structures**

- *Prendere*
- *Mangiare*
- *Bere*
- *Prenotare*
- *Preferire*
- *Mi piace*
- *Potere* + infinitive

**Work after class**

- Al ristorante: https://quizlet.com/62392736/al-ristorante-flash-cards/
- Al bar: https://quizlet.com/subject/al-bar/
- Cucinare: https://quizlet.com/83125480/cucinare-flash-cards/
- Al dente: http://www.scudit.net/mdaldente.htm
- Esercizi *al dente*: http://www.scudit.net/mdaldente_esesol.htm

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to order drinks in a bar, meals in restaurants and to read a recipe.

## Lesson 7: Discovering an Italian city

> [!important] Assessment
> **(ASSESSMENT)**

**Language Functions**

- Saying where the city is
- Describing it
- Giving your opinion
- Talking about the local customs, the architecture, the art and the cuisine

**Grammar structures**

- Definite articles
- *Dove si trova..?*
- Prepositions before continents, countries, towns
- Expressions with *fare*
- The expression: *c'è, ci sono*
- *Il congiuntivo presente*

**Work after class**

- JiTG: L'indicativo & il congiuntivo
- Il congiuntivo - esercizi: http://telelinea.free.fr/italien/congiuntivo.htm

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to describe an Italian city and your likes and dislikes about it.

## Lesson 10: Making hotel reservations

**Language Functions**

- Making a hotel reservation
- Checking in
- Dealing with problems in the hotel.
- Talking about past experience while on travel.

**Grammar structures**

- Modal verbs
- Expressions with *avere*
- *Passato prossimo* of the modal verbs
- *Prima di* +infinitive

**Work after class**

- I verbi modali - esercizi: http://www.speakitalian.org/italian-modal-verbs.html#.V9AqkIVDTL4

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to make hotel reservations and make yourself understood.
- Inquire about tourist attractions and places to visit.
- Describe your trip and stay.
