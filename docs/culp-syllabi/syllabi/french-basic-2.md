# Basic 2 French Syllabus

*Last updated: Tuesday, 13 October 2020*

**Prerequisites:** This course is appropriate for students who already have **a little knowledge of the French language and / or already speak or have studied another Romance language.** [If you already have GCSE French, Intermediate 1 may suit you better.]

**Target level:** Level A2 of the CEFR

## Approach

This course provides the linguistic foundation and skills necessary for communication in predictable everyday situations. Whilst all four skills, speaking, reading, writing and listening will be developed, the primary focus will be on developing speech and conversation. Ultimately, it provides an initial understanding of the cultures within the French-speaking world.

The classes are task-oriented, helping students understand French in use and to acquire it by use. All four communicative skills will be developed and/or strengthened – listening, speaking, reading and writing. The students will engage in interactive language activities, participating in group and pair work according to a syllabus based on systematic grammatical progression. Each class focuses on a topic that is related to the previous and following one; activities build on the students' abilities and on their previous knowledge. Grammar and vocabulary are practised and perfected in context.

The course is divided into 15 sessions.

## Course main objectives

- To provide a basic knowledge of French culture and language.
- To compare the differences between the student's own culture and that of French-speaking countries.
- To understand and use grammatical structures and vocabulary.
- To strengthen a positive and confident attitude towards language learning
- To be able to understand and exchange general and detailed information and to communicate in standard speech on a range of everyday issues.

## Lesson 1: TALKING ABOUT YOURSELF

**Language Functions**

- Greetings
- Introductions
- Stating your marital status
- Stating your occupation
- Stating your nationality
- Saying where you come from and which languages you speak
- Saying your age and your birthday

**Grammar and structures**

- Present tense:
  - Regular –er verbs: parler, habiter, travailler, étudier
  - Irregular verbs: s'appeler, être, avoir, venir de
- Negation
- adjectives of nationality
- interrogatives: Comment? Où? D'où?
- Definite and Indefinite articles
- cardinals 1 – 100
- registers: tu/vous

**Work after class**

- Se présenter: https://www.podcastfrancaisfacile.com/videos/mylene.html
- **Alphabet and Pronunciation**
- **Numbers:**
  - 0 - 100

- Agreement and position of adjectives
- Possessive adjectives

## Lesson 4: EATING IN & EATING OUT

**Language Functions**

- Talking about meals and mealtimes in France
- Comparing the above with meals in your own country
- Reserving a table
- Ordering and paying for a meal
- Querying the bill

**Grammar and structures**

- prendre (to take)
- manger (to eat)
- boire (to drink)
- réserver (to book)
- pouvoir (to be able) + infinitive

**Work after class**

- **Au restaurant**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VKLkAKQocjE

## Lesson 5: TALKING ABOUT FREE TIME

**Language Functions**

- Stating preferences, likes and dislikes
- Talking about hobbies, and cultural interests

**Grammar and structures**

- present tense:
  - aimer (to like)
  - détester (to hate)
  - préférer (to prefer)
- faire (to do) + partitives
- jouer à (to play)
- jouer de
- aller à (to go to)

**Work after class**
