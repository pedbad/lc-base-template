## Lesson 1: Greetings & Introductions

**Language Functions**

- Greeting politely, asking/giving names, saying how you are

**Grammar and Structures**

- Subject pronouns
- "to be" (ni/siyo)
- question forms (jina lako nani?)

**Work After Class**

- Practice introducing yourself in writing
- record a short greeting audio

## Lesson 2: Personal Information & Professions

**Language Functions**

- Introducing self/others
- giving basic details (where from, what you do)
- family members & professions (doctor, nurse, surgeon, midwife)

**Grammar and Structures**

- Possessives (mtoto wangu, mke wangu)
- noun classes for people (mgonjwa, daktari)
- simple present tense

**Work After Class**

- Make a family tree and label members/professions in Swahili

## Lesson 3: Numbers, Dates & Age

**Language Functions**

- Giving age, phone number, date of birth
- exchanging numerical info

**Grammar and Structures**

- Numbers 1–100
- "kuwa na" (to have)
- dates and days of week

**Work After Class**

- Write your date of birth and 3 friends' birthdays in Swahili

## Lesson 4: Time & Daily Activities

**Language Functions**

- Talking about time, daily routines, physical activities
- arranging/attending appointments

**Grammar and Structures**

- Telling time
- present tense verbs (kuamka, kula, kufanya kazi)
- adverbs of frequency

**Work After Class**

- Keep a one-day diary in Swahili with times and main activities

## Lesson 5: Likes & Dislikes (Food & Health)

**Language Functions**

- Expressing preferences, describing tastes (sweet, salty, oily)
- talking about healthy/unhealthy foods

**Grammar and Structures**

- "Napenda / sipendi"
- adjectives (tamu, chungu, mafuta mengi)
- negation

**Work After Class**

- Write 5 sentences about foods you like/dislike and why

## Lesson 6: Descriptions of People

**Language Functions**

- Describing appearance (hair, eyes, height, weight)
- describing personality traits in medical setting

**Grammar and Structures**

- Adjectives (refu, mfupi, mwembamba, mnene, mchovu, mchangamfu)
- agreement rules

**Work After Class**

- Write 5 sentences describing classmates or family members

## Lesson 7: Parts of the Body & Common Ailments

**Language Functions**

- Naming body parts
- saying what hurts
- describing symptoms (fever, pain, fatigue)

**Grammar and Structures**

- Possessives (kichwa changu kinauma)
- "nina…" vs "sina…"
- using 2nd/3rd person forms

**Work After Class**

- Label a diagram of a body in Swahili
- write 3 "doctor–patient" dialogues

## Lesson 8: Places in the Hospital

**Language Functions**

- Naming hospital areas (ward, theatre, reception)
- giving/asking for directions

**Grammar and Structures**

- Location prepositions (katika, upande wa kulia/kushoto)
- "iko / ziko" structures

**Work After Class**

- Draw a simple hospital map, label rooms, write 3 direction instructions

## Lesson 9: Instructions & Future Plans

**Language Functions**

- Giving medical instructions ("Drink water, Rest")
- talking about future appointments

**Grammar and Structures**

- Imperatives (kunywa!, pumzika!)
- simple future tense (nitakuona, tutafanyia upasuaji)

**Work After Class**

- Write 5 imperative sentences a doctor might say to a patient

## Lesson 10: Consolidation & Orals

> [!important] Assessment
> **Oral presentation (live, with Q&A) – 50%**: prepare and present a short oral (3–5 mins)

**Language Functions**

- Revision of topics
- oral presentations
- role play doctor-patient conversations

**Grammar and Structures**

- Integrated use of grammar
- emphasis on fluency and accuracy

**Work After Class**

- Prepare and present a short oral (3–5 mins) using vocabulary from lessons
