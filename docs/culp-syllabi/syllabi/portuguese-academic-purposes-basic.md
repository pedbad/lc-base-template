# Brazilian Portuguese for Academic Purposes (LAP) Basic Course

## Weeks at a glance

| Week | Grammar |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Present tense of regular verbs; ser and estar; articles; gender; number; numbers |
| 2 | Interrogative pronouns; exclamations; personal pronouns |
| 3 | Demonstratives; possessives; relative pronouns |
| 4 | Indefinite adjectives and pronouns; negatives |
| 5 | Prepositions; review of structures |
| 6 | Haver and ter; Simple Past (Indicative) |
| 7 | Imperfect Past; Passado mais-que-perfeito; compound tenses (Indicative) |
| 8 | Future (simple and compound – Indicative) |
| 9 | Present and Future (Subjunctive) |
| 10 | Passive voice |
| 11 | Pretérito Imperfeito (Subjunctive) |
| 12 | Word order; word formation |
| 13 | Functions of "se" |
| 14 | Direct and Indirect speech |
| 15 | Review |

## Week 1

**Reading Material**

- Short story: "Redação" by Adriana Falcão
- Poems:
  1. "Amor Feinho" by Adélia Prado
  2. "Verbo ser" by Carlos Drummond de Andrade
  3. Trechos do texto de Filosofia: Chauí, M. "A Universidade Pública sob nova perspectiva" http://www.scielo.br/pdf/rbedu/n24/n24a02.pdf

**Grammar**

- Present tense of regular verbs
- Irregular verbs: *ser* and *estar*
- Articles
- Gender and Gender Agreement
- Number and number agreement
- Numbers and numerical expressions

## Week 2

**Reading Material**

1. Short Story: "Conversa" by Livia Garcia-Roza
2. Poem: "Por que não haverá de mim um deus" by Ricardo Reis
3. Trechos da entrevista sobre Antropologia: "De Lévi-Strauss aos Índios na Universidade". http://www.revistas.usp.br/ra/article/view/108685/107422

**Grammar**

- Interrogative pronouns
- Exclamations
- Personal Pronouns
