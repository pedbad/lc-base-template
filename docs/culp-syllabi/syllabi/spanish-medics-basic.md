## Lesson 1: Introducing ourselves

**Language Functions**

- Greetings
- Introductions: formal / informal
- Nationality, language
- Marital status

**Grammar structures**

- Interrogative pronouns
- Reflexive verb: *me llamo…*
- Verb SER
- Subject personal pronouns
- *tú* and *usted* (formal and informal registers)
- Demonstrative adjectives: *este/esta*
- The numbers 0-30

## Lesson 3: Talking about daily routine

**Language Functions**

- Talking about studies/work
- Telling the time
- Describing activities and timetables

**Grammar structures**

- Present Indicative of –AR (*trabajar*) –ER (*comer*) and –IR (*vivir*) verbs
- Stem-changing verbs (*empiezo, me acuesto*)
- Reflexive verbs (*me levanto, me baño*).

## Lesson 8: Having a drink and a snack; Having a meal at a restaurant

**Language Functions**

- Ordering drinks and snacks
- Asking for the bill
- Booking a table
- Choosing from a menu and ordering in a restaurant

**Grammar structures**

- Impersonal HABER (*hay…*)
- Verb QUERER
- Indirect object pronouns: *me, nos* e.g. '*¿Nos trae la cuenta por favor?*'
- Countable and non-countable nouns, e.g. *¿Me trae otro café? ¿Nos trae un poco (más) de agua?*
