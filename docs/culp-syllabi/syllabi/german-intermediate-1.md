## Lesson 1: Erster Kontakt (First Contact)

**Language Functions**

- Introductions
- Saying your name, age, your place and date of birth
- Stating your nationality
- Saying which languages you speak
- Stating your occupation

**Grammar structures**

- haben /sein
- reflexive adjectives
- possessive adjectives
- questions
- nationalities
- kommen aus
- accusative
- articles

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to introduce oneself and others, to be able to speak about one's background, nationality, languages and occupation and to be able to ask people relevant questions to obtain the same information

## Lesson 2: Freizeitaktivitäten; Essen gehen und zuhause kochen (Spare time; eating out and cooking at home)

**Language Functions**

- Saying which sports interest you
- Saying what your hobbies / interests are
- Saying what you do at the week-end
- Ordering food and drink in a restaurant
- Paying the bill
- Buying food
- Talking about ingredients and recipes

**Grammar structures**

- verbs with sep + insep. prefix
- word order (inversion)
- subjunctive of modal + auxiliary verbs
- negation

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's own interests, sport, hobbies, weekend activities.
- To be able to make a restaurant booking, read the menu, ask about the dishes, place order, talk about food, ask for the bill and ask for info re the bill.
- To be able to talk about buying and preparing foods, exchanging recipes.

## Lesson 3: Tagesablauf; Leben in der Stadt oder auf dem Land (Daily routine; Living in the city or in the country)

> [!important] Assessment
> **Written homework 1**[^3]

**Language Functions**

- Describing your daily routine when in Cambridge / when at home.
- Saying where you live, who you live with
- Describing your accommodation
- Talking about friends and family.
- Expressing your likes and dislikes; where you would prefer to live: city or country and why

**Grammar structures**

- present and perfect tense
- past participle
- adjectival endings
- adverbs of time and manner
- word order + conjunctions
- use of prepositions
- comparison of adjectives
- use of conditional

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's daily routine, one's living situation, one's family and friends.
- To be able to express one's likes and dislikes, the pros and the cons of city vs. country life.
- **Written homework 1**

## Lesson 4: Gesundheit und Fitness (Health and fitness)

**Language Functions**

- Making an appointment at the doctor's
- Saying what hurts/ how you are feeling
- Understanding prescriptions
- Talking about healthy eating and lifestyle, as well as sports
- Buying food items at the market / in the Health-food store

**Grammar structures**

- infinitive with *zu*
- modal verbs
- imperative

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to make an appointment to see a doctor. To be able to talk about one's health and describe ailments, understand instructions and discuss them.
- To be able to talk about healthy lifestyle, sports, health foods

## Lesson 5: Reisen und Reservierungen; Wegbeschreibung (Travelling and Reservations; directions)

**Language Functions**

- Planning a holiday
- Finding a hotel / campsite
- Buying a train/plane ticket
- Making a reservation
- Asking your way
- Finding your way on foot / by public transport / as a driver
- Complaining about the room.

**Grammar structures**

- time phrase + present tense
- future tense
- prepositions/ dual prepositions
- comparison of adjectives

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to ask for /give directions, understand indications, traffic signs.
- To be able to call a taxi, to explain where to go.
- To be able to buy train tickets, reserve seats, book hotels, complain about the room.

## Lesson 7: Assessed coursework 1 + Sehenswürdigkeiten und Stadtbummel (Sightseeing and shopping in town) + Seasonal Context

> [!important] Assessment
> **Assessed coursework 1**[^1]: Grammar test in class (15 mins.) on lessons 1-6 (10% of final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Visiting sights and museums
- Describing places of interest
- Looking for the shops
- Asking about products.
- Looking for presents.
- Seasonal context: Christmas market

**Grammar structures**

- link words + word order
- adverbs referring to place
- subjunctive 2
- compound nouns

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to describe places and objects of interest.
- To be able to buy food and inquire about prices, size, quantity and get other relevant information on products.
- To learn about Christmas in German speaking countries.

## Lesson 9: Medien und Kommunikation (Media and communication)

**Language Functions**

- Ringing up people
- Leaving a message on the answerphone
- Writing emails and formal letters
- Media in Germany
- Talking about German newspapers/ television programmes
- internet + social networks

**Grammar structures**

- indirect questions
- relative pronouns

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to answer the phone, hold a telephone conversation, leave messages, make an appointment.
- To be able to write emails and more or less formal letters.

**Language Functions**

- Exploring Germany and German speaking countries geographically, politically, economically and socially

**Grammar structures**

- conjunctions / subjunctions

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about the geography of German speaking countries.
- To be able to talk about different varieties of German.
- To be able to talk about living in Germany, and what Germany means to you.

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

## Lesson 13: Assessed coursework 2 + Über die Vergangenheit sprechen (1); Mauerbau, DDR und Wiedervereinigung (2)

*Talking about the past. (GDR: Building the wall; reunification)*

> [!important] Assessment
> **Assessed coursework 2**[^2]: Written essay in German on given topic (120 words) in class; 15 mins. (10% of final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Saying where you went and what you did during the last holiday.
- Talking about past events in general
- Saying how things were when you were a child / younger
- History of postwar Germany
- Building of the wall
- Life in the GDR
- The reunification
- 'Ostalgie'

**Grammar structures**

- simple past
- present perfect
- adverbs referring to time + word order

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to relate an incident.
- To be able to tell a story.
- To be able to talk about your childhood /past.
- To be able to talk about the recent history of Germany.
