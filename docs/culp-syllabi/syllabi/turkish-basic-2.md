## Lesson 1: Time and tenses

**Language Functions**

**Be a thing of the past**

- Remembering the three tenses and explaining a routine and being in a conversation in the past, present and future.
- Learning the differences between two past tenses (narrative and simple past)
- Talking about past events.
- Adverbs of past tense.
- Asking questions in the narrative past

**Grammar structures**

- The narrative past
- Noun+before/after
- Kahvaltıdan önce (before the breakfast)
- Kahvaltı yapmadan önce (before doing breakfast)
- Alışverişten sonra (After shopping)
- Alışveriş yaptıktan sonra (after doing shopping)

**Work after class**

- Workbook, pages 7-8-9-10-11.
- Writing: Work book, page 12 convert the text from the simple past tense to the narrative past.

## Lesson 2: Healthy life

**Language Functions**

**Just what the doctor ordered!**

- Being in a dialogue with the doctor or staff at a hospital
- Learning vocabulary of illnesses and symptoms.

**Grammar structures**

- The narrative form in a noun sentence: Hava soğukmuş. (It was cold)
- Postpositions: gibi/kadar
- Interrogatives and answers: niçin?/neden?-çünkü (Why?-because)
- Stressing adjectives
  - kırmızı>kıpkırmızı red>very red
  - mavi>masmavi blue>very blue

**Work after class**

- Work book, pages 19-20-21-22-23-24-25.
- Writing: write a dialogue like page 15.
- Listening: The dialog between the patient and the doctor: http://turkceninsesi.yee.org.tr/programlar/hayatin-icinden-turkce./hayat%C4%B1n-icinden11-hastane

## Lesson 4: Your preferences

**Language Functions**

**Pick 'n' mix**

- Inviting friends to an event
- Replying to an invitation
- Expressing your preference

**Grammar structures**

- The use of "bana göre", "bence" (according to me, in my opinion)
- The structure of
  - hem… hem (as well as)
  - ne…ne (neither…nor)
  - ya…ya (either…or)
  - ister… ister
  - belki… belki

**Work after class**

- Workbook, page 38-39.
- Writing: write an email and invite your friend to your event using the new structures which you have learnt. (Please write your text on a separate page as the teacher will take them to correct your mistakes and give them back the following week.)

## Lesson 5: Describe your country

**Language Functions**

**Love of country**

- Talking about Turkey, its cuisine, best places to see.
- Talking about your own country.

**Grammar structures**

- Verbal nouns: -mak, -mek, -ma, -me, -iş, -ış, -uş, -üş.
- Using these suffixes with case suffixes
- İstanbul'u görmeyi çok istiyorum. (I really want to see İstanbul).

**Work after class**

- Workbook, pages 42-45-47-49.

## Lesson 6: Where do you go for holiday?

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY READING MOODLE QUIZ (2)**

**Language Functions**

**A place in the sun!**

- Describing places, weather and nature.
- Understanding an advert about a holiday resort
- What kind of holidays do you prefer?
- Talking about traditional food in Turkey and in your country.
- Role play between the staff in a travel agency and the customer.

**Grammar structures**

- Reported speech (imperative form)
- Görevli "Rezervasyon yapın" dedi. (The staff said "please make a reservation")
- Görevli rezervasyon yapmamı istedi. (The staff asked me to make a reservation)

**Work after class**

- Workbook, pages 50-51.
- Writing: Describe your dream holiday include all details; place, weather etc.

## Lesson 9: What can you do?

**Language Functions**

**To the best of your ability**

- Talking about your ability
- Using the –ebilmek structure in different functions: possibility, ability, request, asking for permission.
- Role play: meeting a new neighbour
- Introducing a literary genre: fıkra
- Introducing performing art in the Ottoman era: Meddahlık

**Grammar structures**

- The structure of –abilmek/-ebilmek and its negative form.
- O ud çalabiliyor. (She can play Oud)
- The differences between the structures:
  - Yapamam (I cannot do)
  - Yapmayabilirim (I may not do)
  - Yapamayabilirim (I may not do)

**Work after class**

- Workbook, pages 66-67-68-69-72-73
- Writing: Write a dialog using the –ebilmek structure. See an example on work book page 65.

## Lesson 12: Personality characteristics

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY LISTENING MOODLE QUIZ (4)**

**Language Functions**

**What kind of person are you?:)**

- Describing your character and your best friend.
- Learning new vocabulary which describes your mood. Bireyci (individualist), hoşgörülü (tolerant), mükemmeliyetçi (perfectionist), yardımsever (helpful), maceracı (adventurer) etc.
- Listening practice: Let's find out about your character from your favourite colour.

**Grammar structures**

- The participle suffix: -an/en
- Top oynayan çocuk çok neşeli. (The child who is playing with a ball is very cheerful.)
