## Lesson 1: Greetings and Introducing yourself

**Language Functions**

- Introduction to pronunciation and transliteration scheme
- Greetings at a different time of day;
- Introductions
- Arabic Script: Introduction
- Letters of the alphabet: group 1

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Expressions of greeting;
- 'How' question
- Personal pronouns ('I'; 'you', m. + f. sing.; 'he', 'she')
- Possession ('my'; 'your', m. + f. sing.)
- Yes / no
- Basic negation
- 'What' question

**Work after Class**

- TA Unit 1
- Writing practice joining letters
- Arabic Essentials

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to greet and introduce oneself on a formal and informal occasion

## Lesson 2: Talk about where you come from and your nationality

**Language Functions**

- Review
- Letters of the alphabet: group 2
- Saying where one is from
- Countries
- Nationalities

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- 'Where' question
- 'من' preposition
- The definite article
- Sun/moon letters
- Nisba adjective
- Arabic vowels
- Numbers 0-10

**Work after Class**

- TA Unit 2
- MA Unit 2: handwriting exercises
- Grammar exercise

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to give basic information about oneself

## Lesson 3: Saying where you live; Talk about your Family

**Language Functions**

- Saying where one lives
- Giving your phone number
- Letters of the alphabet: group 3
- Titles and terms of address
- Introducing family

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Active participle ساكن/ة
- Demonstrative pronoun (sing.)
- 'في' preposition
- Negation
- Numbers 11-20

**Work after Class**

- TA Unit 2 and 3
- MA Unit 3: handwriting exercises and reading practice
- Grammar exercise

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to give further information about yourself and your family

## Lesson 4: Talk about your friends, age and profession

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**

**Language Functions**

- Saying what you do for a living
- Saying how old you are
- Arabic script: group 4

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Simple present tense
- Usage of verb 'to like'
- Usage of *biddi* 'to want'
- 'With' and 'without'
- Sound plurals

**Work after Class**

- TA Unit 3
- Handwriting exercises Reading practice
- **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about family, friends, profession and give your age

## Lesson 5: At the cafe

**Language Functions**

Review of Lessons 1-4

- Ordering tea, coffee and other drinks
- Offering, refusing, accepting
- Asking and paying for the bill
- Please / thanks and responses
- Arabic script: group 5

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- 'أيّ' ('which'?)
- Usage of verb 'to drink'
- Usage of verb 'to take'
- Numbers 21-100
- Simple present tense
- 'With' and 'without'

**Work after Class**

- TA Unit 5: handwriting exercises and reading practice

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to order drinks with requirements

## Lesson 6: Around town

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (2)**

**Language Functions**

- Asking what there is …
- … and whereabouts it is
- Telling the time
- Opening and closing times
- Arabic Script: Final letters

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Usage of *mumkin (can?)*
- Usage of verb to say /tell
- Usage of *fiih*
- *Prepositions /adverbs of place*
- *Telling the time*
- *Usage of verb "to open"*
- *Usage of verb "to close"*
- Idafa

**Work after Class**

- TA Unit 5
- Handwriting exercises and reading practice
- **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (2)**

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to find out information about places around town, their whereabouts and opening times

## Lesson 7: How do I get to the hospital etc. …?

**Language Functions**

- Asking the way to the nearest hospital /clinic
- Following directions
- Making enquiries …
- Getting help to understand
- Days of the week

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Superlative
- Verb "to go"
- Verb "to take"
- verb "to turn"
- Ordinal numbers
- imperative
- Numbers 100-2000

**Work after Class**

- TA Unit 6
- Reading and writing exercises

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to ask for and follow directions

## Lesson 8: In the restaurant

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (3)**

**Language Functions**

- Understanding the menu
- Expressing likes and dislikes
- Expressing appreciation

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Review the verb "to like" and negation
- Verb "to be" in the past tense

**Work after Class**

- TA Unit 10
- Reading practice
- Grammar exercises
- **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (3)**

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to order food off the menu

## Lesson 9: Health, feeling unwell

**Language Functions**

- Talking about illness
- Medicines and advice
- Making a medical appointment

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Review the verb "to have" and "to drink"
- Possibilities
- Review the numbers

**Work after Class**

- TA Unit 7
- Review

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to make a simple diagnosis and give advice
