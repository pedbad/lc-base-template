## Topic 1: 黄鹂 Yellow Oriole - A Poem by Du Fu (712 - 770)

**Themes**

- Introduction to the course
- Resources and objectives
- History of Poetry and Song in China
- Brief Introduction to The Book of Poetry (1100 BC - 600 BC)

**Grammar and Usage**

- Vocabulary
- Expressions
- Sentence structure
- Poetry-reading

**Preparation**

- **Read:**
  - *黄鹂 Yellow Oriole* (see pdf file in Moodle)
  - Introduction in *A Brief History of Chinese Poetry: Classical to Contemporary*: https://china360online.org/?property=55-east-52nd-street
- **Watch:**
  - *Du Fu - China's Shakespeare*: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sm0lziolZyM&t=211s
