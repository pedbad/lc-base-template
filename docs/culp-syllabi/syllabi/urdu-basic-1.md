# CULP Urdu – Basic 1 Syllabus

## Lessons at a glance

| Lesson | Objectives /Topics/ Functions | Grammar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One | Greetings; Introducing self and asking very basic questions | SOV structure, pronouns, 'to be' |
| Two | Vocabulary building, counting (numbers 1-13), interrogative | 3rd person Personal Pronouns, Here and there |
| Three | Counting (numbers 14-20), gender distinction and plural formation | Articles, pronouns, possessive pronouns, plurals |
| Four | Asking and answering basic questions about self and others, describing relationships | Possessive pronoun set, whose, which |
| Five | Describing some actions in the present | Prepositional case |
| Six | Time; days of the week; daily routine | Present simple tense |
| Seven | Urdu names for relatives; description of some activities taking place | Present continuous tense; Simple and compound verbs |
| Eight | Ordinal numbers; ability; asking permission; prohibition | Potential tense (present) |
| Nine | Map reading/ following directions; commands; prohibitions | Imperative |
| Ten | Describing future plans; predictions | Future simple |
| Eleven | Describing past and future events with relative clauses | Future continuous; Past continuous |
| Twelve | Describing events of the past; comparing past and present | Past habitual; 'wala' and 'sa' |
| Thirteen | Describing a past event (visit to Pakistan) | Past simple |
| Fourteen | Ordering food; booking a table | Linguistic form of 'need', 'require' etc. |
| Fifteen | Culture and religious practices | Revision of previous topics |

## Lesson One

**Learning resources**

- Immersion lesson
- Teacher's notes

**Objectives /Topics/ Functions**

- Greetings
- Introducing self and asking very basic questions

**Grammar**

- Introduction of SOV structure, 1st (singular) and 2nd (singular/plural) person personal pronouns and their 'to be' verbs, and question words *who, where and what*.

**Script**

- Pronunciation, shapes and joining rules of first three groups.
- in particular of خ and retroflex sound ٹ

**Home work**

- Worksheets for joining letters
- Share 3 sentences on the day's topic on Facebook page (transliteration)
- Find an example of today's lesson in media (drama, video clip, movie, song etc) and share it on the Facebook page
