## Session 1: Meeting New People I

**Language Functions**

- The structure of the Korean language
- The Korean Alphabet I
- Greeting and meeting someone for the first time

**Grammar and Structures**

- How to construct syllables and words
- Korean word order
- Korean sentence structure & distinctive characteristics
- Subject markers
- Basic verb/sentence endings

**Work after Class**

- Learn by heart the alphabet letters covered in class.
- Write a sentence or two introducing yourself

## Session 2: Meeting New People II

**Language Functions**

- The Korean Alphabet II
- Asking about nationalities and names of the countries
- Saying/asking about professions and academic subjects people are studying

**Grammar and Structures**

- More subject markers
- More sentence endings
- Question forms

**Work after Class**

- Learn by heart the alphabet letters covered in class.
- Compile vocabulary used to describe nationalities/ professions/academic subjects

## Session 3: Locations & Directions

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**: Listening and Reading, 5% final mark

**Language Functions**

- The Korean Alphabet III
- Describe where things are
- Give directions
- Negation

**Grammar and Structures**

- Postpositions used to describe where things are or to give directions
- How to say 'there is/are...'
- How to construct negative sentences

**Work after Class**

- Learn by heart all the letters in the Korean alphabet
- Write a paragraph each describing your house/room in relation to where things are and giving directions how to get to your house/room
- **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**

## Session 4: My Day I

**Language Functions**

- Describe daily activities

**Grammar and Structures**

- More postpositions
- Object markers
- Expressions needed to describe a person's daily activities

**Work after Class**

- Write one or more paragraphs describing your daily activities

## Session 5: My Day II

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (2)**: Listening and Reading, 5% final mark

**Language Functions**

- Describe more daily/weekly activities
- hobbies

**Grammar and Structures**

- Asking and answering questions about a person's daily/weekly activities
- Common verbs used to describe a person's activities
- Connectives
- Negating

**Work after Class**

- Complete exercises provided
- **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (2)**

## Session 6: At the Restaurant

**Language Functions**

- Place/receive an order
- Counting
- Names of typical Korean dishes

**Grammar and Structures**

- How to construct a request
- Two different ways of Korean counting and situations where they are used
- Counting units for each object
- **Practice for Writing Assessment 1**

**Work after Class**

- Complete exercises provided

## Session 7: Shopping

> [!important] Assessment
> **Writing Assessment 1**: in class, 10% of the final mark

**Language Functions**

- Korean currency
- Huge numbers
- Asking about products and prices
- Buying something
- Different types of shops

**Grammar and Structures**

- Construct questions to ask about products and prices
- Express whether something is a good price or whether you like it
- Connectives

**Work after Class**

- Complete exercises provided

## Session 8: Time

**Language Functions**

- Tell the time on the clock
- Time of the day, days of the week, and months of the year
- Two different ways of Korean counting

**Grammar and Structures**

- Construct typical question and answer forms to tell the time, the time of the day, the day of the week, and the month of the year
- Commonly used time-related expressions

**Work after Class**

- compile vocabulary related to time
- Complete exercises provided

## Session 9: Making an appointment

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (3)**: Listening and Reading, 5% final mark

**Language Functions**

- More hobbies
- Likes and dislikes
- Making plans with friends

**Grammar and Structures**

- Future tense
- How to construct suggestions
- Agreeing and Disagreeing

**Work after Class**

- Write a paragraph making plans to do your favourite activities with your friend
- **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (3)**

## Session 10: Holiday I

**Language Functions**

**Grammar and Structures**

- Interrogatives

**Work after Class**

- Complete exercises provided

## Session 11: Holiday II

**Grammar and Structures**

**Work after Class**
