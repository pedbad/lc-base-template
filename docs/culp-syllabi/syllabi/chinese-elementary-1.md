## First encounter (weeks 1–4)

### Week 1

**Language function**

- How was your journey?
- Is the hotel far from here?

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 就、只是、之间、离
- 把 sentence

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 1: First Encounter with Mr Zhang

**Objectives**

- To be able to meet and receive someone for the first time;
- To be able to exchange personal information
- To be able to fill out a Chinese arrival card

### Week 2

**Language function**

- Is it your first time to China?
- I lived in London together with my family
- Which section of the company are you going to work in?

**Grammar structure**

- To be able to use particle 了
- Use of 和...一起

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 1: Giving Personal Information

**Objectives**

- To be able to converse about one's study, work and languages spoken.

### Week 3

**Language function**

- Summer weather in Shanghai
- Talk about British football and players

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 又… 又
- 得 have to, must
- Measure word 场

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 1: Making Polite Conversation

**Objectives**

- To be able to make polite conversation on weather, football, etc.

### Week 4

**Language function**

- Hope you will get a good rest today
- Is there anything else you need me to help you with?

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 好好，应该的
- Structure 如果 …就

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 1: Mr Zhang Takes his Leave

**Objectives**

- To be able to read/write one's CV in Chinese

## Induction Day (weeks 5–7)

### Week 5

**Language function**

- I will show you around the company
- What other departments are here apart from the Sales?

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 会、给 and 为
- 除了…还
- 如果…就

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 2: Welcomed by the Human Resources Manager

**Objectives**

- To be able to make introductions among colleagues and some common office talks

### Week 6

**Language function**

- Meeting with the line manager and colleagues
- Being introduced about canteen, office facilities and staff benefits

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 室 and 间
- More on 是 and 有

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 2: Meeting your Line Manager and Colleagues

**Objectives**

- To be able to get familiar with the company working environment and find out new working practice and office facilities

### Week 7

**Language function**

- Discussing about an office outing
- Shall we go to Karaoke this Friday?

**Grammar structure**

- Use of the paired connectors 虽然…但是
- 什么…都 structure

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 2: Joining in an Office Outing

**Objectives**

- To be able to discuss about group activities and accept an invitation; Extract basic info about the company from its website

> [!important] Coursework
> **Writing course work 20 minutes in class**

## Social visit (weeks 8–10)

### Week 8

**Language function**

- Being invited to attend a family meal by a colleague/friend
- Giving Address and detailed directions

**Grammar structure**

- To understand the use of direction indicators such as 出来 and 看到

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 3: Talking about a Further Social Visit

**Objectives**

- To be able to accept an invitation to visit a friend's family; To be able to understand directions

### Week 9

**Language function**

- Do we need to bring a gift when we go for a family meal?
- Choosing a gift based on information about the hobbies of the host

**Grammar structure**

- Use of the indefinite pronouns such as 谁也、什么都
- Use of 别…了

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 3: Choosing a Gift

**Objectives**

- To be able to follow a discussion when choosing a gift

### Week 10

**Language function**

- Discussing about eating culture over a meal
- Talking about food and allergy

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 觉得…怎么样
- Sentences with 把
- Use of conditional structure 如果…的话

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 3: Chatting over a Meal

**Objectives**

- To be able to join in a chat on food, taste, food culture and allergy

## Managing finances (weeks 11–13)

### Week 11

**Language function**

- Discussing about major banks in China
- The procedure of opening a bank account

**Grammar structure**

- Future tense 会
- Use of 和…一样
- 就…了

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 4: Talking about Bank Accounts

**Objectives**

- To be able to make basic inquiry about banking

### Week 12

**Language function**

- I want to open a bank account
- You can open a saving account with a debit card

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 等…后
- 为了…起见

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 4: Opening a Bank Account

**Objectives**

- To be able to open a bank account

### Week 13

**Language function**

- I have lost my bank card
- Providing personal info such as passport number, date of birth, address and telephone number

**Grammar structure**

- Result indicator 到
- Use of 并且

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 4: Reporting the loss of a Bank Card

**Objectives**

- To be able to pay a bill with a bank card; To report the loss of a bank card

> [!important] Coursework
> **Writing course work 20 minutes in class**
