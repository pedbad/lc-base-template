# Portuguese Basic 1 - Syllabus

*Last modified Tuesday, 27 October 2020*

**Prerequisites:** None. This course is suitable for students who want to learn the Portuguese Language and have no prior knowledge of the language.

**Target level:** Level A1 of the CEFR

**Approach:** The classes are **communicative** and consist of tasks oriented at helping students understand Portuguese in use and to acquire it by use. All four communicative skills will be developed – listening, speaking, reading and writing. The students will engage in interactive language activities, participating in group and pair work according to a syllabus based on systematic grammatical progression. Each class focuses on a topic that is related to the previous and following one; activities build on the students' abilities and on their previous knowledge. Grammar and vocabulary are practised and perfected in context. Students will also be offered guidance in self-study and be set optional homework tasks.

## Course main objectives

- A communicative approach is used. All four skills (speaking, reading, listening and writing) are practised, with main emphasis on speaking.
- To learn the basic grammatical structures and lexis, and build on them.
- To develop and/or strengthen a positive and confident attitude towards language learning.
- To encourage speaking and ease the transition to the next level (Basic 2).
- To promote cultural understanding.
- To be able to communicate in everyday situations.
- To be able to understand general information and to communicate in standard speech on a range of everyday issues.
- To be able to read printed and handwritten scripts on a limited number of everyday topics.
- To be able to write simple texts using descriptive language accurately.

## Syllabus: CULP Portuguese Basic 1

### Lesson 1: Introducing ourselves

***MUITO PRAZER!***

**Language Functions**

- Aims of the course – administrative announcements – safety routine
- Greetings
- Introductions: students and teacher
- Nationality, languages
- Studies/work
- Useful classroom vocabulary

**Grammar Structures**

- Interrogative pronouns
- Verb SER + nationality
- Verb FALAR regular conjugation
- Verb CHAMAR-SE: Como você se chama? Eu me chamo...
- Subject personal pronouns: *tu/ você* (informal registers)
- Possessives: *meu/minha*
- Demonstratives: *este/esta*
- numbers 0 - 20

**Work after class**

- **Reading task**: personagens famosos

### Lesson 2: Exchanging contact information

**QUAL É O SEU NÚMERO DE TELEFONE?**

**Language Functions**

- Introductions: formal / informal
- Nationality, language
- Marital status
- Alphabet – phonetics - spelling
- Pronunciation: basic rules
- Como se soletra? Nomes, sobrenomes, números de telefone e e-mail.

**Grammar Structures**

- Articles (*o, a, os, as*)
- Adjectives gender/number
- Prepositions (*de, em*)
- Verb SER + nationality
- Verb SER + preposition + city
- Verb SER + preposition + article + countries
- Verb ESTAR
- Verb MORAR – regular conjugation
- Subject personal pronouns: *tu/ você/o senhor/a senhora* (formal and informal registers)
- numbers 20 – 100

**Work after class**

- **Writing task**: write a text introducing yourself to your new Portuguese speaking pen-friend (name, age, nationality, languages, studies, marital status, place of origin, where do you live).

### Lesson 3: Talking about family and home

**MINHA FAMÍLIA**

> [!important] Assessment
> **On-line Homework – Moodle quiz:** general understanding (10% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Talking about the family
- Age
- Specifying origin and residence
- Occupation
- Socializing – talking about yourself and your family
- Survival vocabulary – months, birthdays

**Grammar Structures**

- Verb TER + age (*tenho 30 anos*)
- Cardinal numbers
- The alphabet
- Verbs SER and ESTAR in contrast (verb TO BE)
- Articles: definite/indefinite
- Nouns: gender and number

**Work after class**

- **Writing/speaking task:** bring a photo of your family (or a drawing in case you don't have a picture) to the next lesson together with a written text about your family (name, age, profession, relationship to you).
- **On-line Homework**
- **Moodle quiz:** general understanding (10% final mark)

### Lesson 4: Talking about daily routine

**ATIVIDADES DIÁRIAS**

**Language Functions**

- Talking about studies/work
- Telling the time
- Describing activities and timetables
- Duration
- Vocabulary: days of the week

**Grammar Structures**

- Present Indicative of regular verbs
- –AR (*trabalhar*)
- –ER (*comer*)
- –IR (*partir*)
- Stem-changing verbs (*fazer, dormir, ir*)
- Reflexive verbs (*levantar-se, pentear-se, vestir-se*).
- Adverbs of frequency: *sempre, nunca, às vezes, frequentemente*.

**Work after class**

- **Writing task:** write down a timetable with your weekly activities.

### Lesson 5: Talking about free time

**LAZER E TEMPO LIVRE**

**Language Functions**

- Talking about free time
- Describing likes and dislikes
- Specifying spare time activities
- Asking personal questions
- Finding activities in common
- Inviting a friend out

**Grammar Structures**

- verb GOSTAR DE (*gosto de ler*)
- verbs ADORAR/DETESTAR
- verbs FAZER (faço ginástica) e JOGAR (*jogo futebol*)

**Work after class**

- **Listening and Reading task**: o fim de semana

### Lesson 6: My home, my town and my country

**MINHA CASA, MINHA CIDADE, MEU PAÍS**

> [!important] Assessment
> **On-line Homework – Moodle quiz:** listening (10% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Describing your bedroom, your house and your town/city
- Talking about geographical location
- Weather
- Talking about relevant aspects of a town/city
- Renting a flat or a house

**Grammar Structures**

- Irregular Verbs in present tense
- Verbs: IR, PODER, TER, QUERER, HAVER, SER, ESTAR, FICAR
- Prepositions and adverbs
- There is/are
- Places in town
- Prepositions POR and PARA
- Making recommendations

**Work after class**

- **Listening task**: big cities – São Paulo
- **On-line Homework**
- **Moodle quiz:** listening (10% final mark)

### Lesson 7: Asking the way

**ONDE FICA ESTA RUA?**

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class Grammar Quiz 1** (15% final mark)
>
> **On-line Homework – Moodle:** Writing (10% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Asking about places of interest
- Finding your way around (on foot, by car)
- Describing a place, e.g. a town
- What can you do there? Is it interesting? Why?

**Grammar Structures**

- Verb FICAR
- Ordinal numbers
- Formal/informal imperative forms
- O futuro imediato IR (presente do indicativo) + verbo principal (infinitivo): vai ver, vai chegar...
- Descriptive adjectives (moderno, antigo)
- Prepositions and adverbs of place (em cima, embaixo, sobre, sob, ao lado, em frente a, à direita/esquerda)

**Work after class**

- **Reading task**: special places in Cambridge.
- **On-line Homework:**
- **Moodle**: Writing (10% final mark)

### Lesson 8: Having a meal at a restaurant

**VAMOS ALMOÇAR?**

**Language Functions**

- Ordering a snack
- Ordering a drink
- Asking for the bill
- Booking a table at a restaurant
- Choosing food items from a menu
- Expressing likes and dislikes
- Expressing wishes and desires
- Vocabulary: food, drinks, desserts.

**Grammar Structures**

- Futuro do pretérito do indicativo (condicional - cortesia)
- verb GOSTAR DE (expressing politeness): *Eu gostaria de pedir…*
- verbs GOSTAR DE, ADORAR, DETESTAR, ODIAR.

**Work after class**

- **Speaking task:** ordering a meal at a restaurant (expressing likes and dislikes, wishes and desires)

### Lesson 9: Shopping

**FAZENDO COMPRAS**

**Language Functions**

- Shopping for food
- Shopping for clothes
- Describing what we want to buy
- Enquiring about prices, weights, measures, sizes
- Expressing likes and dislikes
- Finding typical products
- Describing people: clothes and physical appearance
- Vocabulary: colours, units of weight/quantities/measurement.

**Grammar Structures**

- Expressing politeness:
  - Pretérito imperfeito do indicativo (cortesia): *Eu queria...*
  - Futuro do pretérito do indicativo (Condicional - cortesia): *Eu gostaria de provar..., Você poderia me mostrar...*
- Interrogative pronouns: *quanto, quanta, quantos, quantas.*
- Descriptive adjectives: *grande, médio, pequeno.*
- Indefinite pronouns: *muito, pouco.*

**Work after class**

- **Speaking task:** shopping for clothes (shop assistant and client).

### Lesson 10: Making travel arrangements

***COMO VOCÊ GOSTA DE VIAJAR?***

> [!important] Assessment
> **On-line Homework – Moodle quiz:** reading (10% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Comparing means of transport
- Buying tickets (coach, train, etc)
- Making plans
- Enquiring about tourist attractions

**Grammar Structures**

- Comparison: *mais/menos... que..., tanto/tão... quanto...*
- Interrogative pronouns: *onde, quando, como*.
- Demonstrative pronouns: *esse lugar..., aquela praia...*
- Future tense:
  - Futuro do indicativo: verb SER - *será*
  - Futuro imediato: verb IR (presente do indicativo) + verbo principal no infinitivo: *vai ser*

**Work after class**

- **Reading task:** touristic places in Portuguese speaking countries.
- **On-line Homework**
- **Moodle quiz:** reading (10% final mark)

### Lesson 11: Accommodation

**HOTÉIS E HOSPEDAGENS**

**Language Functions**

- Enquiring about accommodation
- Asking about facilities
- Booking a room
- Making a phone call
- Explaining needs
- Making a complaint
- Talking about the weather (seasons, months)
- Describing your state (hungry, cold)

**Grammar Structures**

- Verb FICAR, location
- Adverbs of place: *longe/perto*
- Verbs SER, ESTAR and impersonal HAVER /TER
- Comparatives/superlatives
- Gerund: verb VIAJAR – *viajando*. *Estou viajando há muitos dias*.

**Work after class**

- **Writing task:** leave written complaint to the manager about a problem you encountered in your hotel room.

### Lesson 12: Our next holiday + Grammar revision

**NOSSAS PRÓXIMAS FÉRIAS**

**Language Functions**

- Planning our next holiday
- Listening to the weather forecast
- Choosing the best itinerary
- Deciding about costs, budget
- Choosing the best means of transport
- Writing a holiday plan
- Expressing preferences

**Grammar Structures**

- Verb PREFERIR: *Eu prefiro viajar no inverno porque...*
- Future tense:
  - Futuro imediato: *Eu vou viajar amanhã.*
- Futuro do pretérito do indicativo (condicional - cortesia): *Eu gostaria de ir a Lisboa...*
- Grammar revision

**Work after class**

- **Grammar exercises**
- **Grammar revision**

### Lesson 13: Organizing an event

**UM EVENTO DE SUCESSO**

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class Grammar Quiz 2** (15% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Asking for and refusing permission
- Expressing rules
- Expressing obligations and needs
- Asking for help
- Offering help
- Refusing to help
- Organising an event
- Writing invitations/e-mail

**Grammar Structures**

- Verb DAR in informal spoken situations: dá pra/daria pra…
- Adjectives
- Presente do indicativo
- Futuro do presente do indicativo
- Futuro do pretérito do indicativo (condicional)
- Imperativo
- General overview of the past tenses (preparing for Basic 2)

**Work after class**

- **Listening task**: como organizar um evento de sucesso.

### Lesson 14: Revision

**REVISÃO**

**Language Functions; Grammar Structures**

- All of the above + practice for the oral presentations

**Work after class**

- **Writing task**: writing activity provided by teacher.

| Assessment | Weight |

and

**Oral presentation**: (30% of the final mark)

Duration: 2 minutes followed by 2 or 3 questions from teacher and colleagues.

As previously, all end of course oral presentations / role plays / debates take place in class time in the presence of classmates. Students may have notes as an aide-memoire, but reading a script will result in deduction of 10 points.

> [!important] To pass the course
> **Students will be required to achieve at least 50% of the available marks in the speaking component, and 50% overall in the assessments, and to have at least 80% attendance in order to pass the course.**
