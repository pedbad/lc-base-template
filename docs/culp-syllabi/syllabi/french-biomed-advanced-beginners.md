## Lesson 1: TALKING ABOUT YOURSELF

**Language Functions**

- Greetings
- Introductions
- Stating your marital status
- Stating your occupation
- Stating your nationality
- Saying where you come from and which languages you speak
- Saying your age and your birthday

**Grammar and structures**

- Present tense:
  - Regular –er verbs: parler, habiter, travailler, étudier
  - Irregular verbs: s'appeler, être, avoir, venir de
- Negation
- adjectives of nationality
- interrogatives: Comment? Où? D'où?
- Definite and Indefinite articles
- cardinals 1 – 100
- registers: tu/vous

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, First Contact**
- **ESSENTIALS, About me**
- **ESSENTIALS, Alphabet and Pronunciation:** + 6 Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, Numbers:**
  - 0-30 and 30-100 + Activity

**Grammar and structures**

- Possessive adjectives
- faire

**Work after class**

## Lesson 3: TALKING ABOUT WHERE YOU LIVE

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**: general understanding, 5% final mark

**Language Functions**

- Saying where you live and with whom
- Describing your house/flat/room
- Saying where your family lives

**Grammar and structures**

- habiter (to live)
- prepositions before countries and towns
- prepositional phrases: à la campagne, au premier étage etc
- definite articles
- more interrogatives

## Lesson 4: EATING IN & EATING OUT

**Language Functions**

- Talking about meals and mealtimes in France
- Comparing the above with meals in your own country
- Reserving a table
- Ordering and paying for a meal
- Querying the bill

**Grammar and structures**

- prendre (to take)
- manger (to eat)
- boire (to drink)
- réserver (to book)
- pouvoir (to be able) + infinitive

**Work after class**

- **FAYF, Restaurant**

## Lesson 5: TALKING ABOUT FREE TIME

**Language Functions**

- Stating preferences, likes and dislikes
- Talking about hobbies, and cultural interests

**Grammar and structures**

- present tense:
  - aimer (to like)
  - détester (to hate)
  - préférer (to prefer)
- faire (to do) + partitives
- jouer à (to play)
- jouer de
- aller à (to go to)

**Work after class**

  - Talking about leisure: Learn it first + activities
  - Chatting: Learn it first + All activities
- **ESSENTIALS, Days of the week**

## Lesson 11: DISCOVERING A FRENCH SPEAKING COUNTRY & MAKING HOTEL RESERVATIONS

**Language Functions**

- Saying where the country is
- Describing it: location, rivers, seas, oceans, mountain ranges, climate, population, other languages spoken etc
- Talking about a region or city
- Giving your opinion
- Talking about local traditions
- Making a hotel reservation and checking in
- Dealing with problems in the hotel and with minor ailments

**Grammar and structures**

- Où se trouve… ?
- Definite articles for countries and continents
- Prepositions before continents, countries, towns
- Il fait chaud / beau etc
- il y a / il n'y a pas de / d'
- avoir mal à

**Work after class**
