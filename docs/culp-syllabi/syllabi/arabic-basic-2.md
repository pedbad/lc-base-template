## Lesson 1: First Contact 1

**Language Functions**

- Arabic script and pronunciation: Review
- Introductions
- Greetings at different times of day

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Expressions of greeting
- 'How?' [كيف]
- Personal pronouns ('I'; 'you', m. + f. sing.; 'he', 'she')
- Possession ('my'; 'your', m. + f. sing.)
- Yes / no
- 'What?' [ما]

**Work after Class**

- Handwriting exercises (1)
- Hayya Bina
- **Arabic Essentials:** Arabic script and the exercises

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to recognise and pronounce Arabic consonants, vowels and basic orthographic signs
- To be able to recognise and pronounce basic nouns
- To greet and introduce oneself on a formal and informal occasion

## Lesson 2: First Contact 2

**Language Functions**

- Saying where one is from
- Nationality
- Saying where you live
- Reviewing numbers

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- 'أين'
- 'من' preposition
- The definite article
- Sun/moon letters
- *Nisba* adjective
- Gender
- Simple present tense
- Numbers 0-100

**Work after Class**

- Handwriting exercises (2)
- Grammar review and exercises
- **Arabic Essentials:** Arabic numbers

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to give basic information about oneself
