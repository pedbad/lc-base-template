# CULP Italian Basic 1

*Last updated 14/08/2023*

Link to grammar-based materials

1. How to say hello, present themselves, to tell their origin, age, titles and ask people relevant questions to get same information.
   - **Saluti (in Essentials)**
   - **Presentazioni**
   - Origini
   - Età
   - Titoli
   - Domande
2. To converse about work/study as well as family and languages spoken.
   - Studio
   - Lavoro
   - Famiglia
   - Lingue
   - L'orario
3. To be able to describe a dwelling, give/ask for address; ask/explain who you live with.
   - Abitazioni
   - Indirizzi
   - Dove e con chi vivi
   - Da quando?
   - I giorni della settimana
4. To talk about preferences, interests, sport, hobbies, foods, weekend activities.
   - Preferenze
   - Gusti
   - Interessi vari
   - Hobbies
   - Sport
   - Cibo
   - Fine settimana
   - Le stagioni
5. Shopping at the supermarket, corner shop and open market. Buying clothes and other items.
   - Fare la spesa
   - Fare le spese
6. How to place orders at the bar, ask people what they want to drink; to make a restaurant booking, read the menu, inquire about meals, place an order, ask for the bill.
   - Al bar
   - Al ristorante
7. To ask for directions, understand indications, traffic signs, buy a ticket for bus, call a taxi, explain where to go.
   - Direzioni
   - Segnali vari
   - Fare/comprare il biglietto
   - Taxi
