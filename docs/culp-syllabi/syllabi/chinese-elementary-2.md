## Looking for a flat (weeks 1–4)

### Week 1

**Language function**

- Talk about the kind of accommodation that you want to rent.
- Ask if there are any vacant places in nearby apartment complexes.
- Tell the Estate Agent you might renew the contract after a year.

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 的 to create a noun phrase
- Use of 另外, 别的 and 其他 in sentences
- Measure words 套, 栋, 幢
- Intention or prediction 会

**Work after class**

- Online materials Unit 5: Looking for a Flat – Flat hunting

**Objectives**

- Be able to enquire about accommodation;
- Be able to express a prediction or an intention.
- Be able to extract basic information from an Estate Agent's website.

### Week 2

**Language function**

- Describe what you think of a flat.
- Say you are worried that the flat is a bit small for you though.
- Ask to see more flats before making a decision.

**Grammar structure**

- 有 + a noun/verb
- Topic-comment structured sentence 是…的
- Use of 了点儿 adverbial phrase

**Work after class**

- Online materials Unit 5: Looking for a Flat – the visit

**Objectives**

- Be able to use topic-comment structured sentences to make a comment/statement.
- Be able to use adverbial phrases to express opinions.

### Week 3

**Language function**

- Talk about how many rooms there are in the flat you found.
- Say you would rather pay a little more for space and comfort.

**Grammar structure**

- Repetitive structure 多…多
- Double comparative structure 越…越

**Work after class**

- Online materials Unit 5: Looking for a Flat – the experience

**Objectives**

- Be able to recount your experiences of looking for accommodation, visiting flats, signing a contract, etc.

### Week 4

**Language function**

- This really is an enormous place and quite crowded too.
- It says on this board if you buy one pack of egg pies, you can get another free.

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 用…做的
- Verb + 着 to describe a status
- Set phrase: 各式各样, 人山人海, 物美价廉, 千真万确, 应有尽有

**Work after class**

- Online materials Unit 6: Shopping experience – Shopping in a supermarket

**Objectives**

- Be able to understand and use some set phrases.
- Be able to talk about your shopping routine.

## Shopping experience (weeks 5–7)

### Week 5

**Language function**

- I learnt a lot about silk.
- It has mostly become mechanized now.
- Fake silk smells like plastic.

**Grammar structure**

- Result indicator 到
- Multiple-pronunciation 了
- Use of 将

**Work after class**

- Online materials Unit 6: Shopping experience – Visiting a silk factory

**Objectives**

- Be able to recount a visit or an event.
- Be able to use a verb + 到 to indicate a result.

### Week 6

**Language function**

- Tell the shop assistant there might be a fault with your camera.
- Being told how to get a replacement for faulty goods.

**Grammar structure**

- Multiple-pronunciation 的
- 如果…就 structure
- Grammar particle 着

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 6: Shopping experience – Reporting faulty goods

**Objectives**

- Be able to report faulty goods
- Be able to tell whether the goods are still under their warranty period.

### Week 7

**Language function**

- Deciding on places to visit
- Planning for a holiday

**Grammar structure**

- Modal verb 得
- Passive voice marker 被
- Aspect markers 着, 过

**Work after class**

- Online materials Unit 7: Traveling in China – Planning a holiday

**Objectives**

- Be able to talk about preparations for a holiday.
- Be able to discuss places to visit.

> [!important] Coursework
> **Writing course work: 20 minutes in class**

## Traveling in China (weeks 8–10)

### Week 8

**Language function**

- It not only has beautiful scenery, but nice food too.
- We could go there first thing tomorrow morning.

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 不仅…还, 一…就, 从…起

**Work after class**

- Online materials Unit 7: Traveling in China – From Hangzhou to Beijing

**Objectives**

- Be able to talk about places that you have been.
- Be able to make further arrangements.

### Week 9

**Language function**

- Checking into a hotel.
- Asking advice on places to go.

**Grammar structure**

- Structure 又…又
- 离…近/远

**Work after class**

- Online materials Unit 7: Traveling in China – Seeking advice at the hotel lobby

**Objectives**

- Be able to follow a discussion when seeking for advice.
- Be able to read information about the Palace Museum and Temple of Heaven.

### Week 10

**Language function**

- Reporting sick to a colleague.
- Talking about food and allergies.

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 觉得…怎么样
- Sentences with 把
- Use of conditional structure 如果…的话

**Work after class**

- Online materials in Unit 3: Chatting Over a Meal

**Objectives**

- Be able to chat about food, taste, food culture and allergies.

## Seeking Medical Advice (weeks 11–13)

### Week 11

**Language function**

- Talking about symptoms of an illness.
- Discussing about what medication to take.

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 什么都, 对…有好处

**Work after class**

- Online materials Unit 8: Seeking medical advice – Reporting sick

**Objectives**

- Be able to talk about health matters.

### Week 12

**Language function**

- Where did you hurt yourself when you fell?
- The nurse is coming straightaway.

**Grammar structure**

- Adjectives + 一点
- 一边…一边

**Work after class**

- Online materials Unit 8: Seeking medical advice – Suffering an accident

**Objectives**

- Be able to show concern and accept help.

### Week 13

**Language function**

- I am always feeling tired these days.
- Many children are now obese.

**Grammar structure**

- 对…不好
- Use of 才

**Work after class**

- Online materials Unit 8: Seeking medical advice – Discussing health

**Objectives**

- Be able to talk about a healthy lifestyle.

> [!important] Coursework
> **Writing course work: 20 minutes in class**
