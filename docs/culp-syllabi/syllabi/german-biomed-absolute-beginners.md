## Lesson 1: Erster Kontakt (First Contact)

**Language Functions**

- Saying Hello!
- Introductions
- Saying your name
- Saying your age
- Stating your nationality

**Grammar structures**

- *dir, Ihnen*
- *sein* (present tense),
- *ich, mich, Sie, Ihnen*
- Nationalities
- *kommen aus*
- Cardinal numbers / Articles
- Interrogatives

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say hello, to present oneself, to be able to say where one comes from, to tell one's age and to be able to ask people the relevant questions to get the same information back.

## Lesson 2: Familie Sprachen und Herkunft (Languages and background; family)

**Language Functions**

- Saying which languages you speak
- Stating your occupation
- Dates (today's date; your birthday)
- Saying where and when you were born
- Talking about your family

**Grammar structures**

- Questions
- *haben* (present tense)
- Articles 1
- Articles 2
- Accusative
- to be + occupation
- *in* + dative
- *sprechen*
- Ordinal numbers

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say which language(s) one speaks, what one does for work, when is one's birthday, what is today's date
- To be able to talk about one's family.

## Lesson 3: Wohnen (Living situation)

> [!important] Assessment
> **Moodle quiz: general understanding** after Lesson 3 (5% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Saying where you live
- Describing your accommodation.
- Saying who you live with.

**Grammar structures**

- Prepositions + dative 1 + 2
- Articles 3 + 4
- Adjectives 1
- Compound nouns

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say where one lives (city / country) and to describe one's home. To be able to say who one lives with (human / animal).

## Lesson 4: Freizeitaktivitäten (Spare time)

**Language Functions**

- Saying which sports interest you
- Saying what your hobbies / interests are
- Saying what you do at the week-end

**Grammar structures**

- Present tense
- *gern(e)*
- Separable verbs
- Inversion

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say which sport one prefers and what one does in one's spare time/ what one is interested in and what one does at the weekend.

## Lesson 5: Im Café; Im Restaurant (In the café / restaurant)

**Language Functions**

- Getting a drink
- Ordering a Snack
- Reserving a table
- Ordering food and drink in a restaurant
- Paying the bill

**Grammar structures**

- *ich hätte gern*
- Modal verbs
- *nehmen, essen*
- Negation of nouns
- *Es gibt*
- *sein, haben* in the Simple past (*Imperfekt*)

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to order a drink / snack. To be able to make a restaurant booking, read the menu, ask questions about the food and drink, place an order, ask for the bill and ask for info regarding the bill.

## Lesson 7: In-class assessment 1 + Wegbeschreibung; Stadtbummel (Directions; going to town)

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class assessment 1**[^1]: Grammar-based quiz (15 mins.) (10% of final mark)
> **Writing** after Lesson 7, to be submitted before Lesson 8 (5% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Asking your way
- Finding your way on foot / by public transport / as a driver
- Looking for the shops
- Looking for presents.
- Seasonal context: Christmas market

**Grammar structures**

- *in* + accusative
- *für* + accusative
- *nach* + dative
- Imperative

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to ask for directions, to be able to read traffic signs, to call a taxi, to explain where to go and getting other relevant information.
- To know about Christmas Celebrations in German speaking countries.

## Lesson 8: Tagesablauf; Arbeit und Studium (Daily routine; work and study)

**Language Functions**

- Saying when your day begins and ends.
- Describing your daily routine when in Cambridge / when at home.
- Talking about your studies /your work/ your degree in more detail.
- Talking about your past.

**Grammar Structures**

- Adverbs (*morgens, abends*, etc)
- Genitive
- Prepositions + dative 3
- Inversion 2
- Conjunctions
- Prepositions (accusative / dative)
- Simple past

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's daily routine, one's work / study, the timetable, and to talk about one's past.

## Lesson 9: Über die Vergangenheit sprechen (Talking about the past)

**Language Functions**

- Saying where you went and what you did during the last holiday.
- Saying how things were when you were a child / younger
- German History

**Grammar Structures**

- Perfect tense
- *haben* / *sein* (simple past)
- *wenn, als*

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's past, about where one has been and what one has done.

## Lesson 10: Gesundheit; Essen einkaufen; kochen (Health; buying food)

> [!important] Assessment
> **Moodle quiz: Reading** after Lesson 10 (5% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Making an appointment at the doctor's
- Saying what hurts/ how you are feeling
- Understanding prescriptions
- Talking about healthy eating and lifestyle
- Buying food items on the market / in the Health-food store
- Specifying quantity

**Grammar Structures**

- Adjectives 2
- Modal verbs
- *seit*
- *zu*+ infinitive
- Imperative 2
- Conjunctions 2

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to make an appointment over the telephone, talk about one's health and fitness, describe ailments, understand instructions and discuss them.
- To be able to buy food items, enquire about quality and quantity.

## Lesson 11: Urlaubsplanung; Reisen und Reservierungen (Planning a holiday; travelling and booking)

**Language Functions**

- Planning a holiday
- Finding a hotel / campsite
- Buying a train/plane ticket
- Making a reservation

**Grammar Structures**

- Expression of future (time-word + present tense)
- Passive mode

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to speak of one's plans for the future, to be able to buy train/plane tickets, to reserve a seat, to book a hotel room / a camping site.
