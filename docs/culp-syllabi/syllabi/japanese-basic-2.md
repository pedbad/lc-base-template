## Michaelmas term

### Lesson 1: Meeting Others and Introducing Yourself

**Language Functions**

- Telling and asking personal information
- Asking about and describing one's hometown (or home country)

**Grammar Structures**

- Revision of grammar introduced in Basic 1 course (*Genki* 1 Chapter 1 to Chapter 4)
- Verbs:
  1. polite forms versus short forms
  2. conjugation patterns of short forms
- Interrogative for "what kind of ~?"

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 先生大学
- write your classmates' names in Katakana

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- introduce yourself on a business occasion
- obtain basic personal information of your conversation partner
- conduct a friendly conversation on a first meeting.

### Lesson 2: A Trip to Okinawa

**Language Functions**

- Talking about past events and own experiences

**Grammar Structures**

- **Conjugating Adjectives**
- Interrogative for "how"
- Conjunctions (then, since, therefore, in short)

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 友見行次

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- explain a series of past events in time order
- make a short presentation in a casual setting

### Lesson 3: Cooking and Japanese Food

**Language Functions**

- Making requests and orders
- read and write a simple instruction

**Grammar Structures**

- **The Te-form of verbs**
  1. The conjugation rules for Te forms
  2. Uses of Te-forms
     1) making requests
     2) stating that something is forbidden

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 食飲作
- Write a recipe of your favorite food.

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- learn how to make Te-forms
- state what you want someone to do and not to do
- read and write a simple instruction

### Lesson 4: E-Mail (business)

**Language Functions**

- Describing two activities in one sentence
- Explaining the reason or cause of a situation
- Asking someone to do a favor for you

**Grammar Structures**

- More uses of Te-forms
  - 3) Connecting verbs
- Particle for because "から"

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 京子今田
- Write an e-mail to your friend and ask him/her to do some small job for you.

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- write an e-mail (or letter) and make a request in polite manner.

### Lesson 5: Office Rules and House Rules

**Language Functions**

- Asking for permission
- Asking about likes and dislikes

**Grammar Structures**

- More uses of Te-forms
  - 4) giving and asking for permission
- Interrogatives (eg. what) and particles: use が not は after a question word (interrogative) ga (adjective) desuka? (eg. なにがすきですか。)

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 会社 仕事 員

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- state the rules at work
- state a daily routine at home
- ask about others' likes and dislikes

### Lesson 6: Alibi

**Language Functions**

- Talking about actions and events that are/were in progress

**Grammar Structures**

- More uses of Te-forms
  - 5) progressive construction
  - (Present progressive) Te form + います
  - (Past progressive) Te form ＋いました

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 午前後私言
- Write a postcard starting with "私は今〜".

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- explain what you are/were doing at a specific moment in time

### Lesson 7: Group Project

> [!important] Assessment
> **Writing course work/assessment (20 minutes in class)**

**Language Functions**

- Carrying out a group project for revising the language functions in context
- **Writing course work/assessment (20 minutes in class)**

**Grammar Structures**

- Revising grammar covered in this term

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 新聞入

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- make a short dialogue for revision
- listen to and understand the simple dialogues

## Lent term

### Lesson 8: Family and Japanese New Years

**Language Functions**

- Talking about continuous state
- Using a casual speech form (short forms)

**Grammar Structures**

- Revisions (L1 ~L7)
- Verbs that are commonly used in the ~ています（ている）framework (to know, to live, to work for etc.)
- **Short forms of verbs**
  1. conjugation patterns
  2. uses of short forms
     1) casual conversations

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 父母帰休

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- talk about your family members (where they live; what they do etc.)
- learn a new paradigm of verb conjugation (short form)

### Lesson 9: Blind Date

**Language Functions**

- Describing features of people and items
- Describing what someone is wearing

**Grammar Structures**

- The sentence construction of 「A は B が(adjective)です。」= as for A, B is (adjective).
- Four Japanese verbs for "wear": きます/はきます/かぶります/かけます

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 出口 白 黒 赤 青

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- describe different features of people and items
- describe what someone is/was wearing

### Lesson 10: Cover Letter

**Language Functions**

- Describing features of people and items by using two or more adjectives
- Writing longer sentences by combining two elements

**Grammar Structures**

- The Te forms of adjectives and the copula for joining sentences
- More Uses of short forms
  - 2) nominalizing verbs (short forms + の)
- Nominalizer "no" (= "one" "the one")

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 高校読書
- Write a cover letter.

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- state distinctive features of your work (and/or yourself)

### Lesson 11: World Heritage Sites in Japan

**Language Functions**

- Describing the purpose of movement

**Grammar Structures**

- The purpose of movement (Verb stem or noun)に いく/くる/かえる= (person) go/come/return in order to do (purpose).
- More uses of short forms (negative)
  - 3) requesting that someone refrain from doing something (negative short form)

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 外国雨
- write a trip plan to one of the world heritage sites in Japan

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- explain plans to a group of people
- ask and answer the questions regarding the plan
- tell people what is allowed and what is not allowed

### Lesson 12: Apologies

**Language Functions**

- Explaining events in the past
- Explaining a reason

**Grammar Structures**

- Past tense short forms of verbs, adjectives, and the copula
- More uses of short forms
  - 4) Explaining a reason (short form + のです)

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 電車毎語文

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- explain why you have to miss the class next week
- explain why you missed the class last week

### Lesson 13: Interview People

> [!important] Assessment
> **Writing course work/assessment (20 minutes in class)**

**Language Functions**

- Quoting somebody's words
- Making a guess
- **Writing course work/assessment (20 minutes in class)**

**Grammar Structures**

- More uses of short forms
  - 5) quotation
- Interrogatives (eg. what) and particles
  1. an interrogative + か = *some*…(eg. something)
  2. an interrogative + も = *any*… (eg. anything) in affirmative sentences / *no*… (eg. nothing) in negative sentences

**Work after Class**

- Kanji: 何思

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- interview somebody and report the result
