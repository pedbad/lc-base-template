## Lesson 1: Erster Kontakt (First contact)

**Language functions**

- Saying Hello!
- Introductions
- Saying your name
- Saying your age
- Stating your nationality

**Grammar structures**

- *dir, Ihnen*
- *ich, mich, Sie, Ihnen*
- Present tense of *sein*
- Conjugation of regular verbs *(wohnen, heißen)*
- Nationalities
- *kommen aus*
- Cardinal numbers
- Definite articles Nominative
- Questions/interrogatives

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say hello, to present oneself, to be able to say where one comes from, to tell one's age and to be able to ask people the relevant questions to get the same information back.

## Lesson 2: Familie, Sprachen und Herkunft (Languages and background, family)

**Language functions**

- Saying which languages you speak
- Stating your occupation
- Dates (today's date, your birthday)
- Saying where and when you were born
- Talking about your family

**Grammar structures**

- More questions/interrogatives
- Present tense of *haben*
- Nominative + Accusative of indefinite and possessive articles
- Adjectives *(groß, klein, älter, jünger* etc.*)* in the Accusative
- to be + occupation
- *in* + Dative
- *sprechen*
- Ordinal numbers
- Plurals of nouns

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say which language(s) one speaks, what one does for work, what the date is and when it is one's birthday.
- To be able to talk about one's family.

## Lesson 3: Wohnen (Living situation)

> [!important] Assessment
> **MOODLE QUIZ 1**: after Lesson 3, General understanding (5% of final mark)

**Language functions**

- Saying where you live
- Describing your accommodation
- Saying who you live with

**Grammar structures**

- Prepositions/Dative + indefinite and definite articles
- Accusative + indefinite articles + adjectives
- Compound nouns

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say where one lives (city / country) and to describe one's home (type of house / flat + rooms). To be able to say who one lives with (human / animal).
- **MOODLE QUIZ 1**

## Lesson 4: Freizeitaktivitäten (Spare time)

**Language functions**

- Saying which sports interest you
- Saying what your hobbies / interests are
- Saying what you do at the week-end

**Grammar structures**

- Present tense of more irregular verbs
- *(nicht) gerne*
- Coordinate conjunctions *(oder, aber)*
- Separable verbs
- Inversions

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say which sport one prefers and what one does in one's spare time / at the weekend.

## Lesson 5: Im Café, im Restaurant (In the café / restaurant)

**Language functions**

- Reserving a table
- Ordering food and drink in a café / restaurant
- Paying the bill

**Grammar structures**

- *ich hätte gern*
- *ich möchte*
- *nehmen, essen*
- *es gibt*
- Accusative + definite articles + adjectives
- Negation of nouns

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to make a restaurant booking, read the menu, ask questions about the food and drink, place an order and ask for the bill.

## Lesson 7: In-class assessment 1 + Wegbeschreibung, Stadtbummel (Directions, going to town)

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class assessment 1**[^1]: Grammar-based quiz (15 mins.) (10% of final mark)
> **Writing Homework, to be submitted before L. 8.** (5% of final mark)

**Language functions**

- Asking your way
- Finding your way on foot / by public transport / as a driver
- Looking for the shops
- Looking for presents
- Seasonal context: Christmas market

**Grammar structures**

- Prepositions/Accusative
- Prepositions/Dative + articles + adjectives
- Imperative

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to ask for directions, read traffic signs, call a taxi and explain where to go.
- To be able to shop for presents.
- To know about Christmas Celebrations in German speaking countries.
- **Writing Homework, to be submitted before L. 8.**

## Lesson 8: Tagesablauf, Arbeit und Studium (Daily routine, work and study)

**Language functions**

- Saying when your day begins and ends
- Describing your daily routine when in Cambridge / when at home
- Talking about your studies / your work

**Grammar structures**

- Adverbs (*morgens, abends*, etc.)
- The clock
- Inversions reviewed

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's daily routine and one's work / study.

## Lesson 9: Über die Vergangenheit sprechen (Talking about the past)

**Language functions**

- Saying where you went and what you did during the last holiday
- Discussing a German historical / cultural topic of general interest

**Grammar structures**

- Perfect tense
- *haben* / *sein* in the simple past
- *als*

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's past, about where one has been and what one has done.

## Lesson 10: Gesundheit, Essen einkaufen (Health, buying food)

> [!important] Assessment
> **MOODLE QUIZ 3**: after Lesson 10, Reading (5% of final mark)

**Language functions**

- Naming the parts of your body
- Saying what hurts / how you are feeling
- Talking about healthy eating and lifestyle
- Buying food items on the market / in the health-food store
- Specifying quantity

**Grammar structures**

- Modal verbs
- *seit*
- *zu* + Infinitive

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's health and fitness and to describe ailments.
- To be able to buy food items and to enquire about quality and quantity.
- **MOODLE QUIZ 3**

## Lesson 11: Urlaubsplanung; Reisen und Reservierungen (Planning a holiday, travelling and booking)

**Language functions**

- Planning a holiday
- Buying a train / plane ticket
- Finding a hotel / campsite
- Making a reservation

**Grammar structures**

- Expression of future (time-word + present tense)

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to speak of one's plans for the future. To be able to buy train / plane tickets, to reserve a seat and to book a hotel room / a camping site.
