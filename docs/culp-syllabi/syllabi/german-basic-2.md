## Lektion 1: Erster Kontakt; Sprachen und Herkunft (First contact; languages and origins)

**Language Functions**

- Greetings (formal + informal)
- Asking + answering questions about
  - nationality/country of origin
  - place of residence
  - languages we speak
  - our professions/fields of study

**Grammar Structures**

- Personal pronouns and possessive articles (Nominative)
- *female 'in'-endings* nationalities
- Conjugation of reg. verbs + irreg. verb *sprechen*
- Interrogatives/question words
- *aus* + country
- conjunctions *denn* + *weil*

**Work after class**

- Homework as set by teacher
- Keep language learning log (due L 3)

## Lektion 2: Berufe; Familie (Professions; family)

**Language Functions**

- Talking about occupations in more detail
  - where we work/study, and
  - since when or for how long we have been doing it
- Saying when and where we were born
- Talking about our families

**Grammar Structures**

- Ordinal numbers
- *an* + Dative
- *seit*
- to be + occupation *(male + female)*
- Personal pronouns and possessive articles (Accusative + Dative)
- Adjective endings following the indef. article (Nom., Acc. + Dat.)
- Negation of nouns

**Work after class**

- Homework as set by teacher
