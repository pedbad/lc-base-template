## Week 1: Exchanging Greetings and Introductions

**Language Function**

1. Swahili greetings
2. Stating your name, nationality, residence, language you speak, your work or place of study/work

**Grammar**

1. Present simple tense
2. Subject pronouns
3. Affirmative and negative forms

**Homework**

1. Learn how to count 1 to 1000 in Swahili
2. Write a short paragraph introducing a family member or a friend

**Learning Objectives**

1. Ability to greet and introduce oneself and others in Swahili
2. Use basic vocabulary and grammar appropriately and effectively

## Week 2: Introductions - family members and friends.

**Language Function**

1. Introduce family members and friends.
2. Give personal details, i.e., phone number, email, address
3. Talk about nationalities, professions, and languages

**Grammar**

1. Demonstrative pronouns
2. Possessive Pronouns
3. Verb to be (Present form)
4. Question words, i.e., *nani? nini? wapi?*
5. Introducing Noun classes 1 and 2 (Animate class)

**Homework**

- Draw a sketch or use a photo of your family and introduce the members in Swahili.

**Learning Objectives**

1. Ability to introduce family and others, give personal information and use numbers
2. Ability to talk about nationalities, languages, and professions
3. Use correct grammar and vocabulary
4. Ability to use grammar appropriately.

## Week 3: Talking about Family and using numbers, days, months, and years.

> [!important] Assessment
> **Moodle Quiz 1 online: General understanding (after lesson)**

**Language Function**

1. Describing family members, stating dates of birth, age, and numbers
2. Use numbers, days, months, and years appropriately

**Grammar**

1. Demonstrative pronouns and Possessive pronouns (cont...)
2. Question words, i.e., *Una miaka mingapi?*
3. Verb to have (present form)
4. Present and Perfective forms *-li-* and *-me-*
5. Noun classes 1 and 2 (Animate class)

**Homework**

1. Write a paragraph about "My family."
2. Learn about telling the time in Swahili
3. **Assessment and review:**
   - **Moodle Quiz 1 online: General understanding (after lesson)**

**Learning Objectives**

1. Ability to talk about family members and express dates and numbers.
2. Ability to conjugate numbers using classes 1 and 2 agreements
3. Ability to use grammar appropriately.

## Week 4: Telling the time and talking about daily activities

**Language Function**

1. Ask and tell time in Swahili
2. Talk about simple activities you do every day

**Grammar**

1. Habitual tense "*hu*"
2. Question words, i.e., *ngapi?*

**Homework**

- Write a list of activities and their time in a day.

**Learning Objectives**

- Ability to tell time effectively and appropriately

## Week 5: My typical day

**Language Function**

1. Talk about routine activities and their timings.
2. Talk about your work or study schedule

**Grammar**

1. Habitual tense "*hu*"
2. Question words, i.e., *ngapi?*

**Homework**

1. Write a paragraph (100 words) about "My Typical Day."
2. Record a journal of your daily activities in Swahili

**Learning Objectives**

1. Ability to tell time effectively and appropriately
2. Ability to describe your typical day and activities you do daily.

## Week 6: Expressing Likes and Dislikes - Hobbies and Interests

> [!important] Assessment
> **Moodle Quiz 2 online (reading comprehension)**

**Language Function**

1. Talk about preferences - the likes and dislikes
2. Talk about leisure activities – hobbies and interests

**Grammar**

1. Present tense + infinitive *-ku-*.
2. Using adverbials – i.e., always, usually, often
3. The use of preference words, "*ninapenda*" and "*ninapendelea*"

**Homework**

- Write a text/paragraph about "My Hobbies and Interests."
- **Moodle Quiz 2 online (reading comprehension)**

**Learning Objectives**

1. Ability to discuss daily routines, ask for and tell time
2. Ability to use present and infinitive -ku- with adverbial phrases and time expressions correctly

## Week 7: Describing people (characters and personalities)

> [!important] Assessment
> **Grammar Test 1 (In-class)**

**Language Function**

1. Describe what people look like and their behaviour/characters
2. Talk about what people look like, habits or behaviour in general

**Grammar**

1. Using adjectives with classes 1 and 2 agreements
2. List key character traits of the people, i.e., appearance, hair, eyes, height, size
3. Incorporating verbs to be and have in describing people

**Homework**

1. Read a text and draw the characters from the description
2. Describe the picture of someone/ describe someone in the picture given.
3. **Grammar Test 1 (In-class)**

**Learning Objectives**

1. Ability to describe people appropriately based on their appearance and character traits
2. Use adjectives verbs to be and have appropriately.

## Week 8: Describing places/ location

**Language Function**

- Talk about a public place or location or home.

**Grammar**

1. Introducing the locative class 16-17-18 (PO-KO-MO) (Demonstratives and Possessive forms)
2. Prepositions and adverbials

**Homework**

- Write a paragraph describing a picture of a location given.

**Learning Objectives**

- Ability to describe a place or a location using correct grammar and structure effectively and appropriately.

## Week 9: Talking about future plans

**Language Function**

- Talk about plans and aspirations – holidays, breaks, etc.

**Grammar**

1. Introducing future tense -ta- (Affirmative and negative forms)

**Learning Objectives**

- Proficiency in describing people and places using appropriate adjectives and prepositions of place

## Week 10: Describing objects, i.e., electronics, apps, and everyday gadgets.

> [!important] Assessment
> **Moodle quiz 3 (listening comprehension)**

**Language Function**

1. Talk about the objects, apps, and gadgets you find helpful for your day-to-day life
2. Describe your favourite item, i.e., mobile phone, tablet, or laptop.

**Grammar**

1. Introducing noun classes 5-6, 7 – 8 and 9-10.
2. Using adjectives and their respective class agreements in 5-6, 7-8 and 9-10.

**Homework**

- Write an essay describing **the three APPS and gadgets** you find most useful in your daily life as a student.
- **Moodle quiz 3 (listening comprehension)**

**Learning Objectives**

1. Ability to describe objects appropriately
2. Ability to use noun classes 7 – 8 and 9-10.
3. Ability to use adjectives and their respective class 7-8 and 9-10 agreements.

## Week 11: Describing colours and clothing

**Language Function**

- Sharing interests, hobbies, and pastimes

**Grammar**

1. Noun classes 3-4, 5-6, 7-8 and 9-10
2. Adjectives and agreement in use

**Homework**

- Write about the video clip you see. "The Sapeurs" of Congo in a 2014 Guinness ad.

**Learning Objectives**

1. Ability to name and talk about clothing and colours in Swahili
2. Ability to use noun class agreement appropriately.

## Week 12: Shopping: Clothing

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class Writing task**

**Language Function**

1. Shopping for clothing, discussing colours, fashion, and fabrics
2. Asking for the price and haggling

**Grammar**

1. Noun classes 3-4, 5-6, 7-8 and 9-10
2. Adjectives and agreement in use
3. Imperative and subjunctive forms

**Homework**

- Create a shopping list and simulate a clothes shopping trip
- **Writing task**

**Learning Objectives**

Ability to:

1. Shop for clothing, discussing colours, fashion, and fabrics
2. Ask for the price and haggle
3. to use noun class agreement appropriately.

## Week 13: Foodstuff and ordering a meal and a drink.

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class Grammar Test (2)**

**Language Function**

1. Shop for various foodstuffs, veggies and fruits
2. Order a meal and drink

**Grammar**

1. Making polite requests
2. Imperative and Subjunctive forms

**Homework**

- Role-play a restaurant scenario
- **In-class Grammar Test (2)**

**Learning Objectives**

- Ability to order food and drinks at a restaurant or hotel
- Use appropriate ordering phrases effectively

## Week 14: Parts of the Body and Common Illnesses

**Language Function**

1. Discussing parts of the body and common health issues
2. Express and describe illness, symptoms

**Grammar**

1. Name Body parts vocabulary; Common illnesses
2. Extensive review of Swahili Noun classes

**Homework**

- Describe common illnesses and their symptoms

**Learning Objectives**

1. Ability to name parts of the body, and illnesses
2. Ability to express and describe illness and symptoms.
