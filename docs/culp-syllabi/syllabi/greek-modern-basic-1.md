## Lesson 1: INTRODUCTIONS

**Language Functions**

- Greek phonology and spelling
- Greetings
- Basic introductions

**Grammar and structures**

- alphabet and pronunciation
- accent
- to be called (*με λένε…*)
- weak personal pronouns (acc)
- to be (*είμαι*)
- personal pronoun (nom)
- definite article
- registers: tu/vous

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, Alphabet and Pronunciation:** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, the verb *είμαι* and personal pronouns:** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, First Contact: Greetings and introductions** Activities

## Lesson 2: TALKING ABOUT YOURSELF (1)

**Language Functions**

- Origin & residence
- Countries
- Stating your nationality
- Saying where you come from
- Saying where you live

**Grammar and structures**

- nouns (nom, acc)
- adjectives of nationality
- final *-ν*
- 1st conjugation verbs: to have (*έχω*)
- to live (*μένω*)
- personal pronouns (nom, acc)
- prepositions before countries/ towns (*σε, από*)
- prepositional phrases
- interrogative clauses

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, About me: Residence, origin and nationality** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, Conj A verbs and Nouns:** Activities

## Lesson 3: TALKING ABOUT YOURSELF (2)

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**: general understanding, 5% final mark

**Language Functions**

- Saying which languages you speak
- Professions & places of work
- Stating your occupation
- Introducing hobbies

**Grammar and structures**

- adverbs of manner
- *I like (μου αρέσει)*
- weak personal pronouns (gen)
- 2nd conjugation verbs
- cardinals 1 – 10

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, About me: Professions and hobbies** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, Conj B verbs:** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, Numbers:** Activity
- **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**

## Lesson 4: FAMILIES

**Language Functions**

- Families
- Stating your marital status
- Saying where your family lives

**Grammar and structures**

- indefinite article
- possessive pronoun
- interrogative pronouns (*ποιος, ποια, ποιο*)
- demonstrative pronouns (*αυτός -ή, -ό, εκείνος -η -ο*)
- plural number of nouns
- cardinals 11 – 100

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, About me: Family and pronouns** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, Singular and plural number:** Activity
- **ESSENTIALS, Numbers:** Activity

## Lesson 5: TALKING ABOUT YOUR STUDIES & HOBBIES

**Language Functions**

- Saying what you are studying/where you work
- Talking about hobbies and interests
- Stating preferences, likes and dislikes

**Grammar and structures**

- more present tense verbs
- contracted verbs (*ακούω, πάω, λέω*)
- leisure activities
- days of the week
- referencing time: the use of the acc

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, About me: Studies** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, Days of the week** Activity
