## Lesson 1: TALKING ABOUT YOURSELF

**Functions**

- Revision of all vocabulary of the previous stage
- Introductions
- Stating your nationality and country
- Saying your name, age, your place and date of birth
- Describing what your occupation is and which languages you speak.

**Grammar**

- Revision of all grammar covered so far
- Alphabet, diphthongs, genders, cases, Articles (definite and indefinite).
- Nouns (masculine -ας, -ης, -ος,-ους, feminine -α, -η, -ος)
- Verbs present tense Α1, Β1.
- Personal pronouns, Possessive pronouns.
- Interrogative pronouns τι, ποιος, πόσος, τίνος

**Study material**

- ESSENTIALS
- Alphabet, pronunciation and reading aloud
- First contact
- Numbers
- Dates
- Nationalities and languages

**Objectives**

- To be able to introduce oneself and others, to speak about your background and origin, your nationality, occupation, languages spoken. To ask people relevant questions to obtain the same information.
- To be able to say and ask what the date is.

## Lesson 2: DAILY ROUTINE

*Living in the city or in the country*

> [!important] Written homework
> **Written homework 1**[^3]

**Functions**

- Describing your daily routine when in town or at home.
- Saying where you live, who you live with.
- Describing your accommodation.
- Talking about friends and family.
- Expressing your likes and dislikes; where you would prefer to live: city or country and why.

**Grammar**

- Nouns (masculine -ας, -ης, -ος,-ους, -ες, feminine -α, -η, -ος/-οι neuter -ο, -ι, -μα/ματα). Indefinite pronouns κανείς.
- Nouns with a change of gender in the plural (χρόνος, χρόνοι/χρόνια)
- Indeclinable nouns
- Verbs present tense Α2, Β2, Γ1. Cardinals 1 -100
- Diminutives –ακι

**Study material**

- ESSENTIALS
- Describing your accommodation and where you would prefer to live in the city or in the country.

**Objectives**

- To be able to talk about your daily routine, your living situation, your family and friends.
- To be able to express one's likes and dislikes, the pros and the cons of city vs. country life.
- **Written homework 1** °

## Lesson 3: SPARE TIME

*EATING OUT OR COOKING AT HOME*

**Functions**

- Saying which sports interest you
- Saying what your hobbies and interests are
- Saying what you do at the week-end
- Ordering food and drink in a restaurant
- Paying the bill
- Buying food
- Talking about ingredients and recipes

**Grammar**

- Adjectives in –ος, -η, -ο/ ος, -ια, -ο/-ος, -η/-α, -ο
- Cardinals 1 -1000000
- Personal pronouns, Possessive pronouns. Interrogative pronouns τι, ποιος, πόσος, τίνος Indefinite pronouns μερικοί, κάθε, κάτι, τίποτα, Demonstrative pronouns αυτός, εκείνος, τέτοιος, Relative pronoun που
- Verbs present tense Α1, Α2, Β1, Β2, Γ1. (transitive/intransitive)
- Some adverbs

**Study material**

- READING TEXTS
- Conversation skills at the market

**Objectives**

- To be able to talk about your own interests about sport, hobbies and weekend activities.
- To be able to make a reservation in a restaurant, read the menu, ask about the dishes, place an order, talk about food, ask for the bill and comment about the bill.
- To be able to talk about cooking exchanging recipes

## Lesson 4: HEALTH AND FITNESS

**Functions**

- Asking about a person's health
- Describing habits
- Making an appointment at the doctor's
- Saying how you are feeling
- Understanding prescriptions
- Talking about healthy eating and lifestyle, as well as sports
- Buying food at the market or in a health-food store.

**Grammar**

- Passive voice
- Deponent verbs (έρχομαι, κοιμάμαι) Passive voice, present tense
- Adverbs in -α
- χρονικά (ακόμη, συχνά, συνήθως, ύστερα, πριν, προχτές, απόψε, μεθαύριο, πρόπερυσι, αμέσως, πάντα, έπειτα, μετά)

**Study material**

- ESSENTIALS
- To be able to describe everyday actions
- To be able to talk about health and lifestyle

**Objectives**

- To be able to provide details in conversation about healthy lifestyle, sports, health foods
- To be able to make an appointment to see a doctor, to talk about your health, understand instructions and discuss them.

## Lesson 7: SIGHTSEEING SHOPPING IN TOWN HAVING FUN

*(Seasonal Context)*

> [!important] Assessment
> **Assessment 1**[^1]

**Functions**

- Sports, music halls, night clubs, theatres, cinema
- Visiting sights and museums
- Describing a place of interest
- Looking for the shops and buying goods.
- Looking for presents.
- (Seasonal Context)

**Grammar**

- Adjectives in –ος, -η, -ο/ ος, -ια, -ι/-ος, -η/-α, -ο degrees: comparative, superlative, absolute superlative (in more than one word)
- More diminutives
- Adverbs
- τοπικά (εδώ, εκεί, πάνω, κάτω, μέσα, έξω, μπροστά, πίσω, δεξιά, αριστερά, κοντά, ίσια, μακριά, δίπλα, γύρω)

**Study material**

- ESSENTIALS
- To be able to describe enjoyment and free time out as well as being in a department store

**Objectives**

- To be able to give further details about how one has fun out and about
- To be able to describe places and objects of interest.
- To be able to buy food and clothing items, inquire about prices, size, quantity and get other relevant information on products.
- (To know about Christmas Celebrations in Greece)

**Grammar**

**Study material**

- ESSENTIALS
- Specific vocabulary for IT

**Objectives**

- To be able to answer the phone, hold a telephone conversation, leave messages, understand voicemail, make an appointment.
- To be able to write SMS, emails and some formal letters.
