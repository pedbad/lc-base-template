## Lesson 1: Introducing ourselves

Speaking of our background and our current studies

**Language Functions**

- Course introduction.
- Resources, aims, assessment and teacher.
- Introducing oneself and each other to the group
- Talking about the family, age, country of origin and residence
- *Me llamo...*
- *Soy + nacionalidad(es)*
- *Hablo...*
- *Tengo ... años.*
- *Vivo en...*

**Grammar structures**

- Definite and indefinite articles
- Gender of nouns
- Subject personal pronouns
- Interrogative pronouns
- Regular and irregular verbs in the present
- *tú* and *usted* (formal and informal registers)
- Verbs SER and ESTAR in contrast

**Work after class**

- **Moodle**
- **Homework set by teacher**

## Lesson 10: Visiting the doctor's; Asking for help

***VAMOS AL MÉDICO***

***TENEMOS PROBLEMAS***

> [!important] Assessment
> **LISTENING COMPREHENSION TEST (Moodle)**: after Lesson 10, closed before Lesson 11, 10% of the final mark

**Language Functions**

- Saying what the problem is
- Asking for and giving advice
- Explaining what happened
- Explaining what we do to keep fit

**Grammar structures**

- Verbs *SENTIRSE* and *ENCONTRARSE*
- *DOLER* and other similar verbs
- The formal imperative (*tómese un analgésico*)
- Verbs *SER* and *ESTAR* contrasted (*soy/estoy gordo/a*)
- The present perfect (*hemos chocado, me he dado un golpe*)
- Pronominal use of verbs (*Me caí… me rompí la pierna*)
- The present subjunctive: giving advice (*te aconsejo que hagas deporte*)

**Work after class**

- **Moodle**
- **Homework set by teacher**
- **LISTENING COMPREHENSION TEST (Moodle)**
