## Lesson 1: Myself, Family and Friends

**Language functions**

- Talking about your family, yourself and your friends
- A letter of introduction
- Relationship and making new friends

**Grammar structures and expressions**

- Revision of possessive pronouns
- The dual
- Present tense

**Work after class**

- Grammar exercise
- Review listening
- Write about your house style, describe its furniture

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to describe your house, its rooms and furniture
- To be able to answer questions of why using because, because of and for

## Lesson 2: Life at Home

**Language functions**

- Talking about houses and their types
- Room types/furniture
- House chores
- Region

**Grammar structures and expressions**

- Present tense contd
- Adjective and noun agreement
- Future tense

**Work after class**

- Grammar exercises
- Review listening
- Write a letter of introduction to a friend

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to write short notices and text messages
- To be able to write a letter of introduction

## Lesson 3: Free time (evening, weekends, meeting people)

**Language functions**

- Things you do during free time/holidays
- Understanding signs and notices
- Receiving a guest

**Grammar structures and expressions**

- Review Al-masdar/Semantic implications of the verb forms
- Past tense

**Work after class**

- Grammar exercises
- Write short notices
- Offering services and asking about things

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to talk and write about your free time, clubs and holidays
- To be able to read signs and notices

## Lesson 4: Special occasions

**Language functions**

- Birthdays
- Invitation
- Wedding party
- A telephone invitation

**Grammar structures and expressions**

- The nominal sentence
- Fronted predicate
- Negating nominal sentence

**Work after class**

- Grammar exercises
- Write an invitation
- Listening review

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to write a party invitation
- To be able to describe a party you had attended

## Lesson 5: Accidents, Injuries and Common Ailments

**Language functions**

- Minor ailments and accidents/Visits to the doctor and dentist
- Health issues: drugs and smoking/alcohol

**Grammar structures and expressions**

- Past tense negation
- كان verb + negation

**Work after class**

- Grammar exercises
- Make an appointment
- Listening review

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to describe the symptoms of an illness
- To be able to request an appointment

## Lesson 6: School Life and Routine

- Subject-verb agreement, verbal sentence

**Work after class**

- Grammar exercises
- Reading comprehension

- **Compulsory quiz on Moodle**

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to talk about your education and study routine
- To be able to read and understand announcements

## Assessment

ASSESSMENT FOR ELEMENTARY 2 ARABIC IS AS FOLLOWS:

**2 pieces of homework:**

| Assessment | Skill | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| After Lesson 6 Moodle quiz | Reading | 15% final mark |
| During Lesson 11 Moodle quiz | Writing | 20% final mark |
| After Lesson 12 Moodle quiz | Listening | 15% final mark |
| In-class grammar quiz 1, Lesson 7 | Grammar | 10% of the final mark |
| In-class grammar quiz 2, Lesson 13 | Grammar | 10% of the final mark |

**Oral (worth 30% of total), in pairs**

Duration 6-8 minutes followed by 2/3 questions from class and teacher.[^1]

> [!important] Attendance
> **80% attendance requirement to pass the course.**

[^1]: Students should enact a role-play in front of the class based on the content of the lessons. The list of units can function as a guideline. Students prepare role-plays outside lesson time and may bring notes for the oral presentation as an aide-memoire, BUT reading a script will result in deduction of 10 points.
