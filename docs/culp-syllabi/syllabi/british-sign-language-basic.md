## Lesson 1: Introducing yourself and starting a conversation

**Language Functions**

- Welcome/Introductions
- Using your hands to create the letters of the alphabet.
- Introducing yourself in British Sign Language (BSL)
- Modes of transport signs

**Grammar and Structures**

- Introducing the BSL alphabet – A-Z
- Fingerspelling your name
- 'Hello, name me…..', 'name you what?' etc
- 'You arrive here how?' with question forms using facial expressions.

**Work after class**

- Practising the alphabet.
- Practising spelling your name/introducing yourself etc.
- Practising modes of transport

## Lesson 2: Asking questions; Using numbers; Describing people

**Language Functions**

- Recap Lesson 1.
- Understanding importance of facial expressions to convey questions.
- Fingerspelling Numbers
- Describing people

**Grammar and Structures**

- 'Arrive here how?'
- Using question signs. 'how, why, what, where, when'
- Demonstrating numbers up to 100
- Using handshapes to describe people

**Work after class**

- Practising what you learnt today
- Practising facial expression on questions
- Practising fingerspelling Numbers
- Practising describing people & types of property

## Lesson 3: Homes; Describing where you live

**Language Functions**

- Recap Lesson 2
- **Describing types of property that people live in**
- Saying which country you live in. Asking someone where they are from.
- Place names – using fingerspellings/signs
- Saying where you live, asking someone where they live

**Grammar and Structures**

- **Using handshapes to describe homes**
- 'Countries name what?' 'Which country you from?'
- Fingerspelling place names
- 'Live where you?'

**Work after class**

- Practising what you learnt today.
- Homework:-
  - Practising different signs for Countries and place names

## Lesson 4: Understanding Deafness; Different types of handshapes; Demonstrating the weather; Giving directions

**Language Functions**

- Recap Lesson 3
- Deaf awareness & culture
- Understanding the importance of clear handshapes and the different categories.
- Use of appropriate signs
- How to give simple directions

**Grammar and Structures**

- 'Good' 'Flat' 'Clawed', 'Pointing', 'Closed fist' – groups of handshapes
- Conveying variations of one sign using hands and facial expressions e.g. light/gentle rain, raining, heavy, torrential etc.
- Using signs and pointing to give directions

**Work after class**

- Practising what you learnt today
- Homework:-
  - Practising & understanding different clear handshapes
  - Practising weather & direction signs

## Lesson 7: Days of the week; Months of the year; Describing Time; Explaining the Calendar including Festive & Bank days; Demonstrating Sports, Hobbies & Interests

**Language Functions**

- Recap lesson 6.
- **Introduction to days and months signs,**
- Past, present & future
- Use of appropriate signs
- Explaining different types of Sport. Asking someone which Sport they like.
- Describing different types of hobbies/interest.

**Grammar and Structures**

- **Using number handshapes to give your date of birth**
- Using handshapes to describe timelines.
- Using the Calendar signs to describe different Bank Holidays, religious festivals, and saint days.
- Using Sport signs to describe your favourite sport, and hobbies/interest

**Work after class**

- Practising what you learnt today.
- Homework:-
  - **Practising Days of the week & Months of the year**
  - Practising Timeline signs, linked to Past, Present and Future.
  - Practising Sport, Hobbies & Interest signs

## Lesson 12: Discussions; Feeling and Emotions; 'G' Person; Sign variations on the word 'look, back' etc

**Language Functions**

- Recap lesson 11

**Grammar and Structures**

**Work after class**
