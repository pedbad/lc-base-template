# MBA Mandarin Chinese

Judge Business School / Language Centre

**Dates:** 2-27 July 2012

**Venues:** Language Centre

**Prerequisites:** None. The Course is for *ab initio* learners of the Mandarin Chinese language.

## Course Aims

The aim of this course is for the students to:

- a) Acquire the basic listening, speaking and reading skills needed to communicate with Chinese speakers in everyday situations and within a business context;
- b) More specifically, upon completion of the course a student will be able to:
  - Introduce oneself and greet people
  - Talk about one's family and origin
  - Order food and drinks in restaurants
  - Access travel information and purchase tickets
  - Introduce one's profession and line of work
  - Make an appointment
  - Discuss a business schedule
  - Reply to an invitation

The Course focuses on three language skills: listening, speaking and reading. Due to the complex nature of the Chinese writing system (characters rather than an alphabet), writing is not one of the main aims of this course.

The Course is designed to match the Association of Language Testers in Europe (ALTE) framework and descriptors and the students will be working towards the A2 -ALTE Level One (http://www.alte.org/framework/index.php).

## Specific objectives

- a) To encourage students with their listening and speaking skills and provide maximum opportunity for practice;
- b) To promote a positive attitude towards language learning in general and Chinese especially;
- c) For students to develop an understanding and a solid, pro-active grasp of the Chinese business practice.

## Didactic materials

The course will make use of a wide range of multimedia online materials that will be accessible through the CamTools virtual learning environment as well as of a textbook:

- a) CULP Chinese online multimedia materials (LC of the University of Cambridge);
- b) Chinese learning resources on CamTools (LC of the University of Cambridge);
- c) Business Language: Huang & Wei. *Business Chinese. Essential Topics*. Cypress Books, 2009.

## Course structure

The Course will be delivered over 4 weeks in July. There will be 2 hours of language tuition every weekday for a total of 40 hours. The group will consist of a maximum of 15 students. In addition to face to face, classroom teaching, students will be expected to work one hour a day on the online multimedia materials as well as spend another 1-2 hours daily on homework and study.
