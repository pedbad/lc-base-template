## Week 1: Talking about interests and hobbies

**Grammar structure**

- Complement of degree: Position of the recipient of a verb
  - S + V + O + V + 得 + adjective
  - S + O + V + 得 + adjective
  - O + S + V + 得 + adjective
- 除了…, 还…

**Work after class**

- 慢速中文, HSK LEVEL 5 Exercises

**Objectives**

- Learn to talk about one's interests and hobbies in sports and entertainment

## Week 2: Invitation

**Grammar structure**

- 就要…了
- 如果（要是）…，就…
- 多…啊！

**Work after class**

- 慢速中文, HSK LEVEL 5 Exercises

**Objectives**

- Learn to invite someone to certain activities and decline invitation politely

## Week 3: Talking about one's physical features

**Grammar structure**

- Reduplication of adjectives
- Noun phrase functioning as the predicate
- Sentences with a subject-predicate phrase as the predicate

**Work after class**

- 慢速中文, HSK LEVEL 5 Exercises

**Objectives**

- Learn to describe someone's physical features

## Week 4: Making an appointment

**Grammar structure**

- Complement of direction
- Expression of possibility of the complement of direction: S + V + 得/不 + complement of direction

**Work after class**

- 慢速中文, HSK LEVEL 5 Exercises

**Objectives**

- Learn to make and change an appointment

## Week 5: Being a guest

**Grammar structure**

- Pivotal Sentences "请/让/叫 + pivot + verb phrase"
- Rhetorical question "Adjective/verb + 什么？"

**Work after class**

- 慢速中文, HSK LEVEL 5 Exercises

**Objectives**

- Learn to say greeting as a guest and master useful terms when presenting gifts

## Week 6: Exam preparation

**Grammar structure**

- Conj.“总之”
- Preposition “顺（着）”、“沿（着）”
- Rhetorical question “何不……（呢）？”
- Preposition “对于”
- Preposition “至于”
- Rhetorical question “何至于……（呢）？”

**Work after class**

- 慢速中文, HSK LEVEL 5 Exercises

## Week 7: Revision

## Week 8: Talking about one's disposition

**Grammar structure**

- Comparison: A 比 B + adjective
- Comparison in negative form: A 没有 B + adjective

**Work after class**

- 慢速中文, HSK LEVEL 5 Exercises

**Objectives**

- Learn to introduce briefly someone's disposition

## Week 9: Talking about sports

**Grammar structure**

- Comparison in complement of degree
  - A + verb + 得 + 比 + B + adjective
- The adverb "却"

**Work after class**

- 慢速中文, HSK LEVEL 5 Exercises

**Objectives**

- Learn to talk briefly about sports games and daily exercises

## Week 10: Making friends

**Grammar structure**

- Complement of duration: verb(phrase) + duration
- 既…又…

**Work after class**

- 慢速中文, HSK LEVEL 5 Exercises

**Objectives**

- Learn to talk about one's friend

## Week 11: Talk about TV programmes

**Grammar structure**

- The 的 phrase
- The adverbs 只 and 只是

**Work after class**

- 慢速中文, HSK LEVEL 5 Exercises

**Objectives**

- Learn to talk about TV programmes and one's favourite programmes

## Week 12: Passing on a message

**Grammar structure**

- Complement of possibility
- The adverbs 又/再 and 就/才

**Work after class**

- 慢速中文, HSK LEVEL 5 Exercises

**Objectives**

- Learn to leave a phone message, send a short message by the mobile phone and leave a written message in simple Chinese

## Week 13: Exam preparation

**Grammar structure**

- “不但不/没……，反而……”
- “除非……，才……”
- “除非……，否则/不然……”
- “非……才……”
- “不……也……”
- “再……也……”
