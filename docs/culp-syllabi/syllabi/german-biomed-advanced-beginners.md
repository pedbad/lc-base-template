## Lesson 1: Erster Kontakt (First Contact)

**Language Functions**

- Greetings
- Introducing yourself

**Grammar structures**

- personal pronouns (nominative, accusative, dative)
- conjugation of reg. + irreg. verbs
- cardinal and ordinal numbers
- interrogatives

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to greet other people, informally and formally, to be able to introduce yourself, to be able to say where you come from, and to be able to ask people the relevant questions to get the same information back.

## Lesson 2: Familie Sprachen und Herkunft (Languages and background; family)

**Language Functions**

- Saying which languages you speak
- Talking about your occupation
- Saying where and when you were born
- Talking about your family
- Reading and Listening practice

**Grammar structures**

- Articles (definite, indefinite)
- Accusative + Dative
- to be + occupation
- *in* + dative
- possessive pronouns

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to say which language(s) you speak, what your occupation is, including details of your subjects of study, to be able to say when is your birthday and what is today's date etc.
- To be able to talk about your family.
- **Listening and reading practice**

## Lesson 3: Wohnen (Living situation)

> [!important] Assessment
> **Moodle quiz: general understanding** after Lesson 3 (5% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Saying where you live
- Describing your accommodation.
- Saying who you live with.

**Grammar structures**

- Prepositions + cases
- Adjectives
- Compound nouns

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to say where you live (city /countryside) and to describe your current accommodation. To be able to say who you live with (human / animal).

## Lesson 4: Freizeitaktivitäten (Spare time)

**Language Functions**

- Saying what your hobbies / interests are
- Saying what you did at the week-end
- Describe your daily routine

**Grammar structures**

- *gern(e), lieber, am liebsten*;
- gefallen;
- Adverbs of frequency
- Separable verbs
- word order
- Perfect Tense

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to speak about your hobbies and what you do in your spare time, which sport(s) or musical instruments you prefer and generally to be able to say what you are interested in and what you do at the weekend. Being able to describe your daily routine, to know how to say the time of day.

## Lesson 5: Im Café; Im Restaurant (In the café / restaurant)

**Language Functions**

- Reserving a table
- Ordering food and drink in a restaurant
- Complaints
- Paying/ Querying the bill

**Grammar structures**

- *ich hätte gern*
- Modal verbs
- Negation of nouns
- *sein, haben* in the Simple past (*Imperfekt*)

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to make a restaurant booking, read the menu, and ask questions about the food and drink, to be able to place an order, to complain about the food, to ask for the bill and to be able to query the bill.

## Lesson 7: In-class assessment 1 + Wegbeschreibung; Stadtbummel (Directions; going to town) + [Seasonal Context]

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class assessment 1**[^1]: Grammar-based quiz (15 mins.) (10% of final mark)
> **Writing** after Lesson 7, to be submitted before Lesson 8 (5% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Asking for/giving/understanding directions
- going shopping
- asking for details about items
- Seasonal context:

**Grammar structures**

- Prepositions
- dual prepositions
- Imperative

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to ask for and to give directions, to be able to read traffic signs, to call a taxi, to explain where you want to go.
- To be able to ask details about items you would like to purchase
- To know about Christmas and/or other celebrations in German speaking countries.

## Lesson 8: Arbeit und Studium (Daily routine; work and study)

**Language Functions**

- Talking about your studies /your work/ your degree in more detail.
- Talking about your future plans.

**Grammar structures**

- Word order
- Genitive
- Conjunctions
- Future

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to talk about the subject(s) you are studying, the degree you are studying towards, your job, to be able to talk about your plans for the future, including your career plans.

## Lesson 9: Über die Vergangenheit sprechen (Talking about the past)

**Language Functions**

- Saying where you went and what you did during the last holiday.
- Saying how things were when you were a child / younger
- Deutsche Geschichte
- Reading practice
- Listening practice

**Grammar structures**

- Simple past
- *wenn, als*
- *während*

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to talk about your past (distant and recent) and about where you went on holiday and what you did while on holiday.
- To learn about some aspects of German History.
- **Listening and reading practice**

## Lesson 10: Gesundheit; gesunde Ernährung (Health; buying and cooking food)

> [!important] Assessment
> **Moodle quiz: Reading** after Lesson 10 (5% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Going to the doctor's /chemist's
- To speak over the telephone
- Healthy eating and lifestyle

**Grammar structures**

- Modal verbs (simple past + subjunctive II)
- *seit*
- *zu*+ infinitive
- Imperative
- Subordinating conjunctions

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to make an appointment over the telephone, to talk to a doctor/ chemist about your health and fitness, describe ailments, understand instructions and discuss them. To be able to buy food items, enquire about quality and quantity, to be able to speak about healthy living.

## Lesson 11: Reisen und Reservierungen (Planning a holiday; travelling and booking)

**Language Functions**

- Planning a holiday
- Finding a hotel / campsite /airbnb
- Dealing with Tripadvisor etc.
- Buying a train/plane ticket
- Making a reservation

**Grammar Structures**

- Passive mode

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to speak of your plans for a holiday. To be able to find a hotel/ airbnb/ campsite. To be able to understand reviews of hotels etc on Tripadvisor. To be able to buy train/plane tickets, to reserve a seat, to pay for the booking and reservation etc.
