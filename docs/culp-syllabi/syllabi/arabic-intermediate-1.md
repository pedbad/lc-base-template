## Lesson 1: Describing people, things, and places

**Language Function**

- Describing yourself, your family, and your friends
- Describing things and places
- Telling the reason why you favour people, things, or places over others

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Adjectives, comparative and superlative
- The possession
- Present tense + negative
- past tense + negative
- what versus who

**Work after class**

- Write a text about people, things, or places you prefer over others and why using the comparative and superlative.

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to describe people, things, and places.
- To be able to answer questions of why they favour people, things, or places over others.

## Lesson 2: Everyday activities

**Language Function**

- Talking about the daily routine on weekdays and weekends
- Telling the time when you do your different activities.
- Talking about house, place of work and public places

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Adverbs of indefinite frequency (every day, always, usually, sometimes, rarely, and never)
- Telling the clock

**Work after class**

- Prepare a 3-minute oral activity to share with the class about what you usually do on one of your days since you get up till you return home

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to speak about a day in your life in detail using the adverbs of indefinite frequency.
- To be able to tell the clock associated with the different daily activities

## Lesson 3: Travelling and making booking

**Language Function**

- Making a holiday plan
- Talking about the different means of transport
- The sightseeing
- Going around a city or a country

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Future tense + negative
- giving directions
- the imperative
- prepositions

**Work after class**

- Choose a city you have never been to, but you would love to visit and tell us why you want to visit it and with whom

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk and write about how to travel, what you need before travelling.
- To be able to ask about and give directions.
- To be able to book flight, train, and hotel room

## Lesson 4: Religious and national festivals and celebrations

**Language Function**

- Talking about the different festivals and celebrations in the Arab and Islamic countries
- Talking about the different festivals and celebrations in the Western and Christian countries

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- The jussive verbs
- The subjunctive verbs
- The equational sentences

**Work after class**

- Write about your favourite festival or celebration

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about the different types of festivals and celebration.
- To be able to distinguish between the religious and national festivals and celebrations.

## Lesson 5: Work and profession

**Language Function**

- Talking about the different professions and places of work
- Talking about working from home virtually
- Work interview
- How to write a CV

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- The relative clause
- The numbers (100s - 1000s)
- Question words (what, why, where, when and how)
- Active participle
- Passive participle

**Work after class**

- Talk about the advantages and disadvantages of working virtually

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about work and work environment.
- To be able to write a CV

## Lesson 6: Online shopping

> [!important] Assessment
> **Quiz 1 Reading 15%** (compulsory quiz on Moodle after class)

**Language Function**

- Talking about the different shopping experiences
- The online shopping pros and cons

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Revising the comparative and superlative
- The nominal sentences versus the verbal sentence
- The idhafa case

**Work after class**

- Grammar exercises
- Reading comprehension
- **Compulsory Quiz on Moodle after class**

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about the best ways to shop according to them.
- To be able to read and understand the description of an online purchase

## Lesson 7: Sports as a hobby and as a Profession

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class Grammar Quiz 1 10%**

**Language Function**

- Talking about the importance of exercising and its positive effects on health
- Talking about sports as entertainment
- Talking about Sports clothes and equipment
- Special sports days
- **Assessment 1 10%**

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- The past continuous using (كان + present tense)
- Connectors (لذلك – لأنَّ – مع ذلك – من أجل ذلك – وهكذا – كذلِك)
- Justifying their choices using (بسبب – لأنَّ)

**Work after class**

- Write about a special sports day in your city

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about the different sports.
- To be able to tell why they favour a sport or a hobby over another

## Lesson 8: When I was younger

**Language Function**

- Talking about the old times memories
- Talking about things they used to do or like
- Talking about things they didn't use to do or like

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- The adjectives with (كان)
- Revising the present tense with (كان) = كنتُ أُحب أن / كنتُ أشرب / كنتُ آكُل

**Work after class**

- Talk about things you used to like when you were in school.

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about things or people they used to like when they were younger.

## Lesson 9: Hopes and wishes.

**Language Function**

- Talking about their hopes, wishes and what they wanted to achieve

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- The conditional using (لو – إذا – إن)
- Using the verbs (أرجو – أتمنّى – آمُل – أُريد) with subjunctive أن

**Work after class**

- Write the answer to the following question: If you had the chance to fulfil one of your old dreams, which one would you choose? Why?

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about their hopes and wishes in the past as well as the future.

## Lesson 10: The natural world

**Language Function**

- Talking about the natural world around us such as mountains, lakes, rivers, deserts, or oases
- Talking about some of the different activities such as hiking, fishing, skiing, skating …etc

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- The different types of adverbs (الحال)

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about the natural world around us

**Language Function**

- Talk about the different types of pollution and how they affect our life and health.
- How to save the environment

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Revising the past, present and future tenses

**Work after class**

- Grammar Homework

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to talk about the negative effects of pollution and how to save the environment

## Lesson 12: Our dream houses.

> [!important] Assessment
> **Quiz 3 Listening 15%** (Moodle quiz after class)

**Language Function**

- Talking about the dream house, how it looks, how many rooms and where it is.
- Talk about the different things they want to have in their houses

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Likes and dislikes
- Numbers and nouns

**Work after class**

- Talk about the house of your dreams and in which city you want it to be
- **Moodle Quiz after class**

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to describe the different rooms in a house and the items they have in them

## Lesson 13: Good news versus bad news

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class Grammar Quiz 2 10%**

**Language Function**

- Talking about what we consider as good news.
- Talking about what we consider as bad news.

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- That = أنَّ + attached pronouns (أنّي / أنَّهُ / أنَّها / أنَّهُم / أنَّكُم ..الخ)

**Work after class**

- Write a text about your best news for this week

**Learning Objectives**

- To complete and consolidate previous learning and to be able to read and write a paragraph in the target language
