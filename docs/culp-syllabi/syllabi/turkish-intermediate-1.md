## Lesson 1: Introduction & Review

**Language Functions**

- Course introduction, class expectations, review of A2 structures and vocabulary

**Grammar Structures**

- Revision of the tenses; review of the previous grammar topics
- Adverbial clause **–(y)ken**

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p. 9–11; review A2 notes and vocabulary list
- Listening: *Bizim Mahalle* (part 4)

## Lesson 2: News

**Language Functions**

- Giving opinions about news and media; expressing curiosity
- Listening: *Akşam Haberleri* (part 4)

**Grammar Structures**

- Adverbial clause **–(y)ken**; **Past continuous tense –(I)yordu** for narration

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p. 11–15; watch a short Turkish news video and summarize it.

## Lesson 3: Social Media

**Language Functions**

- Talking about current events, daily news, and social media

**Grammar Structures**

- **Past continuous tense –(I)yordu**; question forms in complex sentences

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p. 15-17; write a short paragraph about a news story.
- Listening: *Google'ın Öyküsü* (part 1)

## Lesson 4: Interviews

**Language Functions**

- Expressing and comparing opinions; agreeing and disagreeing
- Listening: *Song: Yalan Dünya* (Part 3)

**Grammar Structures**

- Reciprocal Construction: **–(I)ş**

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p. 19–24; prepare a short dialogue expressing opinions

## Lesson 5: Comments and Opinions

**Language Functions**

- Expressing likes, dislikes, and preferences

**Grammar Structures**

- Use of **ile**, **birbiri** structures, connectors, and contrastive words
- Optative / Wish Mood **–sA**

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p. 24-29; write a paragraph comparing two opinions
- *Geçmişten Günümüze Gazete* (Part 1)

## Lesson 6: Education

> [!important] Assessment
> **Reading on Moodle (10% of the final mark)**

**Language Functions**

- Talking about education and learning experiences
- Listening: *Sınav Sonucu Belli Oldu* (Part 2)

**Grammar Structures**

- Conditional mood **–sA**; making hypotheses
- Past Conditional Mood: **(–sAydI)**

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p. 31–38; write about your school or university experience

## Lesson 7

**Language Functions**

- Revision of Units 1–3; oral practice and listening tasks

**Grammar Structures**

- General review of all cases and tenses covered

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p. 38–41; midterm grammar quiz and listening comprehension
- *Dünyadaki İlginç Kütüphaneler* (Part 5)

## Lesson 8: The Importance of Learning a Foreign Language

**Language Functions**

- Talking about languages, personal progress, and teachers

**Grammar Structures**

- Necessitative Mood / Obligation Suffix **–mAlI;**
- expressing time duration: **–AlI, –DIğIndAn beri, –DI … –AlI**

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p. 43-47; short writing "Why is learning Turkish important?"
- Listening: *Her Kapıyı Açan Anahtar: Yabancı Dil* (Part 8)

## Lesson 9: Where I Live

**Language Functions**

- Describing yourself, home, and environment; formal introductions

**Grammar Structures**

- Necessitative mood **–mAlI**;
- Causative Suffixes **–DIr, –Ir, –t, –Ar**

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p. 47–49;
- write a paragraph describing your home and neighborhood
- Listening: *Emlakçıda* (Part 2)

## Lesson 10: Let's Get to Know Each Other

> [!important] Assessment
> **Listening on Moodle (10% of the final mark)**

**Language Functions**

- Talking about possessions, relationships, and cultural background
- Listening: *Servis Görevlisini Beklerken* (Part 2)

**Grammar Structures**

- Causative forms **–t, –Ir, –DIr**;
- connecting words: **bundan dolayı, bu yüzden, ayrıca** etc.

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p. 49–51;
- prepare a short presentation "My Culture and My City"

## Lesson 11: Biographies

**Language Functions**

- Talking about inspiring people

**Grammar Structures**

- Reflexive and passive verbs **–l, –n**;
- reflexive word: **kendi**
- "It is necessary to…"
- "One must…" construction: **-mAk lazım**

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p. 53–57;
- write a short biography of a famous person
- Listening: *Ankara Film Festivali* (Part 1)

## Lesson 12: Removing Obstacles

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class Writing Activity (20% of the final mark)**

**Language Functions**

- Talking about disabilities, challenges

**Grammar Structures**

- "to have to … / to be obliged to …": **-mAk zorunda kal-**
- "need to / should / must": **-mAk gerek-**
- "it is essential / it is a must / it is necessary": **-mAk şart**

**Work After Class**

- Workbook p.57–61; write story of a person who admires you.
