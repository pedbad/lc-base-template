# Latin Basic 1

*Last reviewed, Friday 17 April 2026*

## General Information

### Description

This course aims to provide students with a proficiency-oriented immersion in beginning Latin with the primary goal of reading comprehension. It will emphasise the study of grammar (both morphology and syntax) as well as the memorisation of vocabulary. By the end of Basic Latin 1, students should have a grasp of the fundamentals of Latin grammar and basic Latin vocabulary (roughly equivalent to the OCR GCSE specification). Although the focus of this class is on reading comprehension, students will also develop skills for translation from Latin into English.

### Objectives

- Students will develop a clear understanding of the structure of language and parts of speech (in both Latin and English).
- Students will learn about the morphology of Latin words, based around the principles of stems / endings.
- Students will gain experience reading graded Latin texts which provide comprehensible input. This reading will help students learn and practice the principles required for reading Latin quickly and fluently, namely:
  - Identifying parts of speech and morphology
  - Understanding case usage
  - Recognising clause structure and other syntactic features
  - Gaining familiarity with Latin word order
- Students will build a vocabulary of several hundred of the most frequent Latin words (up to about a thousand), which they will repeatedly encounter in reading and exercises.
- Students will be able to compose sentences and respond to basic prompts in Latin.
- Students will be able to translate sentences from Latin into idiomatic English.
