## Session 1: Meeting New People III

**Language Functions**

- Greeting and meeting someone for the first time
- Introducing yourself and your friend
- Talking about nationality, hometown, age, profession, and your Korean study
- Stating reason(s)

**Grammar and Key Points**

- More bespoke expressions used in meeting new people
- Expressions and vocabulary related to nationality, hometown, age, profession and your Korean study
- Formal sentence ending
- Honorific form of counting unit for people
- Interrogative to ask about length of time

**Work after Class**

- Write a paragraph introducing yourself including information covered in class

## Session 2: Weather and Seasons

**Language Functions**

- Talking about four distinct seasons of Korea
- Different activities by season
- Talking about the weather

**Grammar and Key Points**

- More describing words about the weather
- Irregular adjectives with 'ㅂ'

**Work after Class**

- Write a paragraph about the four seasons and their characteristics

## Session 4: Korean Cooking

**Language Functions**

- Learning essential ingredients of Korean dishes

**Grammar and Key Points**

**Work after Class**

- Compile a list of Korean food names and ingredients
- Write a recipe of your chosen dish
