## Session 1: First Contact (1)

**Language Functions**

- Introductions
- The language used when meeting someone for the first time, expressions used to enquire about someone's name and giving one's name.
  - Greeting and goodbyes
  - Names

**Grammar and structures**

- Basic pronouns: 我, 你
- Simple questions: 吗

**Work after class**

- First Contact:
  - Pronunciation: Syllables, initials, finals and tones
  - Dialogues 1: Greetings and goodbyes and 2: What is your name?

## Session 2: First Contact (2) & Origin and Languages (1)

**Language Functions**

- Look at the language you need when you meet someone to find out where they come from, if they are a student, to ask about their area of study.
  - Origins
  - Education
- Introduction to characters
- Numbers 1-10

**Grammar and structures**

- Nice to meet you
- The use of 是

**Work after class**

- First Contact:
  - Dialogue 3: Nice to meet you
- Origin and Languages:
  - Dialogue 1: Are you a student?
- Write numbers 1-10
