## Session 1: Education (1)

**Language Functions**

- Introductions
- The language used to talk about university related issues: university, college and class etc

**Grammar and structures**

- 从…来／起

**Work after class**

- Unit 1: Education
  - Dialogue 1: Freshers' party
  - Dialogue 2: I study Chinese

## Session 2: Education (2) & Socialising (1)

**Language Functions**

- The language you are likely to need when socialising: subject of study, talk about birthday

**Grammar and structures**

- Verb reduplication
- Fixed expression
- Number + 多

**Work after class**

- Unit 1: Education
  - Dialogue 3: My college
- Unit 2: Socialising
  - Dialogue 1: Happy Birthday

## Session 3: Socialising (2)

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**: Listening and Reading, 10% final mark

**Language Functions**

- The language used when you are talking about date and social activities.

**Grammar and structures**

- 是…的
- 岁
- 给, 送给

**Work after class**

- Unit 2: Socialising
  - Dialogue 2: He was born in Shanghai
  - Dialogue 3: Mark's birthday party
- **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**

## Session 4: Leisure (1)

**Language Functions**

- The language you are likely to need to discuss ball sports and to arrange times to play or watch sport.
  - Internet related terms
  - Leisure activities

**Grammar and structures**

- 给… 为
- Using 了

**Work after class**

- Unit 3: Leisure
  - Dialogue 1: Keeping in touch
  - Dialogue 2: The fitness studio

**Work after class**
