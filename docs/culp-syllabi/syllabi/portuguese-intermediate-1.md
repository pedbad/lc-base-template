## Lesson 1: Introducing ourselves

**Speaking of our background and our current studies**

***APRESENTAÇÕES***

**Language Functions**

- Course introduction.
  - Resources, aims, assessment and teacher.
  - Introducing oneself and each other to the group
  - Talking about the family, age, country of origin and residence
  - Meu nome é...
  - Sou + nacionalidade - Falo...
  - Tenho ... anos.
  - Moro em/no/na...

**Grammar structures**

- Definite and indefinite articles
- Gender of nouns
- Subject personal pronouns
- Interrogative pronouns
- Regular and irregular verbs in the present
- *tu, você* and *o senhor/a senhora* (formal and informal registers)
- Demonstrative pronouns: *este/esse/aquele*
- Prepositions of place
- The alphabet
- Verbs SER and ESTAR in contrast

**Work after class**

- Moodle - 1. Apresentações

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to introduce oneself, giving information about one's age, origin and where one lives.
- To be able to describe one's family and one's study/job in general and currently.

## Lesson 2: Talking about routine and free time

***NOSSO DIA-A-DIA***

**Language Functions**

- Telling the time
- Describing activities and timetables
- Talking about likes and free time activities

**Grammar structures**

- The present tense: regular and irregular verbs
- Reflexive verbs (*Eu me acordo, me levanto, me penteio*...).
- GOSTAR, ADORAR and similar verbs
- Verbs FAZER, JOGAR
- Impersonal: é possível

**Work after class**

- Moodle: 2. Nosso dia-a-dia

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to describe one's routine and what one does during one's free time and personal likes.

## Lesson 3: Bars, restaurants, food

***PARA CURTIR!***

**Language Functions**

- Describing the ingredients of a dish
- Agreeing on a possible menu
- Talking about the past
- Talking about eating habits

**Grammar structures**

- The present tense: revision
- Pretérito imperfeito do indicativo
- The gerund (*está comendo*)
- GOSTAR, ADORAR and similar verbs.

**Work after class**

- Moodle: 3. Bares, restaurantes e comida

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to describe what's in a dish, to discuss menu options, and express likes. To compare eating habits and times in different Brazilian cities.

## Lesson 4: Places and directions

**LUGARES E ENDEREÇOS**

**Language Functions**

- Ask and give instructions to get to a place
- Describing places: geographical situation, main features.
- Making comparisons
- Giving opinions

**Grammar structures**

- Prepositional phrases (*ao lado de*).
- SER, ESTAR and FICAR in contrast
- Adjectives and constructions with *o bom disso…o ruim disso…*
- Connecting words: *mas, no entanto, ainda que, além disso*

**Work after class**

- Moodle: 4. Lugares e endereços

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To ask for and give directions to a place.
- To describe places and cities in Brazil, and compare them.

## Lesson 9: Visiting the doctor's; Keeping fit; Asking for help

**VAMOS AO MÉDICO** – **ESTAR EM FORMA** – **PEDINDO AJUDA**

**Language Functions**

- Saying what the problem is
- Explaining what we do to keep fit
- Asking for and giving advice
- Explaining what happened
- Explaining the causes and consequences of an accident

**Grammar structures**

- DOER and other similar verbs
- The formal imperative (*tome um analgésico*)
- Verbs SER, ESTAR and FICAR contrasted (*sou/estou gordo/a*, fiquei doente)
- The present subjunctive (*Eu aconselho que você faça mais exercícios.*)

**Work after class**

- Moodle: 9. Manter-se em forma

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to express what is wrong with our health and to ask for medicine and understand instructions from a doctor or pharmacist
- To be able to recount what one does and what one ought to do to keep fit, and to analyse what happens if one does something else.
- To be able to recount an accident in general.

## Lesson 11: Telling anecdotes; Story telling

**CONTAR HISTÓRIAS** – **COMO ERA A VIDA ANTIGAMENTE E AGORA**

**Language Functions**

- Explaining details of an event
- Describing the circumstances before and after the event
- Describing how people used to live
- Comparing life in the past with the present
- Analysing changes and historical events

**Grammar structures**

- Possessive adjectives and pronouns (meu lugar, é meu)
- O pretérito imperfeito contínuo (estava chovendo muito)
- Contrast between o pretérito imperfeito e pretérito perfeito (andava de bicicleta quando bateram em mim)
- Conditional structures: se+imperfeito subjuntivo… quando+futuro do subjuntivo (condicional)
- Prepositions *por* and *para*
- Demonstrative pronouns (este era o porto, era isto)

**Work after class**

- Moodle: 11. Como era a vida antigamente e como é agora

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to recount an incident in detail, describing the setting and elaborate what could have happened.
- To be able to analyse what life used to be in the past and compare it with the present, recounting historic events.

## Lesson 13: Working and studying (1); Looking for a job

**TRABALHO E ESTUDO** – **PROCURANDO TRABALHO**

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class assessment 2: grammar test (10% of the final mark)**

**Language Functions**

- Descrever um trabalho
- Falar da experiência no trabalho
- Falar dos estudos realizados
- Falar do que fazia antes
- Descrever obrigações e responsabilidades
- Analisar exigências para um curso/trabalho

**Grammar structures**

- Adjectives and agreement with nouns and verbs
- desde ... há xx anos atrás... and other adverbs of time
- O pretérito perfeito do Indicativo (Fiz/Cursei...)
- O pretérito imperfeito do Indicativo (Trabalhava..., fazia…)
- Obligation with TER QUE… and DEVER
- SER + adjective + infinitivo/que+pres. subjuntivo (É imprescindível conhecer várias línguas/que saibas combinar indústria e design)

**Work after class**

- Moodle: 13. Trabalho e estudo

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to describe one's work experience, pointing at completed studies/jobs, and writing a CV that
- To be able to analyse what one used to do before the current work/study situation, one's current responsibilities, and to produce one's own CV for an interview

**Oral presentation**: on lesson 15, 30% of the final mark (role-play in pairs: 6-8 minutes followed by 2 or 3 questions from the class and teacher)

All end of course oral presentations / role-plays / debates take place in class time in the presence of classmates. Students may have notes as an aide-memoire, but reading a script will result in deduction of 10 points.

> [!important] To pass the course
> **Students will be required to achieve at least 50% of the available marks in the speaking component, and 50% overall in the assessments, and to have at least 80% attendance in order to pass the course.**
