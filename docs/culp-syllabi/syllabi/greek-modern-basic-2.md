## Lesson 1: INTRODUCTIONS & EXCHANGE OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

**Language Functions**

- Introducing myself and others
- Exchanging personal information
- Describing myself (body, facial features, character traits)

**Grammar and structures**

- Revisiting basic verb and noun declensions
- Adjectives ending in *-ός, -ιά, -ό*
- Adjectives: word order and agreement
- Adverbs ending in (-α)

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, First Contact** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, About me** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, Basic grammar:** Activities

## Lesson 2: HOUSING & ACCOMMODATION

**Language Functions**

- Talking about your home
- Types of accommodation
- Reading the classifieds
- Looking for a place to live
- Asking and giving directions
- Doing chores

**Grammar and structures**

- Present tense of verbs
- Present tense of passive verbs, *ενοικιάζεται*
- ordinal numerals
- adverbs of place

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, Describing your home and different types of accommodation** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, Grammar: verbs, numerals and adverbs** Activities

## Lesson 3: TALKING ABOUT THE PAST (1): MY LIFE STORY

> [!important] Assessment
> **SET: COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**: general understanding, 5% final mark

**Language Functions**

- Personal stories
- Narrating a story in the past
- Birthdays
- Telling the date

**Grammar and structures**

- Past tense of A, B1 and B2 verbs
- Past tense of certain passive forms: *ήρθα, γεννήθηκα*
- Nouns: the genitive case
- Numbers and dates

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, About me: origin and personal story** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, the past tense and the genitive case:** Activities
- **SET: COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**

- **ESSENTIALS, Where you went and what you did during the last holiday** Activities

## Lesson 7: CINEMA OR THEATRE?

> [!important] Assessment
> **IN-CLASS QUIZ (1)**: in-class grammar quiz 1, 10% of the final mark
>
> **SET: COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (3) Short essay to be submitted on Moodle before lesson 8**: writing, 5% final mark

**Language Functions**

- Talking about entertainment types
- Talking about hobbies, leisure activities and cultural interests
- Making a suggestion
- Accepting & rejecting invitations
- Expressing preference, likes and dislikes
- Expressing a wish, an intention, a decision
- Wishes (e.g. *καλά να περάσετε*)

**Grammar and structures**

- *Μου αρέσει* + the continuous subjunctive
- Simple subjunctive of A, B1 & B2 verbs
- Irregular verbs in the subjunctive mood
- Aspect: use of the simple or the continuous subjunctive
- Negating the subjunctive mood
- Clitics and the subjunctive

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, Going out** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, the subjunctive mood (simple aspect):** Activity
- **SET: COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (3)**
- **Short essay to be submitted on Moodle before lesson 8**

## Lesson 9: GOING SHOPPING

**Language Functions**

- At the market
- At the shops
- Weights and measures
- Asking about the cost

**Grammar and structures**

- Pronouns:

  - Indefinite pronouns, *κανένας, καμία, κανένα*

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, Shopping** Activities

- Talking about different professions

- Talking about career plans

**Work after class**

## Lesson 11: AT THE DOCTOR'S

**Language Functions**

- Describing a health problem
- Expressing feelings
- Expressing necessity/ obligation
- Specialisations of doctors and body parts
- Giving advice/ instructions
- Talking about the Greek health system
- Healthy eating and lifestyle

**Grammar and structures**

- Usage of the subjunctive instead of the imperative
- The impersonal *πρέπει να*
- Adjectives in *-ης, -ης, -ες*
- The quantifier *όλος, όλη, όλο*
- The irregular adjective *πολύς-πολλή-πολύ* and the adverb *πολύ*

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, Ailments and doctor's orders** Activities
- **ESSENTIALS, Grammar** Activities
