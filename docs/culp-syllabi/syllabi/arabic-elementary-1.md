## Lesson 1: First contact 1

**Language Functions**

- Review the Arabic script
- Greetings
- Talking about yourself
- Nationalities

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Questions 1: ... هل \ لماذا \ ما
- Possession
- Non verbal sentences
- Present tense 1
- Review numbers 0-100
- Nisba adjectives

**Work after Class**

- Grammar exercises
- Review listening
- Fill out a form with your personal details

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to introduce yourself and exchange personal information
- Talk about your nationality and profession
- To be able to ask questions.

## Lesson 2: First contact 2

**Language Functions**

- Talking about your family
- Describing character
- Talking about childhood

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Review numbers 100 – 2000
- Past tense
- Pronouns
- Idafa
- Connectors 1: ثم \ ف \ اِن
- Review كان

**Work after Class**

- Grammar exercises
- Review listening
- Write a short paragraph about your family

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to describe your family
- Introducing famous people

## Lesson 3: House & Home

**Language Functions**

- Describing your home
- Describing your city and neighbourhood

**Grammar Structures & Expressions**

- Adjectives
- The plural
- Connectors 2: اَمّا\ لذلك

**Work after Class**

- Grammar exercises
- Reading comprehension

**Learning Objectives**

- To be able to describe your home and town

## Assessment

**ASSESSMENT FOR ELEMENTARY 1 ARABIC IS AS FOLLOWS:**

**2 pieces of homework:**

| Assessment | Skill | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **After Lesson 6 Moodle quiz** | Listening | 15% final mark |
| **After Lesson 10 Moodle quiz** | Reading | 15% final mark |
| **After Lesson 11 Moodle quiz** | Writing | 20% final mark |

| Assessment | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| **In-class grammar quiz 1**, Lesson 7 | 10% of the final mark |
| **In-class grammar quiz 2**, Lesson 13 | 10% of the final mark |

**ORAL: (worth 30% of total) In groups of 2 or 3.**

**Duration: 6-8 minutes for groups of 2 and 8-10 minutes for groups of 3.**

> [!important] Attendance
> **80% attendance requirement to pass the course.**

[^1]: **NB:** Students should enact a role play in front of the class based on the content of the lessons. The list of units can function as a guideline. Students prepare the role plays outside lesson time and may bring notes as an aide-memoire for the oral presentation, but reading a script will result in deduction of 10 points.
