## Lesson 1: TALKING ABOUT YOURSELF (1)

**Language Functions**

- Greetings
- Introductions
- Stating your nationality
- Stating your marital status
- Stating your occupation
- Spelling in French

**Grammar and structures**

- to be called (s'appeler)
- to be (être)
- adjectives of nationality
- subject pronouns
- negation
- interrogative: comment
- registers: tu/vous

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, First Contact**
  - Greetings + Activities 1 and 2
  - Addressing people
  - Introducing someone + Activities 1 and 2
  - Marital status + Activities 1 & 2
- **ESSENTIALS, About me:**
  - Name + Activity
  - Nationality activities
  - Professions + Activities 1 & 2
- **ESSENTIALS, Alphabet and Pronunciation:** + 6 Activities

## Lesson 2: TALKING ABOUT YOURSELF (2)

**Language Functions**

- Talking about your family
- Saying your age
- Saying where you come from
- Saying which languages you speak

**Grammar and structures**

- to have (avoir)
- indefinite articles (un, une, des)
- possessive adjectives (mon, ma, mes, ton, ta, tes, votre, vos)
- to come from (venir de)
- –er verb: parler
- more interrogatives
- cardinals 1 – 50

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, About me:**
  - Family + Activity
- **ESSENTIALS, Numbers:**
  - 0-30 and 30-100 + Activity

## Lesson 3: DISCOVERING FRANCE

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**: general understanding, 5% final mark

**Language Functions**

- Saying where France is
- Describing France: neighbours, rivers, mountain ranges, population etc
- Talking about Paris

**Grammar and structures**

- definite articles
- Où se trouve… ?
- prepositions before continents
- Il y a

**Work after class**

- GÉOGRAPHIE: http://www.jeuxpedago.com/jeux-histoire-geo-ce1-cm2-la-france-et-ses-voisins-_pageid283.html
- PARIS: http://monumentsdeparis.net/
- **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**

## Lesson 4: TALKING ABOUT WHERE YOU LIVE

**Language Functions**

- Saying where you live
- Saying where your family lives
- Stating your place of birth

**Grammar and structures**

- to live (habiter)
- Prepositions before countries/ towns
- Prepositional phrases: à la campagne etc
- definite articles cont'd agreement & position of adjectives
- more interrogatives

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, About me:**
  - Residence + Activities

## Lesson 5: TALKING ABOUT FREE TIME

**Language Functions**

- Stating preferences, likes and dislikes
- Talking about hobbies and interests

**Grammar and structures**

- present tense:
  - to like (aimer)
  - to hate (détester)
  - to prefer (préférer)
- to do (faire) + partitives
- jouer à (to play)
- jouer de

**Work after class**

- **FAYF, BAR:**
  - Talking about leisure: Learn it first + activities
  - Chatting: Learn it first + All activities
- **ESSENTIALS, Days of the week**

## Lesson 8: GOING TO A CAFÉ

**Language Functions**

- Exchanging phone numbers
- Arranging to meet
- Ordering and paying for drinks and snacks

**Grammar and structures**

- quel?
- to take (prendre)
- vouloir + infinitive (to want to)
- aller à (to go to)
- Je voudrais (I'd like)
- Quantities: une bouteille de etc
- Fillings, toppings, flavours: Une pizza à la tomate… etc

**Work after class**

- **FAYF: BAR**
  - Ordering a drink

## Lesson 9: FINDING YOUR WAY ABOUT TOWN

**Language Functions**

- Asking for and giving directions
- Discovering a French town

**Grammar and structures**

- Pour aller à … ?
- Est-ce qu'il y a … près d'ici ?
- Imperative
- Prepositions of place: derrière, devant, à, à côté de, en face de, près de, (pas) loin de

**Work after class**

- **FAYF, CAMPSITE**
  - Asking for directions + activities

## Lesson 10: TALKING ABOUT YOUR STUDIES

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (3)**: reading, 5% final mark

**Language Functions**

- Saying when your day starts and ends in term-time
- Saying what you're studying/where you work
- Describing what you do during a typical week day
- Saying how long you've been working in/studying at Cambridge

**Grammar and structures**

- to start (commencer)
- to finish (terminer/finir)
- to study (étudier)
- to read (lire)
- to learn (apprendre)
- to write (écrire)
- to work (travailler)
- present tense with depuis

**Work after class**

- **ESSENTIALS, Time**
  - all activities
- **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (3)**
