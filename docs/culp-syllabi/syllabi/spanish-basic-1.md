# CULP Programa de Estudios Básico 1

*Last reviewed Monday 04 August 2025*

**Prerequisites:** This course is suitable for ***ALL*** students who have no knowledge of the Spanish language or any other Romance Language.

**Target level:** Level A1 of the CEFR

## Approach

This course provides the linguistic foundation and skills necessary for communication in predictable everyday situations. Whilst all four skills, speaking, reading, writing and listening will be developed the primary focus will be on developing speech and conversation. Ultimately, it provides an initial understanding of the cultures within the Spanish-speaking world.

The classes are task-oriented, helping students understand Spanish in use and to acquire it by use. All four communicative skills will be developed and/or strengthened – listening, speaking, reading and writing. The students will engage in interactive language activities, participating in group and pair work according to a syllabus based on systematic grammatical progression. Each class focuses on a topic that is related to the previous and following one; activities build on the students' abilities and on their previous knowledge. Grammar and vocabulary are practiced and perfected in context.

The course is divided into 15 sessions with each one introducing new and different concepts. Although all sessions follow a common thread they can easily be accessed on a standalone basis. This means that should a session be missed, the student does not lose the value of the whole course and can pick up the missed elements in later sessions. As part of the Language Centre services the students will also be offered guidance in self-study and language learning by the **John Trim Centre's services**.

## Course main objectives

- To provide a basic knowledge of Spanish culture and language.
- To revise and identify everyday topics appropriate to Level 1 and absolute beginners.
- To compare the differences between the student's own culture and that of the Spanish-speaking countries.
- To understand and use grammatical structures and vocabulary.
- To strengthen a positive and confident attitude towards language learning
- To be able to understand and exchange general and detailed information and to communicate in standard speech on a range of everyday issues.

## CULP - Spanish Basic 1: syllabus

### Lesson 1: Introducing ourselves

*NOS CONOCEMOS*

**Language functions**

- Introduction – Aims of the course – Administrative announcements – Safety Routine.
- Students and teacher introduction
- Yo me llamo… ¿Cómo te llamas?
- Survival vocabulary
- Greetings
- Useful vocabulary during class.

**Grammar structures**

- Interrogative pronouns
- Reflexive verb: *me llamo*… Verb SER
- Subject personal pronouns *tú* and *usted* (formal and informal registers)
- Demonstrative adjectives: *este/esta*
- Introduce yourself to your classmate. Mini-dialogues using the greeting structures.
- Numbers 1 to 10.

**Work after class**

- Moodle
- Homework set by teacher

### Lesson 2: Can I contact you?

*INTERCAMBIO DE INFORMACIÓN*

**Language functions**

- Introductions: formal / informal
- Nationality, language
- Marital status
- Pronunciation: basic rules
- Alphabet – Phonetics – Reading and writing Practice – Spelling
- Personal Pronouns – Verb To Be (introduction).
- Grammar basis
- Articles (un, una, unos, unas) (el, la, los, las)
- Personal pronouns
- Quantifiers: muy, mucho/muchas/os

**Grammar structures**

- The verbs in Spanish
- Present Indicative of –AR (*trabajar*) –ER (*comer*) and –IR (*vivir*) verbs
- Regular verbs in present tense
- Spelling of words and phonetics in Spanish
- Reading and transcribing (sounds into words/ words into meaning)
- Numbers 0-30

**Work after class**

- Moodle
- Homework set by teacher

### Lesson 3: Talking about family and home

*HABLAMOS DE LA FAMILIA Y DEL HOGAR*

**Language functions**

- Talking about the family
- Age
- Specifying origin and residence
- Occupation
- Meeting people, Socializing – talking about yourself.
- ***¿Cómo se escribe?***
- Spelling names – last names – giving phone numbers – and emails.
- Countries and Nationalities – Jobs – Family

**Grammar structures**

- Basic vocabulary: *family, jobs*
- Verbs: *tener, ser, estar, hacer, dedicarse (a), trabajar*
- Adjectives of origin (gentilicios)
- *Verb TENER*
- *Cardinal numbers*
- *The alphabet*
- *Verbs SER and ESTAR in contrast*
- *Articles: determined / undetermined*
- *Nouns: gender and number*
- Numbers 0-100

**Work after class**

- Moodle
- Homework set by teacher

- Present tense of *ser/estar/tener/llamarse*

### Lesson 7: My home, my town and the big city

*Hablamos de nuestras casas, pueblos y ciudades*

> [!important] Assessment
> **GRAMMAR TEST 1 (IN-CLASS)**: 15% of the final mark

**Language functions**

- Talking about our town and places
- Talk about geographical location
- The weather
- Talk about relevant aspects of a town and a city

**Grammar structures**

- Irregular verbs in the present tense
- Verbs: IR, PODER, QUERER, HABER, SER, ESTAR, TENER

- HAY: *there is/are*
- Vocabulary: places in town

**Work after class**

- Moodle
- Homework set by teacher, including:
  - The weather
  - The home
  - Writing activity

### Lesson 8: Asking the way

*PEDIMOS INDICACIONES*

**Language functions**

- Asking about places of interest
- Finding your way around (on foot, by car)
- Describing a place, e.g. a town

**Grammar structures**

- *Ser* and *estar* in context
- Ordinal numbers (*primer/a, segundo/a*…)
- ESTAR for location
- Prepositions and adverbs of place: *al lado de, enfrente de, a la derecha / a la izquierda*…
- Descriptive adjectives: *moderno, antiguo*…

**Work after class**

- Moodle
- Homework set by teacher

### Lesson 9: Having a drink and a snack; Having a meal at a restaurant

*VAMOS AL BAR; VAMOS AL RESTAURANTE*

**Language functions**

- Ordering a snack.
- Ordering a drink
- Asking for the bill
- Booking a table in a restaurant
- Choosing food items from a menu
- Expressing likes and dislikes

**Grammar structures**

- Impersonal HABER (hay…)
- Verb QUERER
- Indirect object pronouns: *me, nos* e.g. *¿Nos trae la cuenta por favor?*
- Verbs: Present tense practice (regular and irregular verbs)
- Verbo GUSTAR, and others with the same type of conjugation (*me encanta, me fascina*)

**Work after class**

- Moodle
- Homework set by teacher

**Work after class**

- **Moodle test (listening comprehension)**
