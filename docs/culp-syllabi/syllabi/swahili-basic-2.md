## Lesson 1: Talking about self in detail and Describing People

**Language Function**

- Giving personal information, talking about personal qualities etc
- making statements about favourite games, food, places etc

**Grammar Structures**

- Ninapendelea Ku…
  - Use of adjectives
  - Noun classes (Extended revision)

**Work after Class**

- Writing to describe a person you admire and say "Why"

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to: introduce yourself, giving; name, date of birth, gender, status, occupation; address, phone; describe looks, likes, dislikes, etc and ask for the corresponding information from others.
- To be able to: describe people i.e., using adjectives class 1 – 2 (M/WA – A/WA)

## Lesson 2: Finding a way around from one location to another East to West Africa to Europe etc

**Language Function**

- Finding the location
- Understand public signs and announcements.
- Ordering in-flight food and drink

**Grammar Structures**

- Subjunctive (making requests)
- unipe/utupe

**Work after Class**

- Using a map and asking questions
- Giving directions to the selected places of interest.

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to find location; understand public signs; understand public announcements
- To be able to: order in-flight food and a drink

## Lesson 3: Parts of the body and Common Maladies

> [!important] Assessment
> **Mandatory Online Quiz**

**Language Function**

- Naming key parts of the body
- Relating parts of the bodies to common maladies, e.g., *Ninaumwa na kichwa* (I have a headache)

**Grammar Structures**

- (i) Classifying parts of the body into their respective noun classes
- (ii) Using passive voices

**Work after Class**

- a) Grammar exercises on noun classes and
- b) Passive voice exercises
- c) Prepare a role-play between a patient and a GP
- **d) Mandatory Online Quiz**

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to name parts of the body and talk about maladies

## Lesson 4: Seeing a doctor

**Language Function**

- Describing symptoms of common maladies to the doctor/GP

**Grammar Structures**

- Using passive forms in describing/expressing illness, symptoms or conditions

**Work after Class**

- Writing about COVID-19 – Symptoms and prevention measures

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to describe various symptoms and prevention measures of certain illnesses or maladies

## Lesson 5: Major towns and cities

**Language Function**

- Describing major cities and towns i.e., directions, population, safety etc

**Grammar Structures**

- Using the locative class PO-KO-MO (PA-MU-KU)
- The quantifiers *-engi* (many), *chache* (few)

**Work after Class**

- Writing about your favourite town or city and say why

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to describe the world's major towns and cities

## Lesson 8: Describing lost property items

**Language Function**

- Saying what you have lost. Describing the item
- Saying where and when you lost the item

**Grammar Structures**

- Using a relative pronoun in the indefinite present tense to describe items or people
- **huyu ni** …ambaye/si…mtu ambaye
- **hawa ni**…/si…**hiki ni** kitambaa changu kipya **amba**cho nilinunua jana etc

**Work after Class**

- Using the variations mtoto huyu ndiye **ambaye** alipotea mzigo huu ndio wangu

**Learning objectives**

- To be able to describe people and things; use the general relative pronoun with the demonstratives
