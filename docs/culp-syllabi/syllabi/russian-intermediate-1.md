## Week 1

**Language functions**

- Course introduction: aims, resources, assessment
- Introductions, saying hello, good bye, etc.
- Stating your name, nationality, occupation

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- General review of basic grammar functions (verbal conjugation, case usage)
- Nationalities and languages
- Professions and occupations

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say hello, present oneself, answer/ask relevant questions
- To be able to state your origin/nationality and language you speak

## Week 2

**Language functions**

- Talk about daily routine – when in Cambridge and at home
- Saying where you come from/live
- Talk about your family

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Review of case usage and prepositions
- Numerals
- Talking about age
- Use of comparative adjectives (старше/младше +)

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say where you are from
- To be able to talk about one's family
- To be able to say what is your profession/occupation/area of study

## Week 3

> [!important] Assessment
> ***NB: HOMEWORK ASSIGNMENT 1***

**Language functions**

- Talk about hobbies and interests
- Talk about sports
- Talk about your profession, area of research
- Talk about past and future professional plans

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Review of irregular verbs conjugation
- Use of past tense
- Introduction to future tense

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's hobbies and interests, including sports
- To be able to talk about professional plans
- To be able to use future tense

***NB: HOMEWORK ASSIGNMENT 1***

## Week 4

**Language functions**

- Talking about food: types of Russian food, Russian cuisine
- Choosing and ordering drink and food in a café/restaurant

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Review of present tense
- Imperative mood
- Review of Russian numerals
- Use of Dative

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to make a restaurant booking, read the menu, discuss food

## Week 5

**Language functions**

- Planning a holiday
- Hotels – booking, amenities, etc.
- Train and plane bookings
- Asking and finding your way

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Future tense: simple and complex forms
- Revise numerals, days of week, dates, etc.

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to ask/understand directions
- To be able to understand information about hotel and make bookings
- To be able to understand information about trains and planes and make bookings
