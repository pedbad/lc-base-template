## Syllabus

### Cours 1: Introductory

**Topic**

- Introductory
- The Pronunciation Problem
- Limits and Aims of the course

**Activities**

- Listen to Intro and take part
- Practise in pairs
- Group Practice
- Class Practice

**Materials**

Handouts:

1. Texts for practice
2. Isolated words and phrases
3. Lines

**Homework**

- Record yourself reading Flaubert or/and Rimbaud: send file to Fabienne
- Bring and share your own texts

### Cours 1A: The Practice of oral vowels

**Topic**

- The Practice of oral vowels

**Activities**

- Drawing and phonetic writing.
- Eliding the 'e', inserting 'e'.
- Reading aloud practice

**Materials**

- Typed and written by hand poems
- Audio book Proust '*Du côté de chez Swann*'

**Homework**

- Listen to *Trois Contes*, 'Un Coeur Simple' Chapter 1 (Audio Book)

### Cours 2: Racine (1633 - 1699)

**Topic**

- Racine (1633 - 1699)
- The Practice of Reading - XVII century verse
- Rhythm
  - End line words
  - Punctuation

**Activities**

- Practise in pairs
- Bring your own reading of Racine for practice
- Listen to Act I *Phèdre*

**Materials**

1. Text for Practice
2. Text for Rhythm Analysis
3. DVD Production *Phèdre* by Chéreau

**Homework**

- Record yourselves reading Act I of *Andromaque*
- Bring and share short prose passages

### Cours 3: Nasal vowels and glides

**Topic**

- Nasal vowels and glides

**Activities**

- Review the Oral vowels; Write on the chart
- Learn the phonetic writing of nasal vowels
- Practise in groups short prose passages of your choice

**Materials**

1. Handout for practice
2. Texts for Practice: Rimbaud and Verlaine poems and letter extracts

**Homework**

- Record yourself reading *Le Crépuscule du matin* and *Chanson d'automne*: send file to Fabienne
- Learn one poem, or part of a poem

### Cours 4: Consonants

**Topic**

- Consonants:
- The question of assimilation

**Activities**

- Practise in pairs
- Class practice
- Recap of chart and pronunciation
- Recitation of a poem

**Materials**

- Bring your own text

**Homework**

- Feedback and evaluation
