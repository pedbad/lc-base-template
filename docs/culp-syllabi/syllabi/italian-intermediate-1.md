## Lesson 1: Introductions

**Language Functions**

- How to greet someone, introduce oneself, ask about background and family, age and study
- Where do you live, what is your house like and where is it?

**Grammar structures**

- Essere, avere and fare
- Nouns
- Articles
- Adjectives
- Present tense of regular verbs 1st and 2nd conjugation

**Work after class**

- CULP Int. unit 1
- Note culturali: La famiglia / Titoli / Buongiorno
- CULP Int. unit 2
- Note culturali: Abitazione e residenza / Comune e provincia

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to say hello, present oneself, to tell your origin, age and ask people relevant questions
- To be able to converse about one's work/study as well as family and languages spoken
- To be able to describe one's dwelling, give/ask for address, give/ask who with do people live with...

## Lesson 2: Talking about the daily routine, spare time, hobbies.

**Language Functions**

- How to talk about one's daily routine
- Talk about free time, hobbies, outings and sport

**Grammar structures**

- Present tense of regular verbs 3rd conjugation
- Reflexive verbs
- Irregular verbs
- Days of the week
- Telling the Time
- The months

**Work after class**

- CULP Int. unit 1
- Note culturali: *I giovani d'oggi…*

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's own preferences, interests, sport, hobbies, weekend activities...

## Lesson 3: At the bar, restaurant

**Language Functions**

- Talk about free time, hobbies, outings and sport
- Ordering drinks and food
- Talking to waiters and bartenders
- How to pay
- Favorite colours

**Grammar structures**

- Present tense of regular verbs 3rd conjugation
- *Preposizioni articolate*
- The *Lei* form and polite register
- Numbers
- *Piacere, preferire*
- *Demonstrative pronouns*

**Work after class**

- CULP Int. unit 3
- Note culturali: Il caffè / I caffè storici italiani / I giovani d'oggi mangiano / La pizza / Lo scontrino / I tipi di caffè

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to place orders at the bar, explain one's likes, dislikes, ask people what they want to drink...
- To be able to make a restaurant booking, read the menu, ask about the dishes, place order, talk about food, ask for the bill and ask for info re the bill...

## Lesson 4: Directions, to live in a city or in the countryside

**Language Functions**

- Ask for directions (on foot and by car)
- Talk about different types of dwellings
- Discuss lifestyle in the city and in the countryside

**Grammar structures**

- Modal verbs
- Irregular verbs
- *Quanto tempo ci vuole*
- Preferences
- The comparative/superlative
- Adverbs

**Work after class**

- CULP Int. unit 2
- *Note culturali: Abitazione e residenza, Comune e provincia, Perugia, Roma*

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to ask for directions and describe your house/flat
- To be able to discuss the pros and cons of living in a city versus living in the countryside

**Work after class**

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- Talk about one's own past

## Lesson 10: Wellbeing and the environment

**Language Functions**

- Talk about the body, health and the environment
- How to talk to medical personnel

**Grammar structures**

- Subjunctive – present / pp / imp
- Irregular plural of some parts of the body
- Modal verbs with pronouns
- *Mi fa male*

**Work after class**

- CULP Int. unit 8
- *Note culturali: Dal dottore in Italia, In farmacia, Le erbe aromatiche, La dolce vita*

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To talk about wellbeing, organic foods, health issues, the environment...
- To use the future tense

## Lesson 11: Geography and history of Italy, Italian culture

**Language Functions**

- Talk about the geography
- Introduction to and discussion about the basic notions of Italian culture

**Grammar structures**

- *Passato remoto* (reg. verbs)
- Revision of the past tenses

**Work after class**

- CULP Int. unit 9, 10, 11
- Note culturali: I caffè storici italiani, "Fratelli d'Italia", *Gli italiani e l'Italia, L'inno nazionale, I laghi, La popolazione italiana, Le regioni, Roma, Perugia,*

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To develop a basic understanding of the geography and history of Italy and to be able to talk about it
- To be able to appreciate and discuss the culture of Italy
- To be able to tell stories
