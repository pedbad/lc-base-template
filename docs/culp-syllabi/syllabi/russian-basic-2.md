## Week 1

**Language functions**

- Course introduction: aims, resources, assessment.
- Introducing oneself – name, nationality, occupation, area of study, languages you speak
- Saying where you come from /live

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- General review of basic grammar functions (verbal conjugation; case usage)

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to give and request personal information

## Week 2

**Language functions**

- Describing one's interests and hobbies
- Daily routine
- Talking about your typical weekend.
- Going places
- Counting to 100

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Review of the Accusative case
- Revision of the Nominative plural of nouns; irregular plurals
- Accusative plural forms for the inanimate nouns and modifiers
- Introduction of the verbs of motion – answering the question 'Куда?'

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's interests and hobbies, likes and dislikes
- To be able to describe one's weekend
- To be able to talk about one's free time

## Week 3

**Language functions**

- Describing a house or an apartment
- Talking about past events
- Counting from 100-900

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Possessives
- Construction 'to have'. Genitive case forms for personal pronouns
- The past tense of verbs
- Declension of the demonstrative 'этот'

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about past events
- To be able to describe one's home.

## Week 4

**Language functions**

- Asking for information about an apartment to rent
- Discussing rent
- The names of articles of furniture
- Counting from 1000 to 100,000

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- The Genitive case singular: negation
- The use of the Genitive case after numbers
- Possessive pronoun 'Чей?' (Чья? Чьё? Чьи?)
- The modal verb 'must'

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to find a place to live

## Week 5

**Language functions**

- Describing one's neighbourhood
- Patronymics
- Ordinal numbers 1-10

**Expressions and grammar structures**

- Prepositions of place
- The Prepositional case of adjectives and ordinal numbers
- Formation of patronymics

**Learning objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to talk about one's area
