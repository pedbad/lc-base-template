## Lesson 1

**Topics**

- *Language*
  - INTRODUCTION TO THE GREEK LANGUAGE
  - GREETINGS
  - INTRODUCING ONESELF
  - EXCHANGING INFORMATION ABOUT ORIGIN
- *Civilization*
  - THE HISTORY OF THE GREEK LANGUAGE

**Cultural notes**

- a. An introduction to Greece and the Greek language
- b. Greek Language Skills 1

**Grammar structures**

- Diphthongs
- General introduction about genders, cases and articles
- Some nouns
- Verbs: to be in the present tense (είμαι) and the negative form (δεν είμαι)
- I am called (με λένε - σε λένε)
- My name is (το όνομά μου είναι)
- Some personal pronouns (εγώ, εσύ, εσείς/με, σε, σας)
- Adverb of place πού
- Adverb of manner πώς
- Preposition από + accusative

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- Reading Aloud
- Greet people and introduce yourself.
- Know the names of some countries.
- Know the Greek alphabet, pronunciation and reading

## Lesson 2

**Topics**

- INTRODUCTIONS
- BASIC CONVERSATIONS ABOUT YOURSELF, YOUR NATIONALITY

**Cultural notes**

- a. A historical timeline
- b. Greek Language Skills 2

**Grammar structures**

- Cases: nominative and vocative
- Introduction to articles: definite (ο, η, το)
- Introduction to nouns: (masculine, feminine and neuter)
- Pronouns: personal (εγώ, εσύ και με, σε), possessive (μου, σου etc)
- Verbs: to be, to do in the present tense (είμαι, κάνω) and the negative form (δεν είμαι, δεν κάνω)
- Conjunctions και, ή

**Objectives for the next lesson**

- To be able to greet people at a different time of day, introduce yourself and present others
- To be able to give information about your nationality and your family
- Saying where you were born, your occupation and your studies
- Talking about where and with whom you live
- To be able to state one's age.

## Lesson 8

**Topics**

- EXPRESSING LIKES AND DISLIKES
- USING NUMBERS

**Cultural notes**

- a. The Greek educational system
- b. Greek Language Skills 8

**Grammar structures**

- Nouns in nominative Masculine in -ας, -ης, -ος, feminine in -α, -η and neuters in –ο, -ι, -μα

**Objectives for the next lesson**
