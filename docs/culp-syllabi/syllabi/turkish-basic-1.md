## Lesson 1: Introducing ourselves

**Language Functions**

**Introduce yourself and get to know your friend**

- Introduction-aims of the course
- Introduce the materials (main book, work book, audio) and explain which units will be covered during the term.
- Students and teacher introduction
- Survival vocabulary
- Useful vocabulary during the class

**Grammar structures**

- Introduction dialog
- Meeting friends
- Alphabet-phonetics-presenting Turkish characters.
- Forming basic questions
- Bu ne? Şu ne? Bu kim (What is this/that? Who is this?)

**Work after class**

- Workbook, pages 7-8-9-10-11

## Lesson 2: Where are you from?

**Language Functions**

- Country, nationality, language.
- The present tense of 'to be'
- Personal pronouns
- Daily life greetings (Görüşürüz, afiyet olsun, hoşça kal etc.)

**Grammar structures**

- Useful nouns and adjectives
- Basic sentence structures.
- Ben öğrenciyim, Sen Londralısın.(I am a student, you are from London etc.)
- Vowel harmony rules
- Variation in suffixes: buffer consonants

**Work after class**

- Workbook, pages 12-13-14-15-16-17
- Listening: http://turkceninsesi.yee.org.tr/programlar/hayatin-icinden-turkce. (part 2)

## Lesson 3: Talking about family and home

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**

**Language Functions**

- Introduce family. How old are they? What are their jobs?
- Family members (uncle, aunt etc.)
- Name of rooms in the house. (living room, kitchen etc.)
- Colours

**Grammar structures**

- Var/yok (there is/there is not)
- Question suffix (-mı,-mi, mu, mü)
- Mutfakta tencere var **mı**? (Is there a saucepan in the kitchen?)
- The possessive suffixes
- Kardeşin var mı? (Do you have siblings?)
- Nominal derivation:-lI, -lIk, -sIz
- Şekerli çay, şekerlik (tea with sugar, sugar bowl)
- Plural suffix (-lar, ler)

**Work after class**

- Workbook, pages 19-20-27/22
- Worksheets about locative case
- Working on numbers, colours and family members.
- Listening: http://turkceninsesi.yee.org.tr/programlar/hayatin-icinden-turkce. (part 3)
- **COMPULSORY MOODLE QUIZ (1)**

## Lesson 4: Our daily routine

**Language Functions**

**Daily Life**

- Talking about daily life-what do you do?
- Expressing routines
- Talk about location of objects, people and buildings.

**Grammar structures**

- Present continuous tense (affirmative and negative forms)
- The locative case
- Odada masa var? (There is a table **in** the room?)
- The ablative case
- Odadan çıkıyorum (I'm leaving the room)

**Work after class**

- Workbook, page 21-22-23-24-25-26
- Writing: write a text and say what you do during the day.
- http://turkceninsesi.yee.org.tr/programlar/hayatin-icinden-turkce. (part 5)
- Please write your text on a separate page as the teacher will take them to correct your mistakes and give them back the following week.

## Lesson 5: Hobbies; Free time

**Language Functions**

- Talking about yourself (height, job, date of birth, age, favourite food/colours)
- Expressing hobbies
- Saying what you want to do
- Specifying free time activities
- Describing likes and dislikes

**Grammar structures**

- The structure of –mAk istemek (want to do)
- New words and verbs related to the topic
- Numbers
- The dative case
- Okuldan eve gidiyorum. (I go **to** school **from** home)

**Work after class**

- Workbook, pages 28-29
- Worksheets about dative case
- Working on numbers
- Writing: write a text and introduce yourself, describe your hobbies with the structures and the words you have learnt so far. Please write your text on a separate page.

> [!important] Assessment
> **COMPULSORY READING MOODLE QUIZ (2)**

**Language Functions**

**A blast from the past!**

- Talking about past events.
- Adverbs of past tense.
- Asking questions about past

**Grammar structures**

- Past tense
- Variation in suffixes: consonant harmony
- Interrogatives: Niçin?, Neden? Nereye?

**Work after class**

## Lesson 7: What time is it?

> [!important] Assessment
> ***In class-Zoom grammar quiz 1***

**Language Functions**

**Time is money!**

- Telling the time
- Explaining what you did and what time you did it?
- Before/after (I drank a tea before work)
- Describing activities and timetables
- How did you get to work?
- Role play-chatting with friends and asking what you did today, what you did last weekend, where you are going next summer.

**Grammar structures**

- The structure of expressing time
- Interrogatives: Nasıl? İşe nasıl gittin? (How did you go to work?)
- Instrumental suffix İşe arabayla gittim. (I went to work **by** car)
- dan önce/dan sonra (before/after + noun)

**Work after class**

- Workbook, pages 31-32-33-34
- Working on the following vocabulary:
  - Months and seasons
  - Winter clothes
  - Summer clothes
  - Seasonal clothes

## Lesson 8: Shopping

**Language Functions**

**Let's go shopping!**

- Shopping for food and clothes
- Asking for what you want.
- Units of weight/quantities/measurement
- How to start and finish a conversation with the shopkeeper
- How to say what you want to buy
- Enquiring about prices, weights, measurements, sizes
- Introduce Turkish Liras
- Role play seller and customer
- Asking someone how tall they are/how much they weigh

**Grammar structures**

- Ne kadar? Kaç tane? (How much? How many?)
- New vocabulary related to the topic
- Interrogatives: hangi? (which?)
- How tall are you?
- How much do you weigh?

**Work after class**

- Work book, pages 35-36-37-38-41
- Revision with worksheets
- Writing: Make up a conversation between a customer and a shopkeeper.
- http://turkceninsesi.yee.org.tr/programlar/hayatin-icinden-turkce. (part 8)

## Lesson 10: Asking way; Describing the way and where the object is

**Language Functions**

- Asking about places of interest
- Finding your way around
- Describing a place
- Where is the park/market?
- Describing where an object is eg. Where is the book?

**Grammar structures**

- Prepositions
- İçinde, üstünde, arkasında, altında (in the bag, on the table, behind the car, under the table)
- imperative form
- Dön. Gel. (Turn. Come.)
- adverbs of place
- Sağa/sola/ileriye/geriye dön (turn right/left, go forward etc.)

**Work after class**

- Workbook, page 47
- Text 1: Write a text and describe how to go from home to school according to the given map.
- Text 2: write where the objects are in the picture.
