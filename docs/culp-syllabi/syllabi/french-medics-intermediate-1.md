## Lesson 1: TALKING ABOUT YOURSELF AND YOUR FAMILY

**Language Functions**

- Introductions
- Name, nationality, age, etc.
- Talking about your studies
- Saying which languages you speak
- Describing your family
- Spelling in French
- Numbers

**Grammar structures**

- **present tense**
- adjectives
- interrogatives
- indefinite and definite articles
- prepositions (in and to = countries and towns) and prepositional phrases
- negation
- possessive adjectives
- registers: tu/vous

**After class**

- **ESSENTIALS**
  - **First Contact**
  - **About me**
  - **Alphabet and Pronunciation**
  - **Numbers**
  - **Dates**
- **Medical Studies in France**
  - Cursus info and students' interviews: http://www.onisep.fr/Choisir-mes-etudes/Apres-le-bac/Principaux-domaines-d-etudes/Les-etudes-medicales/Les-etudes-de-medecine

## Lesson 2: WORK AND STUDY TIMETABLE & FREE TIME

**Language Functions**

- Saying how long you've been working/studying at Cambridge
- Saying when your day starts and ends
- Describing a typical working day
- Talking about hobbies
- Talking about your likes and dislikes
- Saying what you did yesterday

**Grammar structures**

- depuis and ça fait
- de... à / du... au
- **present tense cont'd:** regular, irregular and reflexive verbs
- partitives
- to play: (jouer à, jouer de)
- negation, adverbs
- **perfect tense**: regular, irregular and reflexive verbs

## Lesson 3: HEALTH AND FITNESS

**Language Functions**

- Talking about healthy eating
- Saying what you do, or ought to do, to maintain a good level of fitness
- Talking about what you do to relax
- Describing your daily routine when you were at school

**Grammar structures**

- adverbs of time and manner
- **infinitives** followed by de : éviter de, essayer de, etc.
- to have to (devoir/ il faut + infinitive)
- **imperfect:** regular, irregular and reflexive verbs

**After class**

- **FAYF Intermediate**
  - **On se soigne**
  - ***Rester en forme***
  - Comment rester en forme ; une bonne hygiène de vie.

- Cette année-là – Imparfait: http://enseigner.tv5monde.com/fle/cette-annee-la

## Lesson 4: AT THE CHEMIST'S & AT THE DOCTOR'S

**Language Functions**

- Parts of the body
- Saying what's wrong and where it hurts
- Describing how you feel
- Giving and asking for advice
- Various types of medicine; instructions and dosage

**Grammar structures**

- J'ai mal à…
- Je me sens…
- qui, que
- **future** (vous prendrez)
- Introduction to direct and indirect object pronouns
- **conditional**
- Use of **'Si' + imperfect**
- **imperative/infinitive**

**After class**

- **FAYF Intermediate**
  - **On se soigne**
  - ***Chez le pharmacien/le docteur***
  - Dire ce qui ne va pas ; Demander un médicament ; Chez le docteur ; *Extension* (décrire les symptômes + visite chez le docteur)
- **Reading Practice:** Le Doliprane
- Human body (*Le corps humain*): http://www.francaisfacile.com/exercices/exercice-francais-2/exercice-francais-48042.php
- Le français facile : expressions liées au corps humain: http://www.francaisfacile.com/exercices/exercice-francais-2/exercice-francais-70191.php

## Lesson 5: PROFESSIONS; WORKING AT THE HOSPITAL

**Language Functions**

- Meeting the hospital staff
- Talking about your own and others' duties
- Saying how long you've had this occupation
- Talking about past work experience
- Hospital wards: finding your way around the hospital

**Grammar structures**

- to be (être) + profession
- **use of tenses** pendant, depuis and il y a
- prepositions of place: derrière, devant, à, à côté de, en face de, près de, (pas) loin de

**After class**

- **FAYF Intermediate**
