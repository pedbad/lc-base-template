# Portuguese Basic 2

**Prerequisites:** Level A1 of the CEFR or equivalent. This course is a continuation of the Portuguese Basic 1 course, suitable for students who have some prior knowledge of the language and have picked up the basics of the language when travelling or at school.

**Target level:** Level A2 of the CEFR

**Approach:** The classes are **communicative** and consist of tasks oriented at helping students understand Portuguese in use and to acquire it by use. All four communicative skills will be developed and/or strengthened – listening, speaking, reading and writing. The students will engage in interactive language activities, participating in group and pair work according to a syllabus based on systematic grammatical progression. Each class focuses on a topic that is related to the previous and following one; activities build on the students' abilities and on their previous knowledge. Grammar and vocabulary are practised and perfected in context. Students will also be offered guidance in self-study and be set optional homework tasks.

## Course main objectives

- A communicative approach is used. All four skills (speaking, reading, listening and writing) are practised, with main emphasis on speaking.
- To learn the basic grammatical structures and lexis, and build on them.
- To develop and/or strengthen a positive and confident attitude towards language learning.
- To encourage speaking and ease the transition to the next level (Intermediate 1).
- To promote cultural awareness.
- To be able to communicate in everyday situations.
- To be able to understand general information and to communicate in standard speech on a range of everyday issues.
- To be able to read printed and handwritten scripts on a limited number of everyday topics.
- To be able to write simple texts using descriptive language accurately.

## Syllabus: CULP Portuguese Basic 2

### Lesson 1: Quem somos?

**Language Functions**

- Aims of the course – administrative announcements – safety routine
- Previous knowledge: what do we already know about the Portuguese Language?
- Greetings and introductions (formal and informal registers)
- Saying who we are and introducing friends
- Saying our nationality
- Talking about languages and the languages we know
- Stating our job/profession
- Useful classroom language

**Grammar Structures**

- Basic vocabulary: greetings and introductions
- Personal pronouns
- Formal and informal registers
- Demonstrative pronouns
- Verbs SER, ESTAR, TER
- Numbers 0 – 20
- The alphabet and pronunciation
- Basic vocabulary: family, jobs, nationality

**Work after class**

- **Reading task**: how to greet professionally in Latin American countries.

### Lesson 2: Perguntas pessoais

**Language Functions**

- Talking in detail about family, profession and studies
- Explaining our daily routines
- Developing our social skills: asking personal questions/making friends

**Grammar Structures**

- Interrogative pronouns: *como, quando, quanto, onde, de onde, com quem, por que?*
- Gender and number of nouns and adjectives
- Numbers 20 – 100
- Reflexive verbs: chamar-se/ vestir-se
- Comparative: *mais velho do que, mais novo do que*
- The present tense (regular verbs)

**Work after class**

- **Writing task**: interviewing and answering questions.

### Lesson 3: Lar, doce lar

> [!important] Assessment
> **On-line Homework – Moodle quiz:** general understanding (5% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Saying where you live and where you are from
- Talking about cities and places
- Describing places: your home
- Renting a house/apartment

**Grammar Structures**

- Vocabulary: parts of the house, furniture, facilities
- Ordinal numbers: 1st to 10th
- Adverbs and adverbial phrases: aqui/lá, à esquerda/direita.
- The present tense (irregular verbs)

**Work after class**

- **Speaking task**: bring a picture of a city/home town to describe it to our colleagues.
- **On-line Homework**
- **Moodle quiz:** general understanding (5% final mark)

### Lesson 4: Atividades de lazer

**Language Functions**

- Talking about free time, preferences and replying to invitations
- Describing our routine
- Specifying spare time activities
- Telling the time and when we do things
- What we like to do in our free time
- Sports and hobbies
- Expressing likes and dislikes
- Talking about past activities: completed actions in the past

**Grammar Structures**

- Basic vocabulary: days of the week, sports and hobbies, food and drink
- Verbs: GOSTAR DE, ADORAR, DETESTAR, ODIAR
- Verbs: JOGAR, FAZER, TOCAR, LER, OUVIR, PREFERIR
- The past tense: *pretérito perfeito do indicativo*

**Work after class**

- **Writing task**: O que você fez no fim de semana passado?

### Lesson 5: Vamos ao mercado?

**Language Functions**

- Describing what we want to buy
- Buying vegetables and fruits
- Enquiring about prices, discounts
- Serving /being served
- Shopping for clothes
- Shopping for souvenirs
- Finding typical products

**Grammar Structures**

- Verb PODER/GOSTAR DE: *O senhor poderia me mostrar...* / Eu gostaria de provar... (cortesia - futuro do pretérito do indicativo - condicional)
- Verb QUERER: Eu queria um quilo de... (cortesia – pretérito imperfeito do indicativo)
- Direct object pronouns: *Posso ajudá-lo? Posso ajudá-la?*
- Vocabulary: food, vegetable, fruit, dairy products, weights and measures
- Vocabulary: clothes, colours

**Work after class**

- **Speaking task**: write your weekly shopping list and let's go to the market!

### Lesson 6: Gastronomia + revisão (expressing likes, talking about routines and daily activities)

> [!important] Assessment
> **On-line Homework – Moodle quiz:** general understanding (5% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Talking about food and timetables
- Ordering a snack
- Asking for the bill
- Booking a table in a restaurant
- Choosing food items from a menu
- Learning about traditional food
- Giving instructions

**Grammar Structures**

- Vocabulary: food and drinks
- Numbers 100 to 1 million
- Gender and number agreement
- Impersonal HAVER/TER
- Verb QUERER/ GOSTAR DE (cortesia)
- Imperative: instructions (informal)

**Work after class**

- **Listening task**: a special recipe
- **On-line Homework**
- **Moodle quiz:** general understanding (5% final mark)

### Lesson 7: Minhas próximas férias

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class Grammar Quiz 1: 10% final mark**
>
> **On-line Written Homework: 10% final mark**

**Language Functions**

- Talking about future holidays
- Talking about your dream holiday
- Stating likes and dislikes
- Describing holiday activities
- Booking a hotel room
- Making phone calls

**Grammar Structures**

- Vocabulary: holiday activities, landmarks
- Verb QUERER
- Verb FICAR (location)
- Future tense
  - Futuro imediato: *Vou viajar a Lisboa.*
  - Futuro do presente do indicativo: *Eu viajarei no fim do ano.*
- Futuro do pretérito do indicativo: *Eu gostaria de viajar a Lisboa.*

**Work after class**

- **Reading task**: touristic attractions in the lusophone countries.
- **On-line Written Homework: 10% final mark**

### Lesson 8: Minhas últimas férias

**Language Functions**

- Talking about past holidays
- Talking about past activities
- Describing events in the past
- Talking about the weather
- Expressing likes and dislikes

**Grammar Structures**

- Vocabulary: holiday activities, places and monuments, transport, the weather
- The past tense:
  - Pretérito perfeito do indicativo: *Eu viajei a Porto Alegre./A viagem foi longa*.
  - Pretérito imperfeito do indicativo: *Eu estava muito feliz/ Eu estava muito cansado.*
- Verb GOSTAR DE
- Verbs SER and ESTAR

**Work after class**

- **Writing and speaking task**: select a couple of pictures from your last holiday and write a text about this experience. Prepare a brief presentation to share it with your classmates.

### Lesson 9: Festas tradicionais

**Language Functions**

- Learning and sharing experiences of traditional celebrations in the Portuguese speaking countries
- Expanding cultural awareness

**Grammar Structures**

- Vocabulary: *festas e costumes*
- Impersonal: *Nesta época, se come muita comida feita de milho. Há muitas fogueiras pelas ruas. Costuma-se dançar até tarde.*

**Work after class**

- **Listening task**: celebrations in Portuguese speaking countries.

### Lesson 10: Morar na cidade ou campo?

> [!important] Assessment
> **On-line Homework – Moodle quiz:** reading (5% final mark)

**Language Functions**

- Talking about cities and places
- Describing advantages and disadvantages of living in a big city
- Stating preferences
- Giving directions
- Asking for and giving directions

**Grammar Structures**

- Vocabulary: cities, villages, activities
- Comparative and superlative
- Verb PREFERIR
- Imperative: directions (formal)
- Prepositions and adverbs of place

**Work after class**

- **Reading task**: a vida no campo.
- **On-line Homework**
- **Moodle quiz:** reading (5% final mark)

### Lesson 11: Como o tempo passa depressa!

**Language Functions**

- Talking about past experiences
- Talking about anecdotes
- Talking about how things used to be and were a few years back

**Grammar Structures**

- Past tense: Pretérito perfeito and pretérito imperfeito do indicativo
- Conjunctions and adverbs of time: *antigamente, enquanto, quando eu era pequeno, naquele tempo, naquela época*.

**Work after class**

- **Writing task**: write a text about a special memory from your hometown, childhood, a special occasion.

### Lesson 12: Saúde e bem-estar + Revisão (talking about the past: *pretérito perfeito e imperfeito do indicativo*)

**Language Functions**

- Expressing what hurts
- Expressing symptoms
- Describing what happened
- Giving and getting advice

**Grammar Structures**

- Vocabulary: parts of the body
- Verb SENTIR-SE/SENTIR: *Sinto-me cansado. Sinto frio*.
- Verb ESTAR: *Estou com dor de cabeça*.
- Giving impersonal advice: *É importante beber muita água.*

**Work after class**

- **Reading task**: important advice on how to live a healthy life.

### Lesson 13: Nosso planeta

> [!important] Assessment
> **In-class Grammar Quiz 2: 10% final mark**

**Language Functions**

- Talking about the environment
- Discussing about different lifestyles
- Talking about daily activities/house work

**Grammar Structures**

- Vocabulary: materials
- Verbs
  - Pretérito perfeito do indicativo: *comprei*
  - Pretérito imperfeito do indicativo: *comprava*
  - Futuro do presente do indicativo: *comprarei*
  - Futuro do pretérito do indicativo (condicional): *compraria*
  - Futuro imediato: *vou comprar*

**Work after class**

- **Writing task**: write a list of some of the most important pieces of advice you can give to someone about looking after our planet.

**Language Functions; Grammar Structures**

- All of the above + practice for the oral presentations

**Work after class**

- **Writing task**: writing activity provided by teacher.
