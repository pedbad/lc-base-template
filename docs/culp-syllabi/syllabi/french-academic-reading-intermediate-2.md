## Week 1

**Reading material**

- La Rochefoucauld : Selected Maximes

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- **Overview of Reading skills**: identify cognates, look for morphosyntactic clues, understand sentence structure. Use of a dictionary. Translation. Reading aloud. (Strategies to be applied throughout the course)
- Verbs in the present; Adjectives; Nouns/Gender; Comparison

## Week 2

**Reading material**

- *2.1 Discours sur l'origine et les fondements de l'inégalité parmi les hommes - l'amour-propre*, Jean-Jacques Rousseau
- *2.2 Les Misérables,* Vol.4. Chap.1 *(historical background)* and Chap.3 *(Louis-Philippe),* Victor Hugo

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- Relative Pronouns 'Qui' and 'Que'
- Relative Pronouns 'Ce qui', 'Où', 'Auquel'; Adverbial Pronouns 'Y' and 'En'; Negation; Use of 'On'; Present Participle

## Week 3

**Reading material**

- *Le Spleen de Paris*, *Petits poèmes en prose*, Baudelaire.
- *1. Enivrez-vous ; 2. Les Fenêtres*

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- Imperative and the use of 'Il faut';
- More relatives 'Celui qui', 'Ce que'

## Week 4

**Reading material**

- *Le deuxième sexe,* Simone de Beauvoir
- *1. Les données de la biologie* ; 2. *L'enfance*

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- Present; Adjectives ; Adverbs ; Uses of 'même'

## Week 5

**Reading material**

- *5.1 L'Étranger*, Albert Camus
- *5.2 Les animaux malades de la peste*

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- Perfect; Imperfect; Pluperfect – Sequence of tenses
- Introduction to Simple Past

## Week 6

**Reading material**

- *La plus grande famine du siècle*, B. M. d'Orléans, manuscrit.

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- Imperfect; Simple Past; Introduction to Pluperfect

## Week 7

**Reading material**

- *7.1 Discours sur l'origine et les fondements de l'inégalité parmi les hommes* - Part 2, J.J. Rousseau
- *7.2 Un certain sourire*, Françoise Sagan

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- Present, Perfect, Imperfect and Simple Past; verbs followed by prepositions 'à' and 'de'; Pluperfect; Introduction to Subjunctive tenses

## Week 8

**Reading material**

- *8.1 Traité sur la nature de l'églogue,* Bernard de Fontenelle.
- 8.2 *Cyrano de Bergerac*: La tirade du nez, Edmond Rostand or *Complainte amoureuse*, Alphonse Allais

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- Present, Past, Imperfect and Pluperfect Subjunctive; Introduction to Past Anterior; Relative Pronoun 'Dont'

## Week 9

**Reading material**

- *9.1 Le Code Napoléon*
  - 1.Titre préliminaire; 2. Quelques articles
- *9.2 L'hymne à L'amour,* Édith Piaf

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- Future and Conditional; Object pronouns

## Week 10

**Reading material**

- *À la Recherche du Temps Perdu*, Marcel Proust

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- Revision of main Indicative and Subjunctive tenses; Object pronouns

## Week 11

**Reading material**

- *Montpellier à béton rompu,* Antoine Guiral (envoyé spécial)

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- **Practise reading journalistic style**
- Link words; Present participle; Gerund;

## Week 12

**Reading material**

- *Mozart, Don Giovanni* - Adrien De Vries

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- Passive form; Past Conditional

## Week 13

**Reading material**

- *Le Théâtre de l'absurde :* extracts from Eugène Ionesco and Samuel Beckett plays

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- **Stylistic devices**
- Revision of tenses.

## Weeks 14/15

**Reading material**

- Texts from students

**Grammar and structures / Key Objectives**

- Applied grammar
