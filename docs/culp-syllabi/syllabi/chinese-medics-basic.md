# Chinese Medics Basic Syllabus

## Lessons at a glance

| Lesson | Language function |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Greet at different times of the day |
| 2 | Ask about surname and given name |
| 3 | Tell your name, subject, nationality |
| 4 | Talk about where you come from, what you do |
| 5 | Briefly introduce your basic information |
| 6 | Tell time and day of the week |
| 7 | Order tea, coffee, soft drinks |
| 8 | Book and ask for a hotel room |
| 9 | Make complaints on broken hotel facilities |
| 10 | Revision of all functions in context |

## Lesson 1

**Language function**

- greet at different times of the day

**Grammar structure**

- basic Chinese sentence structures

**Objectives**

- To be able to greet and tell one's name on an informal occasion

## Lesson 2

**Language function**

- ask about surname and given name

**Grammar structure**

- "什么 shénme what" question
- the usage of "很 hěn" (very) and "也 yě" (also)
- "yes/no" question;

**Objectives**

- To be able to tell basic information about oneself
- To be able to count/write 1-10

## Lesson 3

**Language function**

- tell your name, subject, nationality;
- what to say when exchanging name cards on a business occasion

**Grammar structure**

- ellipsis question;
- "which/where" question;
- negative sentences

**Objectives**

- To be able to greet on a formal/business occasion

## Lesson 4

**Language function**

- Talk about where you come from, what you do
- Tell your age and ask for others

**Grammar structure**

- alternative question;
- verb complement (e.g. speak Chinese very well);
- the usage of "会 huì" (can)
- the usage of "从 cóng... lái" (come from)

**Objectives**

- To be able to tell your country, hometown

## Lesson 5

**Language function**

- briefly introduce your basic information, including study, work, and family

**Grammar structure**

- measure word "个 gè"
- the usage of "在 zài" (at);
- present perfect tense (1) － "how many" question

**Objectives**

- To be able to tell your mother tongue and foreign languages you can speak
- To be able to count/write 11-99

## Lesson 6

**Language function**

- Tell time and day of the week
- More on character recognition

**Grammar structure**

- Use of 两 liǎng
- More on 是 shì and 有 yǒu

**Objectives**

- To be able to tell time, day, month and year
- Read and understand Chinese Calendar

## Lesson 7

**Language function**

- order tea, coffee, soft drinks with specific requirements

**Grammar structure**

- the usage of "一点儿 yīdiǎnr" (a little bit);
- the usage of "要 yào" (to want to);
- the usage of "喜欢 xǐhuan" (to like);
- the usage of "给 gěi" (for);
- the usage of "太 tài" (too much)

**Objectives**

- To be able to order drinks with requirements

## Lesson 8

**Language function**

- book and ask for a hotel room; enquire about prices, breakfast, facilities

**Grammar structure**

- "how much" question;
- "when" question;
- the usage of "还 hái" (in addition);
- the usage of "一下 yīxià" to denote brevity
- structure "从 cóng... 到 dào" (from……to)

**Objectives**

- To be able to converse with hotel staff when you first arrive

## Lesson 9

**Language function**

- make complaints on broken hotel facilities;
- leave luggage at hotel,
- check out of a hotel room

**Grammar structure**

- consecutive verbs in a sentence;
- the usage of "可以 kěyǐ" (can, to be allowed to)

**Objectives**

- To be able to converse with hotel staff when you leave and when you meet unexpected situations

## Lesson 10

- Revision of all functions in context
- In-class exercises
