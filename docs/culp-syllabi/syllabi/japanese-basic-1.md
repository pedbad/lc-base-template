## Michaelmas term

### Lesson 1: Meeting People 1

**Language Functions**

- Greeting at different time of a day
- Meeting others and introducing yourself
- Telling and asking personal information

**Grammar Structures**

- Introduction to Japanese grammar (the word classifications, the basic structure of a sentence)
- Identification: **The copula "*desu*"**
- Topic marker "*wa*"
- Numbers: 1 – 12

**Work after Class**

- Hiragana: あいうえお かきくけこ さしすせそ
- Kanji: 一二三十山川
- Online: Essentials

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- make right sounds for the Japanese language
- tell and ask one's name and academic major
- exchange business cards in a formal occasion

### Lesson 2: Meeting People 2; At a Cafe

**Language Functions**

- Talking more about yourself
- Identifying people and items
- Telling and asking personal information
- Ordering food/drinks
- Specifying quantity

**Grammar Structures**

- Identifying people and items
- The negative form of "*desu*": "*dewa(ja)-arimasen*"
- **Introduction to particles** (definition, functions, when to use)
- Particle "*mo*" = also
- Particle "*kara*" = from
- Making yes-no questions: Question marker "*ka*"
- Requesting (Noun): "(Noun) *onegaishimasu*" = I'd like to have (Noun), please.
- Basic counter for items (1-10)
- Numbers: 10-99; 100–1,000

**Work after Class**

- Hiragana: たちつてと なにぬねの はひふへほ
- Kanji: 百千円日本人

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- introduce yourself at your work place or at a party.
- ask and tell personal information (age, nationality, hobbies, phone numbers)
- order food/drinks at a fast food shop

### Lesson 3: Shopping 1: At a kiosk

**Language Functions**

- Asking and telling time
- Asking about the business hour
- Asking about price

**Grammar Structures**

- Personal pronouns (I, you, he, she) and Demonstratives (this and 2 thats)
- Making wh-questions: Interrogatives (what time, how much)
- Particle "to" = and
- Particle "made" = till (a time)
- Numbers: 1,000 – 100,000

**Work after Class**

- Hiragana: まみむめも・やゆよ
- Kanji: 四時半

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- ask and tell time (o'clock, AM, PM)
- ask about the time in other places
- ask how much something is
- make a purchase at a store

### Lesson 4: At a Restaurant

**Language Functions**

- Asking what something is
- Asking where something is from
- Asking whose it is

**Grammar Structures**

- Asking questions: more interrogatives (what, who, how many people)
- Possessive marker "*no*"
- Demonstrative adjectives: "This Noun/That Noun"
- Counter for people

**Work after Class**

- Hiragana: らりるれろ・わをん
- Kanji: 五六七八九

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- ask about the ingredients of a dish
- ask where a certain food/drink is from (eg. Japanese beer) and order the food/drink
- tell how many people in your group
- ask and tell who possesses a particular item

### Lesson 5: Shopping 2: At a Souvenir Shop

**Language Functions**

- Asking about existence
- Reading a short letter

**Grammar Structures**

- **The basic structure of Japanese verbs**
- The verbs for "there is/there are"
- Basic adjectives (big, small, expensive, inexpensive)

**Work after Class**

- Write a paragraph to state what your college has/does not have.
- Kanji: 大 小

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- ask if the shop has something you want.
- ask if there are any products with different colours, sizes or prices

### Lesson 6: Public Transportation

**Language Functions**

- Stating where somebody (something) goes
- Taking the train/bus

**Grammar Structures**

- The non-past, polite form of verbs
- The verbs for "going and coming"
- Particle "*ni*" = to (place)
- Location words (north, south, east and west)

**Work after Class**

- Write a letter to introduce yourself in hiragana.
- Kanji: 東西南北

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- ask if the bus (or train) goes to your destination and buy the ticket
- take a taxi and tell your destination to the driver
- listen and understand the simple dialogues

### Lesson 7: Making an Appointment

> [!important] Assessment
> **Writing assessment (20 min. in class)**

**Language Functions**

- Inviting somebody to do something

**Grammar Structures**

- Transitive Verbs
- Object marker "*o*"
- Making verb forms for "Let's ~" and "Won't you ~?"
- Sentence-ending particle: "*ne*"

**Work after Class**

- Write a New Year's card
- Kanji: 男女

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- invite a friend to have lunch with you
- accept or refuse the invitation in a polite manner

## Lent term

### Lesson 8: Talking About Schedules

**Language Functions**

- Talking about daily/weekly activities and events
- Talking about future activities and events

**Grammar Structures**

- Revisions (L1 ~L7)
- Intransitive verbs
- (Person) do ~ at/in (place) (time). = *(Person) wa (time) ni (place) de ~ masu.*

**Work after Class**

- Katakana: アイウエオ・カキクケコ
- Kanji: 月火水

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- talk about your daily/weekly activities
- talk about future activities and events
- confirm your schedule with a Japanese colleague

### Lesson 9: Weekends and Holidays

**Language Functions**

- Talking about activities and events in the past
- Describing features or characteristics of a place
- Stating the duration of an activity

**Grammar Structures**

- The past, polite form of verbs
- **The basic structure of Japanese adjectives**
- The non-past, affirmative form of adjectives
- Expressions of quantity (many/a lot, few/little)
- Basic conjunctions (And, But)
- Sentence-ending particle "*yo*"

**Work after Class**

- Write a diary
- Katakana: サシスセソ・タチツテト
- Kanji: 木金土

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- state what you did yesterday
- talk about your holidays

### Lesson 10: Likes and Dislikes

**Language Functions**

- Talking about likes and dislikes
- Expressing preference

**Grammar Structures**

- The sentence construction of (Noun) *ga* (Adjective) *desu.*
  1. I like (Noun).
  2. I dislike (Noun).
  3. I like to have (Noun).
- The non-past negative form of adjectives
- Frequency adverbs (everyday, often, sometimes, rarely, never)

**Work after Class**

- Write a personal advertisement
- Katakana: ナニヌネノ・ハヒフヘホ

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- talk about what you like and dislike
- express your preference

### Lesson 11: Hotel／Ryokan

**Language Functions**

- Asking location
- Asking what is available
- Making reservations

**Grammar Structures**

- More interrogative (where)
- Location words (right/left)
- More adjectives (new/old, far/near)
- Counter for floors of a building (X *kai*)

**Work after Class**

- Katakana: マミムメモ・ヤユヨ
- Kanji: 右 左

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- ask where the hotel is located
- ask if the hotel has some facility (eg. a swimming pool)
- ask where the breakfast is served
- make inquiries about a hotel and make a booking for a room on the phone

### Lesson 12: Convenience store

**Language Functions**

- Indicating the location
- Reading a memo that describes where something is located

**Grammar Structures**

- The sentence construction of (Item) *wa* (Place) *no* (Location) *ni arimasu.* = (Item) is (location) of (Place).
- More location words (on; above; up, under; below; down, in; inside, front, back, between, nearby, next to)

**Work after Class**

- Katakana: ラリルレロ・ワヲン
- Kanji: 上 下 中
- write a memo and explain where the Language Centre is located.

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- ask and tell the location of items you need
- read and write a memo that indicates where something is located.

### Lesson 13: Visiting a Friend's House

**Language Functions**

- Expressing apologies and gratitude

**Grammar Structures**

- The past affirmative form of adjectives
- (Person) *wa* (Place) *ni imasu.* = (Person) is at/in (Place).

**Work after Class**

- write about your family
- Kanji: 天気 元気

**Learning objectives**

To be able to:

- learn etiquettes when you visit a Japanese friend's home
- talk about your family
